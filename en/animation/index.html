Animate and make cutscenes¶

The timeline, at the bottom of the view, animates objects frame by frame, without code: a door that opens, a platform that rises, a drawbridge that lowers, a lamp that blinks. It also puts cutscenes together: cameras, characters who walk or wave, sounds, subtitles, while the player watches.

A timeline belongs to the scene, like its objects: it is saved with it, and every gesture is undone with Ctrl+Z.

Open the timeline¶

  • In the status bar, at the bottom of the window, click Timeline, next to Library.
  • Or, in the View menu, Animation timeline.
  • Or create a timeline straight away: in the scene list, + then Animation or Cutscene.

At the top of the timeline, the list chooses the open timeline; it also offers New animation and New cutscene.

Animate an object¶

  1. Select the object (the door), in the view or the scene list.
  2. New animation: it starts from the object as it is, with a first keyframe at 0 seconds (a diamond on its row).
  3. Move the playhead: click or drag on the ruler of seconds, at the top of the timeline.
  4. Move the object with the gizmo: move it, turn it, scale it. It does not move in the scene: it gets a keyframe at that moment.
  5. Play (the triangle, or Space with the mouse over the timeline): the view shows the animation live.

The Keyframe button sets a keyframe of the selected objects as they show, at the playhead: that is how you add another object to an animation. An object's children follow it: animating the door carries its handle.

Values are kept relative to the object's parent: moving the house later does not break its door's animation. A rotation from 0 to 360 degrees makes a full turn.

Set a keyframe¶

A click on a diamond chooses it, and the row above sets it:

Setting What it does
Linear, Smooth, In steps How the object goes to the next keyframe: at a steady pace, starting and stopping gently, or waiting then jumping.
Visible Shows or hides the object from this moment on: a blinking lamp, a bridge that appears.
Color The object's tint at this moment; it changes smoothly from one keyframe to the next.
Remove the keyframe The object goes straight from the previous one to the next. Delete does the same.

Drag a diamond to move it in time. A right-click on a row offers Keyframe here and Remove this object from the timeline.

Playback¶

Playback For
Once A door that opens and stays open.
Looping A blinking lamp, a turning wheel.
There and back A platform that goes up and down, forever.

Length: the length of the timeline, in seconds. From the start of the game plays it as soon as the game starts: for a platform that comes and goes on its own.

Make a cutscene¶

New cutscene opens a ten-second timeline, with its rows: Cameras, Characters, Sounds, Subtitles, Events, besides the objects it animates. A right-click on a row, Add here, sets a moment at that time; a click on it chooses it to set it.

Row A moment Its settings
Cameras Show the game through a placed camera (see Create, Camera) The camera, or Player camera; Glide: it glides from the previous one; Fade to black: black centred on the change. A camera can also have its own row of keyframes, to move during the shot.
Characters Play an animation, or Walk to an object The character, its animation (read from its model), Looping; for walking, the target, the Speed, and the animation once there.
Sounds A sound from the library Music: it loops and replaces the one playing; Volume.
Subtitles A text at the bottom of the screen The text, its Length.
Events A name sent at that moment A Trigger rule (When an event happens) or a script can wait for it.

During a cutscene, the player has no control: black bars frame the picture. At the end, the camera from before comes back and the player gets control again.

  • World stopped: the rest of the game stops (enemies, physics, scripts); only the cutscene goes on, with its characters. Otherwise the world lives around it.
  • Can be skipped: Esc (or Start on a gamepad) skips the cutscene. Everything is set as at its end: objects at their last pose, characters arrived, the events left sent. The sounds left do not play.

Play it in the game¶

  • Without code, with the Trigger: Play an animation, Play a cutscene, Stop an animation. The At the end, send the event field chains another rule (When an event happens): the drawbridge is down, a chest appears.
  • From the start of the game, with nothing else.
  • In a script: game.play_timeline("door_opens"), game.stop_timeline(...), game.timeline_playing(...). The timeline's id is in its … menu. See scripts.

The game announces timeline.debut and timeline.fin (see Events).

With the agent¶

Just ask: "make the door open when I pull the lever", "an intro cutscene that flies over the village". The agent writes the timeline, links it to the Trigger, and checks it with pictures at several moments, then by playing the game. You see it in the timeline and touch it up with the mouse.
