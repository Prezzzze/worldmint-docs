Creator mode¶

Creator mode is the simplest way to make your game: you describe what you want to the agent, look at the result in the view, touch objects by hand, then try it with Play. It only shows what these gestures need. All the tools (the scene tree, scripts, the timeline, terrain...) stay in Developer mode, on the same project.

The first time, WorldMint opens in Creator mode; after that, in the last mode you used. A project created with Describe your game (the home screen) always opens in Creator mode.

The Creator | Developer switch¶

At the top of the window, in the same place in both modes, the Creator | Developer switch changes mode in one click. Nothing is lost: the scene, the selection and the conversation stay as they are.

The top bar also keeps:

  • the logo and WorldMint: a click opens the home screen;
  • ☰: the editor menus (File, Edit...), grouped; ☰ > Game settings opens the settings of the whole game in the drawer on the right, and the creation assistant is there too;
  • the project name, followed by the list of scenes: pick another scene (another place in the game) to open it. If the open scene has unsaved changes, WorldMint first asks: Save, Don't save or Keep working. Creating, renaming or deleting a scene is done in Developer mode;
  • undo and redo, Export, Preferences and Play.

The conversation, on the left¶

The agent is on the left, like a messaging app, with its field always at the bottom. It builds the game in the open scene, with your own account (see The agent).

  • With no agent connected, the column shows Connect your agent and the first steps. The rest of the editor works by hand. A description written on the home screen waits in the field, and the screen says so: "Your description is waiting: it will be sent as soon as the agent is connected." It is sent by itself when the agent is ready; clear it from the field if you no longer want to send it.
  • A script error during play becomes a message in the conversation, with Fix it, which prepares the request to the agent.
  • Fold the conversation: the arrow at the top right of the column shrinks it to a narrow strip, and the view grows. The strip's icon opens it again, as you left it. A coloured dot under the icon shows that the agent is working, or that an error is waiting to be fixed.
  • Widen the conversation: drag the handle on its right edge (the small line, mint when hovered). The view always keeps its room.

The view and the gestures, in the middle¶

The 3D view is the protected area: nothing stays on it. Only the gesture tools float in the bottom left corner:

Tool What it does
Select A click on an object picks it.
Move (W) The gizmo arrows move the picked object.
Rotate (E) The gizmo rings turn it.
Scale (R) The gizmo handles make it bigger or smaller.
Frame the selection (F) The view moves to the picked object. Greyed out while nothing is picked.

The mouse works as in Developer mode (see The 3D view). Creator mode keeps a single view.

The drawer, on the right¶

The drawer opens only when it is useful:

  • one object picked: its name, its place (position, rotation, scale), its systems with their main settings only (health, damage, range, the text of a prompt...). The other settings are in Developer mode. Ask the agent prepares a message about this object in the conversation ("About …: "): you only have to write the rest;
  • several objects picked: their common place, to move them together (a single Ctrl+Z undoes it), and ways to put them on the ground, group, duplicate or delete them;
  • the terrain (a click on the ground): Ask the agent, and Edit in Developer mode to sculpt it;
  • ☰ > Game settings: the settings of the whole game, with nothing picked.

The library, at the bottom¶

The library shows thumbnails, sorted into sections:

Section What you find there
My models The 3D models of your project and your library.
Shapes and lights What the editor can create without a file: Cube, Sphere, Cylinder, Capsule, Plane, Light, Camera, Zone, Arrival point.
Reusable objects The objects you kept to place several times.
Online The models of the online library.
  • Place an object: drag its thumbnail into the view, where you want it. A shape from Shapes and lights can be dragged too; a simple click places it where you are looking (this is the only gesture for a light, a camera, a zone or an arrival point). The placed object is picked, and its drawer opens.
  • Search: the Search field, right of the sections, filters the chosen section.
  • Fold: the arrow left of the sections folds the library; choosing a section opens it again.
  • Enlarge: drag the handle on its top edge to see more thumbnails.

In a narrow window, the sections become a list.

And Developer mode?¶

It has all the tools, in panels you can move, group into tabs or detach: the Scene, the full Properties, Game systems, Scripts, the Timeline, Terrain, the Console... See The editor window. Switch whenever you like: the work stays the same.
