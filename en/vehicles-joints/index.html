Vehicles, joints and ragdolls¶

Three game systems give objects real game mechanics, without writing code: Joint, for what turns on a hinge, slides on a rail or hangs from a rope; Vehicle, for a car, a motorbike or a boat you drive; Ragdoll, for a character that falls limp then gets back up. They are set in the Properties, and the agent can place them for you.

As with physics, nothing is tied to a model pack: the attachment point is taken on the model's box, the wheels are measured on their models, and the ragdoll on the character's skeleton.

Joints¶

A joint links an object to another one, or to the world: a door on its hinges, a drawbridge, a wheel on a cart, a drawer, an elevator, a chandelier, a plank that gives way under a weight.

  1. Turn the system on: Game tab, Systems section, Turn on under Joint. The Physics system comes with it: the jointed object is an object that moves.
  2. Place it on the moving part (the door, not the wall): drag Joint from the Systems folder of the library, or + System in its Properties. Give it a Mass under Physics: 20 to 40 kg for a door.
  3. Set it under Joint. When the object is selected, the 3D view shows its attachment point (a dot) and its axis (a line): you see at once whether the door turns the right way.
Setting What it does
Kind of joint Hinge (turns around an axis), Slider (slides along an axis), Ball (turns every way around a point), Fixed (holds in place until it breaks), Rope (never goes further than a length), Spring (comes back to its length).
Attached to (empty: the world) The object that holds it, chosen in the scene: the cart of a wheel, the furniture of a drawer. Empty, it is held where it stands.
Attachment point Where it is held, on its model's box: in the middle, on a side, at the bottom, at the top, at the front or at the back.
Axis For a hinge or a slider: Vertical (a door, an elevator), Sideways (a drawbridge, a wheel), Front to back (a drawer).
Stops How far it turns (in degrees) or slides (in meters) from where it starts. A hinge from −180 to 180 turns endlessly, like a wheel. For a ball, how far it swings.
Springiness (0: rigid) Stops that cushion like a spring; for a spring, its stiffness.
Motor None: it moves when pushed. To a position: it stays closed, and opens to its stop when asked. At a steady speed: it turns or slides without stopping (a mill, a conveyor).
Motor strength 500 for a door, 20,000 for an elevator carrying the player.
Open at the start With a motor to a position: it starts open.
Breaking force (N, 0: unbreakable) Beyond it, the joint gives way and the object falls: 800 for a rotten plank the player breaks by jumping on it.

Open, close, break¶

The Trigger has the actions Open, Close and Break its joint, with On which object: a lever (Interaction system) that opens the door, a zone in front of the drawbridge that lowers it. And the rule Its joint breaks reacts when it gives way: a message, a sound, what happens next.

Examples¶

  • A door you push: Hinge, attachment point on a side, vertical axis, stops 0 and 100. The player opens it by walking into it.
  • A door opened by a lever: the same, with the motor To a position and a strength of 800; on the lever, a rule The player uses it → Open, on the door.
  • A drawbridge: Hinge, attachment point at the bottom, sideways axis, stops −90 and 0, motor to a position, Open at the start; a Close rule lowers it.
  • A chandelier: Ball, attachment point at the top, stop 30 degrees.
  • A plank that gives way: Fixed, breaking force 800.

Vehicles¶

The Vehicle system turns a model into a car: its wheels spin, steer and follow the suspension, and the player drives it.

  1. Turn the system on, then place it on the car itself, or on the folder that holds its body and wheels. Place it on the ground.
  2. The wheels: the objects inside the car whose name contains "roue", "wheel", "tire", "tyre" or "pneu" are found on their own. Otherwise, list them in Wheels. Their size and place come from their models.
  3. A one-piece model (the car and its wheels in a single file, without separate wheels): with Wheels of a one-piece model checked (the starting setting), the system looks for its wheels inside the model itself, the round pieces at its bottom seen from the side. When the car is selected, a circle marks each one. In the game, they come off the body and turn like real wheels.
  4. If none is found (wheels welded to the body), four invisible wheels carry it, at the corners of its underside, and the model's wheels do not turn.
  5. The front of the model: most face +Z. When the car is selected, an arrow shows its front, and a circle marks each wheel found. The front wheels steer.

Getting in and out: the player walks up and presses Use (E, or Y on a gamepad). At the wheel, Move forward and Move back accelerate or brake then reverse, Move left and Move right steer, Jump holds the hand brake. The camera follows the car and comes back behind it when you do not turn it. Use again, and they get out on the left side.

Setting What it does
Mass (kg) A car 1,200, a kart 150, a truck 8,000.
Engine power (N·m) It makes it accelerate: 600 for a car, 150 for a kart.
Top speed (km/h) Beyond it, it no longer speeds up.
Grip (1: dry road) 0.4 slides as on snow, 1.5 sticks to the road.
Suspension travel (m), Suspension stiffness A soft or firm car, an off-roader that rides high.
Front wheel steering (degrees) More, it turns tighter.
Brake strength The hand brake locks the rear wheels.
Driving assist (1: arcade) 1, as in an arcade game: at speed it steers less, the wheel turns smoothly even on the keyboard, and it follows the road without spinning out or sliding (the hand brake still makes it drift). 0: physics alone, like a simulation.

The driver stays visible: seated in the driver seat (on the left), hands on the wheel, you see them through the windows; on a motorbike, astride the saddle, hands on the handlebars, they lean with it. Their place is measured on the model. If they sink into the seat or float above it, set Seat: forwards, upwards and to the right (in meters). If the player's character has a driving animation (its name is about driving or sitting: drive, ride, sit, seat…), it is played; otherwise a seated pose is made from their own. Driving animation picks another one. Uncheck Visible driver to hide them, as behind tinted windows. With several players, everyone sees the others at the wheel.

A zone sees the driven car as the player: a finish line works for a race.

Copying a car during the game: a script that calls game.clone on a vehicle (a hidden model used as a template, or a placed car) gets another vehicle, with its wheels, that the player can drive. A car hidden then shown again is a vehicle again too.

The motorbike¶

A vehicle of the Motorbike kind rolls on two wheels, one behind the other. Place the system on the bike (or the folder holding its frame and wheels), choose Kind of vehicle: Motorbike, and set Mass (200 for a motorbike, 110 for a scooter) and Engine power (150): the starting values are a car's.

  • Its wheels are found like a car's: by their name, in the Wheels list, or inside a one-piece model. The front one steers, the rear one drives. With no wheel found, two invisible wheels carry it.
  • It stands upright by itself, parked or ridden, as on its kickstand, and leans into turns, the more so the faster it goes.
  • It can fall over: hit from the side (a rock, a car) or knocked down in a crash, it falls on its side and its rider is thrown off (falling limp if they carry the Ragdoll system). To set it upright, just get back on with Use.
  • It is driven like a car, and the camera follows it the same way.
Setting (motorbike) What it does
Lean in turns (degrees) How far it leans at most: 45 for a road bike, 55 for a racing bike, 25 for a scooter.
Balance (1: ordinary) The strength that keeps it upright: 2 very steady, 0.5 wobbly.
Handling (1: ordinary) How fast the handlebars turn: 2 quick, 0.5 heavy. Too quick, a sharp turn can knock it down.
Hit that unbalances it (m/s) A sideways blow sharper than this makes it lose its balance for a moment: 4 ordinary, 8 sturdy, 0 never.
Can fall over Unchecked: it never falls, as in an arcade game.

The Trigger and scripts know when it falls: the vehicule.tombe event.

The boat¶

A vehicle of the Boat kind floats instead of rolling. Place the system on the boat (or the folder that holds its hull, mast, cabin), choose Kind of vehicle: Boat, and place it on the water of a lake or the sea.

  • Its hull is measured on its model: its length and width, slice by slice from the back to the front, and its bottom. A thin bow carries less than a wide stern. How deep it sits comes from its Mass: the water it pushes aside weighs as much as it does. Its center of gravity is at its waterline: a mast does not make it capsize.
  • An engine at the back pushes it, a rudder turns it, the better the faster it moves. You drive it like a car: Move forward to speed up, Move back to slow down then go backwards, Move left and Move right to turn. The camera follows it.
  • It rocks with the waves and follows the water's surface; a current carries it along.
  • It runs aground on land: its engine only pushes in water. Sent at a beach, it climbs a little, then stays there.
Setting (boat) What it does
Mass (kg) A motorboat 1,200, a rowing boat 150, a fishing boat 8,000. Heavier, it sits deeper.
Engine thrust (N) It makes it speed up: 4,000 for a 1,200 kg motorboat, 800 for a rowing boat.
Top speed (km/h) The water holds it back more and more: at full thrust, it levels off at this speed.
Turn at full rudder (degrees per second) 40 for a motorboat, 15 for a big boat.

Ragdolls¶

An animated character carrying the Ragdoll system falls limp: its body is built on its own from its skeleton (long enough bones become limbs, fingers and toes follow their hand or foot), then it can get back up, and its animation resumes smoothly.

Setting What it does
Falls limp when it dies (Health system) When its health reaches zero, it collapses, and stays down.
Falls under a hit harder than (m/s) An object that hits it hard enough knocks it down: a rolling rock, a thrown barrel. 0: never.
Gets back up after a hit, Gets up after (s) Knocked down by a hit, it gets up once it has been still for that long.
Mass (kg) The body's weight, shared among its limbs.
Limb looseness (degrees) 45 is natural, 20 falls stiff, 90 is very floppy.

Fallen into water, the rag doll floats, the head and shoulders just above the water, and drifts with it; untick Floats in water to make it sink, like a knight in armor.

The Trigger has the actions Make it fall limp and Get it back up. The character must be animated: a model without a skeleton cannot fall as a ragdoll.

Asking the agent¶

The agent places these systems and checks its work by running the game: the report tells it the angle each joint reached and whether it broke, the speed each vehicle reached, the distance travelled and whether it turned over (for a motorbike: how far it leaned and how many times it fell; for a boat: whether it floats or ran aground, and how deep its hull sits), and how many times each character fell and got back up. Ask it for example:

  • "A wooden door in the castle entrance, that you push to get in."
  • "A drawbridge that lowers when you pull the lever."
  • "A car parked in front of the house, that the player can drive."
  • "A rowing boat moored at the jetty, to cross the lake."
  • "A racing motorbike on the starting line, that leans well into turns."
  • "The guards fall like rag dolls when they die."
