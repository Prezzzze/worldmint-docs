Round¶

How a game with several players unfolds, without code: everyone at their starting place, frozen during "3, 2, 1, Go!", a stopwatch or a time limit, an end (everyone arrived, a score reached, only one left standing), a ranking, and each player sees their own result. On time, on points or last one standing, alone or in teams, in one or several rounds. Placed on an object, it makes it a starting place.
Identifier manche
Version 4
Placed on objects yes

Events¶

The descriptions come from the code.

What it announces¶

Event Fields Description
manche.nouvelle numero (number), total (number) A round is set up (Round module): every player is at their starting place, frozen during the countdown. numero counts from 1; total is the setting manches. The Race resets its laps here.
manche.depart numero (number), total (number) The countdown is over: Go! (Round module). The stopwatch of the round starts now.
manche.resultat joueur (id), temps (number?), rang (number?), elimine (boolean?) A player's round result is known (Round module): they arrived (temps in seconds since Go, rang their place), or they are out (elimine).
manche.fin numero (number), total (number), classement (table), raison (text), gagnant (text?) A round ended (Round module). classement: the ranking, best first, a list of rows {joueur or equipe, nom, rang, temps?, points, vies, progression, elimine, arrive}; gagnant: the first's player id or team name; raison: arrivee, limite, score or dernier.
manche.terminee classement (table), gagnant (text?) The whole game ended (Round module), after the last round: classement is the final ranking (with victoires when there were several rounds). Each player's result is shown by game.finish.
sante.ranimer cible (id), part (number?), montant (number?), invulnerabilite (number?) Ask the health module to bring an object back to life, even dead: part (0 to 1 of its maximum, all by default) or montant (points), then invulnerabilite seconds without losing health. Sent by the death and respawn module.

What it listens to¶

Event Fields Description
manche.lancer — Ask the Round module to start the game now (a menu's Start button, a script), when its setting attente waits for players or for the host. Without effect once started.
manche.arrivee joueur (id) A player arrived (the Race at the end of the last lap, a zone or a script): the Round module notes their time. Counts once per round.
manche.progression joueur (id), valeur (number) How far a player got, a number that grows as they advance (the Race: laps and checkpoints passed, then the share of the way to the next). The Round module ranks those who have not arrived by it.
manche.points joueur (id?), equipe (text?), points (number?) Give points to a player or a team (Round module, ranking on points): from the Trigger, a script or another system. points may be negative; 1 by default. equipe is a team's name or number.
manche.eliminer joueur (id) Put a player out of the round (Round module): they are ranked after those still in, and see Out on screen.
manche.equipe joueur (id), equipe (text) Put a player in a team (Round module, teams chosen by the game): equipe is the team's name or number. Takes effect at the next round's setup for the starting places.
sante.mort cible (id), source (id?) An object's health reached zero. What happens next belongs to the game: the health module does nothing else, unless its setting says to destroy the object.
joueur.reapparu joueur (id), cible (id), position (vector) A dead player stands up again (death and respawn module): respawned, or brought back by sante.ranimer.
joueur.arrivee joueur (id), numero (number), nom (text) Sent by the engine, in a game with several players (the game setting Multiplayer), on the computer hosting the game, when a guest joins: joueur is their character (a copy of the player's), numero their place (1 to 7, the host being 0), nom the name they chose. game.players() lists them all. Scripts only run on the host.
joueur.depart joueur (id), numero (number) Sent by the engine on the host when a guest leaves the game (they quit, or their connection was lost and they did not come back in time, the game setting Place kept after a drop): their character is removed right after. A module forgets what it kept for them.
langue.change langue (text) Sent by the engine when the player changes the game's language (game.set_language, the Menu options). A module redraws what it already shows: its texts go through game.t and game.language.
menu.jouer — The player left the title screen with Play: the game really starts. For an intro message, a music, a timer that should not run behind the title.

Points a script can replace (game.override)¶

The descriptions come from the code.

  • manche.texte: function(default, key, joueur) -> the text shown for that key: attente, attente_hote, lancer, partez, arrive, elimine, reste, points, vies, rang, manche, classement, victoire, place, defaite, temps, gagne, gagnent, victoires, pas_arrive (some hold {n}, {total}, {nom}...). joueur is the player who will read it (nil: everyone). The default is the built-in text in the game's language.
  • manche.points: function(default, qui, points) -> the points really given (a number; 0: none). qui is a player id, or a team's name. For double points in the last minute, or points refused to a player.
  • manche.classement: function(default, classement) -> the ranking, a list of rows {joueur or equipe, nom, temps?, points, vies, progression, elimine, arrive}, best first. Called each time the ranking is made (on screen, at the end of a round). Reorder it for a rule of your own.

What the agent knows about this system¶

The text the agent reads before using this system.

Round (manche)¶

How a game with several players unfolds (ADR-045): starting places, a frozen countdown "3, 2, 1, Go!", a stopwatch or a time limit, an end, a ranking, and each player sees their own result. For a race, an arena, a party game, a team match or any "first to..." game, add this module instead of scripting a countdown, a timer, an end or a ranking yourself. For checkpoints and laps add course too (it requires this one).

  • Starting places: objects carrying this system (systems_attach manche on an invisible marker or a pad at ground level, values place: 1, 2, 3... and equipe: a team's name, empty for anyone). Each player is set there, facing like the object (entities_update its rotation). Without any, players start where they are. More players than places: next to them.
  • Before the first round (attente): aucune (the countdown starts at once), joueurs (wait for joueurs_min players), hote (the host sees a "Start the game" button, the others "The host will start the game"). A script or a menu can start it: game.emit("manche.lancer", {}).
  • With the menu title screen (ecran_titre): the round follows the menu. Title screen → Play (or Host a game) → the waiting room of this system (the waiting text, and « Start the game » for the host) → the countdown. Nothing of the round shows and nothing starts while the title screen is open (menu.jouer ends it). While the pause menu is open, the Start button goes away and comes back when it closes. Never add another start button.
  • Countdown (compte, 3 s): players cannot move (speed 0, no jump; one pushed or sliding is put back at their place). Then "Go!" and manche.depart.
  • Ranking (mode):
  • temps: the first to arrive wins. The arrival is game.emit("manche.arrivee", {joueur = id}): from the course module, or from your own script on a finish zone (zone.entree with e.joueur). The round ends when everyone arrived or at limite; those who did not arrive are ranked by manche.progression {joueur, valeur} (any growing number: the race sends it).
  • points: game.emit("manche.points", {joueur = id, points = 1}) (or equipe = "Rouges"), from the Trigger (declencheur, action "event") or a script; the first at score_cible wins, else the most points at limite.
  • dernier: last one standing. Each death (the sante system on the player) costs one of vies; with no life left the player is out (lying down until the next round) and sees "Out". game.emit("manche.eliminer", {joueur = id}) puts one out directly (fell off the arena). With the mort module, it respawns players between lives; without it, the round raises them at their starting place.
  • Teams (equipes_mode): seul, equilibrees (each arriving player joins the smallest team) or choix (a menu sends manche.equipe {joueur, equipe}; those who chose nothing are shared out at the start). equipes: list of {nom, couleur}. The ranking and the win count per team: on time the best place of its players, on points the sum, last one standing the last team with someone left. All the winning team sees "Victory".
  • Time limit (limite, seconds, 0: none): each player sees the time left, else their own stopwatch (it stops at their arrival).
  • Several rounds (manches, manches_mode): nombre (in N rounds, most wins wins) or victoires (first to N wins). Between rounds the board (tableau_duree s), then everyone back to their starting place and a new countdown (manche.nouvelle).
  • Its screens (delivered as data, modules/manche/ecrans, one screen per moment, no shape shared; each player sees their own): "Lancer la partie" (the host's button Bouton Lancer, action lancer), "Attente des joueurs" (Message: the wait, or "The host will start the game" for a guest), "Compte à rebours" (Chiffre), "Départ" (Partez), "Annonces" (Éliminé, Arrivé), "Bandeau de la partie" (Chrono, Place in gold, Info: points, lives or team, optional; Course: the Race's line, optional), "Tableau entre deux manches" (Titre, the list Lignes with its row Ligne, Suite) and "Classement final" (Titre, Lignes/Ligne; drawn above the end screen's veil). Change their look with styles and the theme in the screen editor, never by editing the system or its script; the script fills their texts (game.screen_set, game.screen_list).
  • On screen (each player their own, afficher): the banner at the top centre on a dark translucent background: the stopwatch or time left and the place "2nd / 4" in gold, then the points or lives (in teams on points: every team's score in its color, "Rouges 6 · Bleus 4 / 10"), and the race's lap and checkpoint; in the middle, each on its own screen, the countdown, "Go!", "Finished! 1st" or "Out"; the board between rounds. hud_y no longer moves the banner: move the shape "Fond" of "Bandeau de la partie" in the screen editor (when something else uses the top, such as the row of alterations at y 0.07).
  • The end: each player gets their own result through game.finish (Victory, their place, their time or points); the full ranking stays on everyone's screen, under the end title and above its darkening (an element whose role ends with .fin, or inside a panel that has one, is drawn over the engine's end screen: a game's own end board can do the same). manche.terminee {classement, gagnant}.
  • Events for the game: manche.nouvelle {numero, total}, manche.depart {numero}, manche.resultat {joueur, temps?, rang?, elimine?}, manche.fin {numero, classement, raison, gagnant} (each round), manche.terminee. React to them in your scripts (a sound at Go, fireworks for the winner).
  • Change a rule without touching the module (game.override): manche.texte (any text: function(default, cle, joueur) if cle == "partez" then return "Allez !" end return default(cle, joueur) end), manche.points (double points at the end, points refused), manche.classement (reorder the ranking for your own rule).
  • It sets players' speed and jump during the countdown and back to normal at Go: do not fight it with game.set_player_speed in the same moments.

Several players¶

One for the whole game, on the host: it decides arrivals, points, eliminations and the end; each player (guests included) sees their own stopwatch, place, score and result. A guest who joins during the countdown gets a place and is frozen like the others; one who joins later plays from there. Prove it with game_run « players »: the report shows each guest's HUD (Bandeau de la partie/Chrono, Bandeau de la partie/Place).
