Particle effect¶

Particles coming out of the object: a campfire, a torch, smoke, sparks, magic, rain or snow around the player. Choose the effect, its size, its colour and its amount; it loops or plays once. For an effect on its own, Create > Particle effect places an invisible box that carries it.
Identifier particules
Version 1
Placed on objects yes
Run by the engine yes: the engine runs it itself, without a script

In the Game tab, Systems section, by opening the system's card.

None.

Particle effect (particules)¶

An object carrying this system gives off particles, simulated by the game and drawn by the renderer: a campfire, a torch, smoke, sparks, magic, rain or snow. The system has no script: the engine runs it. The same game gives the same particles, and the game report counts them under particles.

  • Place it on the object the effect comes from (systems_attach): a campfire model, a torch, a cauldron. For an effect on its own (a fire in the middle of a camp, snow over a valley), create a box (builtin:cube) with visible: false, no collider, then attach the system on it: a hidden box still gives off its effect.
  • Per object (systems_set): effet (feu, fumee, etincelles, magie, pluie, neige, poussiere, eclats, eclats_bois, eclats_pierre, explosion, impact), taille (1 a campfire, 0.4 a torch; for rain and snow, the width of the area), teinte (effet, or choisie with couleur "#rrggbb": a blue fire), quantite (the number of particles, multiplied), boucle (true: runs while the object is there; false: plays once at the start), duree (seconds; 0 endless when looping), hauteur (metres above the object's base: the tip of a torch).
  • Fire, explosion, magic, sparks and hit give their own light: it follows the effect, flickers with a fire, grows with taille, and goes out with the effect. Fire, explosion and magic cast shadows from it (a crate next to a campfire casts its shadow); the object carrying the effect does not. « lumiere »: effet (default: its light, with its shadows), sans_ombres (the light alone, lighter to draw), aucune (no light). « force_lumiere »: multiplied (0.5 a discreet glow, 2 a blaze). Never add a placed light next to a fire made with this system: it already lights. These lights count with the placed lights (the closest first) and follow the shadow quality (world_set environment shadowQuality); they change nothing in the game, only the image.
  • On a visible object, the effect stops while the object is hidden and comes back when it is shown; destroyed, it stops. game.object_effect(id, false) puts it out, game.object_effect(id, true) lights it again; the Trigger does the same with « Jouer un effet » (effet « objet ») and « Arrêter un effet ».
  • Rain or snow over the whole game: place the system on the player, effet pluie or neige, taille 1 to 2: it follows them.
  • A one-off effect from a script, at a place or on an object: game.effect("explosion", pos), game.effect("eclats", rock) (chips take the rock's colour). See the script reference.
  • What the engine already does: a little dust when the player lands (landingDust of the player), chips and dust when an object bursts or collapses (game.effect(id, "burst")), dust where a falling object lands. The Gathering system sends chips at each hit, and the Combat system a hit impact; each has a setting to turn them off.
  • Effects follow the graphics quality, on three levels (High, Medium, Low: 6,000, 3,000 and 1,500 particles at once; fewer and slightly larger particles given off, simpler light on smoke at lower levels). An effect never breaks the game, it only looks lighter. A game run by the agent keeps High, so its report does not depend on the machine; the report gives the level under particles.quality and particles.budget.
  • The fire preset already has its flames, a bright core, a warm glow, embers and smoke: do not stack several fire effects to make one campfire, raise taille or quantite instead. In the editor, a visible effect animates on its own (30 frames per second in the active view, 10 in the others).

Several players¶

The engine runs it once for the whole game: effects are shown on the host.
