Health bar¶

Shows the player's health on screen, as a bar and in numbers.
Identifier barre_de_vie
Version 2
Placed on objects no
Also activates Health

Settings for the whole game¶

In the Game tab, Systems section, by opening the system's card.

Setting What it does Name in code Type Default Limits or choices
Horizontal position (0 to 1) Where the bar starts, from left to right: 0 at the left edge of the screen, 1 at the right edge. 0.02 puts it almost against the left edge. x Number 0.02 from 0 to 1
Vertical position (0 to 1) Where the bar sits, from top to bottom: 0 at the top of the screen, 1 at the bottom. 0.03 puts it at the very top. y Number 0.03 from 0 to 1
Width (share of the screen) The length of the full bar, as a share of the screen width: 0.25 is a quarter of the screen. largeur Number 0.25 from 0.02 to 1
Height (share of the screen) The thickness of the bar, as a share of the screen height: 0.025 gives a thin bar, 0.05 a thick one. The numbers grow with it. hauteur Number 0.025 from 0.005 to 0.2
Color (#RRGGBB) The color of the remaining health, written #RRGGBB (red, green, blue in hexadecimal), for example #d94848 for a soft red. A badly written color gives red. couleur Text #d94848
Show the numbers Writes the remaining and maximum health to the right of the bar, for example "75 / 100". chiffres Yes or no yes

Health bar (barre_de_vie)¶

Shows the player's health on screen, from the health module (sante), which it activates if missing. The player must carry sante (systems_attach on the player): otherwise the bar never shows. It does nothing else, and is not placed on objects.

  • It draws the HUD keys barre_de_vie, barre_de_vie.fond and barre_de_vie.chiffres: do not reuse them.
  • Settings: position, width and height as fractions of the screen, color as #RRGGBB, and whether the numbers show.
  • It shows the player only. For a bar above enemies, say it is not available yet.
  • A full bar that never moves usually means nothing in the game sends sante.blesser to the player.

Several players¶

One copy per player: each player sees their own health bar (the health of their own character, sante.change with cible = that player).
