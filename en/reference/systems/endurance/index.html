Stamina¶

Placed on the player: running, jumping and striking tire them. Exhausted, they can only walk until they recover. Shows its bar under the health bar.
Identifier endurance
Version 3
Placed on objects yes

Settings for the whole game¶

In the Game tab, Systems section, by opening the system's card.

Setting What it does Name in code Type Default Limits or choices
Show the bar Shows the stamina bar on screen, under the health bar. It fades while the player is exhausted. afficher Yes or no yes
Horizontal position (0 to 1) Where the bar starts, from left to right: 0 at the left edge of the screen, 1 at the right edge. 0.02 lines it up with the health bar. x Number 0.02 from 0 to 1
Vertical position (0 to 1) Where the bar sits, from top to bottom: 0 at the top of the screen, 1 at the bottom. 0.065 puts it just under the health bar. y Number 0.065 from 0 to 1
Width (share of the screen) The length of the full bar, as a share of the screen width: 0.25 is a quarter of the screen. largeur Number 0.25 from 0.02 to 1
Height (share of the screen) The thickness of the bar, as a share of the screen height: 0.015 gives a thin bar, 0.03 a thick one. hauteur Number 0.015 from 0.005 to 0.2
Color (#RRGGBB) The color of the remaining stamina, written #RRGGBB (red, green, blue in hexadecimal), for example #e0b83c for a golden yellow. A badly written color gives that yellow. couleur Text #e0b83c

Settings on each object¶

In the Properties of an object or folder that carries the system, Systems section. A folder passes its values on to what it contains; an object can change a value for itself only.

Setting What it does Name in code Type Default Limits or choices
Maximum stamina The full stamina reserve, which the costs are compared with: with 100 and running at 20 per second, the player runs for 5 seconds in a row. max Number 100 from 1 to 100 000
Running cost (per second) The stamina spent per second of running. Higher, the player runs out of breath sooner; 0: running is not tiring. course Number 20 from 0 to 100 000
Cost of a jump The stamina spent at each jump. 0: jumping is not tiring, and stays possible even when exhausted. saut Number 15 from 0 to 100 000
Cost of a hit (Combat module) The stamina spent at each hit of the Combat system. 0: striking is not tiring. attaque Number 10 from 0 to 100 000
Recovery (per second) The stamina regained per second of rest: 25 with a maximum of 100 refills the reserve in 4 seconds. recuperation Number 25 from 0 to 100 000
Delay before recovering (s) How many seconds after an effort stamina starts coming back. Longer, the player really has to catch their breath before recovering. delai Number 1 from 0 to 30
Exhausted: % to regain before running again At zero, the player can no longer run, jump or strike until they regain this share of their stamina, as a percentage of the maximum: 30, they wait until they are back to 30 %. reprise Number 30 from 1 to 100

Events¶

The descriptions come from the code.

What it announces¶

Event Fields Description
endurance.change valeur (number), max (number), epuise (boolean) The player's stamina changed. epuise is true from zero until they regain a share of it: meanwhile they cannot run, jump or strike.

What it listens to¶

Event Fields Description
combat.attaque source (id) Someone struck, whether it hit or not: the stamina module counts it.

What the agent knows about this system¶

The text the agent reads before using this system.

Stamina (endurance)¶

Place it on the player (systems_attach): running, jumping and striking (with combat.basique) cost stamina; resting gives it back after a short delay. At zero, the player is exhausted: they can still walk, but not run, jump or strike until they regain a share of it (reprise). Placed on anything else, it does nothing.

  • The player's values (systems_set): maximum, costs, recovery, delay, share to regain.
  • For the whole game (modules_set): the bar under the health bar (HUD keys endurance and endurance.fond), dimmed while exhausted, its place, size and color.
  • Know the value: listen to endurance.change (valeur, max, epuise), announced on the first step, then when it changes.
  • Only running, jumping and the strikes of combat.basique cost stamina. If the user wants another action to tire the player, say it is not available yet.
  • It uses game.set_player_can_run and game.set_player_can_jump: do not call them yourself while this module is in the game.

Several players¶

One copy per player: each player has their own stamina gauge, exhaustion and bar.
