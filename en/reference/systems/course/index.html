Race¶

A race without code, on top of the Round: numbered checkpoints (the system placed on objects), passed in order (a skipped checkpoint does not count), a finish line that only counts after the last one, laps, and the return to the last checkpoint passed (a fall, a "return" zone, a hazard, or a key for an overturned vehicle). Everyone sees their lap, their checkpoint and their place.
Identifier course
Version 3
Placed on objects yes
Also activates Round

Events¶

The descriptions come from the code.

What it announces¶

Event Fields Description
course.point joueur (id), numero (number), tour (number), cible (id) A player passed the checkpoint they had to pass (Race module): numero its number, tour the lap, cible the object.
course.tour joueur (id), tour (number), total (number) A player finished a lap (Race module): tour is the lap just finished, total the number of laps.
course.retour joueur (id), cible (id?), raison (text) A player (or their vehicle) was put back at their last checkpoint, facing the next (Race module). raison: chute, zone, danger, touche, mort or script.
manche.arrivee joueur (id) A player arrived (the Race at the end of the last lap, a zone or a script): the Round module notes their time. Counts once per round.
manche.progression joueur (id), valeur (number) How far a player got, a number that grows as they advance (the Race: laps and checkpoints passed, then the share of the way to the next). The Round module ranks those who have not arrived by it.

What it listens to¶

Event Fields Description
manche.nouvelle numero (number), total (number) A round is set up (Round module): every player is at their starting place, frozen during the countdown. numero counts from 1; total is the setting manches. The Race resets its laps here.
manche.depart numero (number), total (number) The countdown is over: Go! (Round module). The stopwatch of the round starts now.
manche.fin numero (number), total (number), classement (table), raison (text), gagnant (text?) A round ended (Round module). classement: the ranking, best first, a list of rows {joueur or equipe, nom, rang, temps?, points, vies, progression, elimine, arrive}; gagnant: the first's player id or team name; raison: arrivee, limite, score or dernier.
course.replacer joueur (id) Ask the Race module to put a player back at their last checkpoint, facing the next one (a trap, a button of the game).
joueur.reapparu joueur (id), cible (id), position (vector) A dead player stands up again (death and respawn module): respawned, or brought back by sante.ranimer.
joueur.depart joueur (id), numero (number) Sent by the engine on the host when a guest leaves the game (they quit, or their connection was lost and they did not come back in time, the game setting Place kept after a drop): their character is removed right after. A module forgets what it kept for them.
langue.change langue (text) Sent by the engine when the player changes the game's language (game.set_language, the Menu options). A module redraws what it already shows: its texts go through game.t and game.language.

Points a script can replace (game.override)¶

The descriptions come from the code.

  • course.texte: function(default, key, joueur) -> the text shown for that key: tour (Lap {n}/{total}), point (Checkpoint {n}/{total}), rate (a checkpoint was missed). The default is the built-in text in the game's language.

Race (course)¶

Checkpoints, laps and the return to the last checkpoint, on top of the Round (manche, added with it, mode temps) which does the countdown, the stopwatch, the ranking and the end (ADR-045). For any race (on foot, by car, by boat, an obstacle course), use this instead of scripting checkpoints, laps or respawns.

  • Checkpoints: objects carrying this system, sorte point with numero 1, 2, 3... in the order of passage: an arch, a ring, or an invisible box (builtin:cube, visible: false, no collider) the size of the passage, 1 m deep or more. Passed by entering the object's box (rayon 0), or within rayon metres of its centre. Only the expected checkpoint counts: a skipped one does not, and the player must go back for it. Two objects with the same number are two ways through the same checkpoint.
  • The start and finish line: one object sorte arrivee. It closes each lap, only after the last checkpoint. Without it, the last checkpoint closes the lap. Put the Round's starting places (manche on markers) just behind it.
  • Laps (tours, 1). After the last lap, the player has arrived: the Round notes their time and place.
  • Back to the last checkpoint (facing the next one; their vehicle too if they drive): falling below hauteur_chute (chute), entering an object sorte retour (a pit, the sea, off track), the event named in evenement_retour (lave.contact by default, with joueur or cible: a lava or a trap of your game), a respawn of the mort module, the control course_replacer (Backspace; touche) for an overturned car or a stuck player, or game.emit("course.replacer", {joueur = id}). Before any checkpoint, back at their start.
  • Progress: sent to the Round (manche.progression): laps and checkpoints passed, then the share of the way to the next. That is the place each player sees while racing, and the ranking of those who did not finish at the time limit.
  • On screen (each player their own, afficher): "Lap 1/3 Checkpoint 2/5" inside the Round's top banner, under its stopwatch (the optional shape Course of its screen "Bandeau de la partie", filled with game.screen_set; deleted from the screen, the line is not shown) (when the Round's afficher is off: its own screen « Avancée dans la course », Fond and Ligne, which the user restyles in the screen editor; never draw it yourself, and do not name another screen like it); the place is the Round's. Texts: override course.texte.
  • Events: course.point {joueur, numero, tour, cible}, course.tour {joueur, tour, total}, course.retour {joueur, cible, raison}. A sound or an effect at each checkpoint: listen to course.point.
  • Check it with game_run « players » and the report's HUD of each guest; measure the track (game.bounds) for hauteur_chute, never guess.

Several players¶

One for the whole game, on the host: it watches every player's position; each sees their own lap, checkpoint and place.
