Crafting¶

Recipes that turn what the player carries into something else: three wood and two stones make an axe. One crafts from a window (C), bare-handed or in front of a workbench: an object of the world carrying this system, such as a campfire or a forge, which allows its own recipes.
Identifier fabrication
Version 2
Placed on objects yes
Also activates Inventory, Interaction
Also needs, on the same object Interaction

Events¶

The descriptions come from the code.

What it announces¶

Event Fields Description
fabrication.fabrique objet (text), quantite (number), etabli (text) The player crafted a recipe: its ingredients left the inventory and objet (quantite of it) came in. etabli is the workbench where it was made, empty bare-handed.
fabrication.manque objet (text), manque (text?), etabli (text?) A recipe could not be crafted: manque lists what is missing, in the player's language ("2 Wood, 1 Stone"), or etabli names the workbench it needs.
inventaire.ajouter objet (text), quantite (number), joueur (id?) Ask the inventory to add items, such as a reward. Answered by inventaire.change, or inventaire.plein. With several players, joueur says whose bag; without it, the player whose action is being handled, or every player.
inventaire.retirer objet (text), quantite (number) Ask the inventory to remove items, such as a payment. Answered by inventaire.change, or inventaire.manque if there are not enough.

What it listens to¶

Event Fields Description
fabrication.ouvrir etabli (text?) Ask the crafting system to open its window, with the recipes of the workbench named etabli (empty or missing: bare-handed): a Trigger rule, a button of a drawn screen.
fabrication.fabriquer objet (text), etabli (text?) Ask the crafting system to craft the recipe that gives objet, as its button would, at the workbench named etabli (empty or missing: bare-handed). Refused, with fabrication.manque, if something is missing.
interaction.utiliser cible (id), joueur (id) The player used the object in reach: a door, a merchant, an item to pick up. Whoever owns that object decides what it does.
inventaire.change objet (text), quantite (number), total (number), chargement (boolean?) The quantity of an item changed. total is the new quantity. chargement is true when a saved game gives the items back (game.load): nothing was picked up.
inventaire.plein objet (text) An item could not be added: every slot holds another kind of item.
inventaire.manque objet (text), quantite (number) Items could not be removed: there are not enough.
hud.clic cible (text), joueur (id?) A button of the HUD was clicked: pressed, then released on it; or chosen with A (Enter) while it has the gamepad or keyboard focus; or the window's back button (back = true), with B (Escape). cible is the button's key (game.hud_button), joueur the player who clicked (a guest clicks on their own computer; the host plays the click). Announced by the engine, before the scripts' on_update of that step.

Points a script can replace (game.override)¶

The descriptions come from the code.

  • fabrication.permise: function(default, recette) -> true, or false and the reason shown (text): whether this recipe can be crafted now. recette is its title (the product's original name). The default is true. To unlock a recipe with a quest, a book read, a level.
  • fabrication.texte: function(default, key) -> the text the crafting window shows for that key: titre, titre_etabli, aucune, fabriquer, fermer, fabrique, manque, place, il_faut, refus. It may hold {objet}, {n} and {etabli}, replaced afterwards (in the player's language). The default is the game's translation of the text in the original language (translations_get lists them as built-in texts of the crafting window); without one, French or English, after the player's language.

Crafting (fabrication)¶

Recipes that turn items of the inventory into another item: three wood and two stones make an axe. Add it once with modules_add (with inventaire and interaction). Use it whenever the user talks about crafting, making, cooking, forging or building tools: never write a crafting script.

Its game setting recettes (modules_set) is a list of rows, in order. A recette row starts a recipe; the ingredient rows under it are what it needs.

  • recette: objet (the item it gives, an inventory kind, in the user's language; it also names the recipe), quantite (how many one crafting gives), etabli (the workbench needed, by its name; empty: bare-handed, anywhere).
  • ingredient: ingredient (an inventory kind), nombre, garde (on: the player must have it but keeps it, a tool such as a hammer).
  • Example, an axe by hand and cooked meat at a campfire: [{"sorte": "recette", "objet": "Hache", "quantite": 1}, {"sorte": "ingredient", "ingredient": "Bois", "nombre": 3}, {"sorte": "ingredient", "ingredient": "Pierre", "nombre": 2}, {"sorte": "recette", "objet": "Viande cuite", "etabli": "Feu de camp"}, {"sorte": "ingredient", "ingredient": "Viande crue", "nombre": 1}].
  • A workbench is an object of the world carrying this system (systems_attach; interaction comes with it): using it (E) opens the window with its recipes and the bare-handed ones. Its name is nom_etabli, or its name without a trailing number: a « Feu de camp 3 » is a « Feu de camp ». A piece the player builds with construction keeps its model's systems: a campfire model carrying fabrication becomes a workbench wherever it is built.
  • The window: the control craft (C, or the left stick pressed on a gamepad) opens it with the bare-handed recipes, plus those of a workbench within reach; it closes with the same key or Close (B / Escape). A recipe lacking something shows in grey with what is missing (montrer_impossibles). pause_fenetre stops the world meanwhile.
  • Spell every item exactly as the inventory stores it: the same kind as what collecte gives (ressource) and what the player picks up.
  • Events announced: fabrication.fabrique (objet, quantite, etabli), fabrication.manque (objet, manque or etabli). Send fabrication.ouvrir (etabli?) to open the window from a declencheur rule or a drawn screen's button, fabrication.fabriquer (objet, etabli?) to craft without the window.
  • Reserve a recipe until something happened (a book read, a quest done) from your own script, never the module's: game.override("fabrication.permise", function(default, recette) if recette == "Arc" and not appris then return false, game.t("Il faut d'abord lire le livre du chasseur.") end return default(recette) end). A refused recipe is not shown.
  • Texts follow the player's language; its own texts (Crafting, Craft, Missing...) are built-in texts listed by translations_get. Change one word with game.override("fabrication.texte", ...).
  • Saved games: nothing of its own to keep (the inventory keeps the items).
  • Gamepad and keyboard: the window's buttons get the focus like every window; B or Escape closes it (fabrication.fermer). HUD keys start with fabrication..
  • Testing: in game_run, give the ingredients with inventaire.ajouter in a test script (or gather them), press craft, then send fabrication.fabriquer or click fabrication.faire.1 (the first recipe shown); read fabrication.fabrique and the inventory in the report's hud.

Several players¶

One copy per player: each player has their own window and ingredients (their own inventory); a workbench serves whoever uses it.
