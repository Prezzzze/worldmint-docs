Loot¶

Loot tables, rolled when an enemy dies, a chest opens, on harvest, at the end of a quest or by the Trigger: what drops, with its rarity (color, glow), its random bonuses and its generated name for unique copies. On the ground, in the inventory, or in a "Take all" window.
Identifier butin
Version 6
Placed on objects yes
Also activates Inventory

Events¶

The descriptions come from the code.

What it announces¶

Event Fields Description
butin.tombe table (text), nombre (number), cible (text?), joueur (id?) Loot was rolled and placed (on the ground, in the inventory or in a content): how many lines dropped.
butin.ramasse objet (text), quantite (number), fiche (number?), joueur (id) A player took a piece of loot (from the ground or a Take all window). fiche: the unique item's record, if it is one.
butin.refus cible (text), joueur (id), raison (text) The host refused a player taking loot reserved for someone else (sharing personal, killer or in turn).
butin.ouvrir cible (text), joueur (id?) Open the Take all window of a content (a body, a chest) for a player.
butin.contenu cible (text), nombre (number) A content (a body, a chest) changed: its Take all windows redraw.
butin.prendre cible (text), numero (number?), joueur (id) A player asks the host to take from a content: one line (numero) or all they may take.
inventaire.ajouter objet (text), quantite (number), joueur (id?), fiche (number?) Ask the inventory to add items, such as a reward. Answered by inventaire.change, or inventaire.plein. With several players, joueur says whose bag; without it, the player whose action is being handled, or every player.
inventaire.retirer objet (text), quantite (number), fiche (number?) Ask the inventory to remove items, such as a payment. Answered by inventaire.change, or inventaire.manque if there are not enough. For a kind with unique copies (version 15): fiche removes that copy, otherwise the first ones; their records are destroyed.
inventaire.sortir objet (text), fiche (number?), case (text?), joueur (id?) Ask the inventory to take one item out of the bag without destroying it (version 16): objet is its kind, fiche a unique copy (it keeps its record; whoever takes it says its new holder). The Equipment module takes what it makes the player wear. A stack gives one from its smallest stack, or from case (version 21), a slot's key.
butin.sac joueur (id), cible (id?), nombre (number), position (vector) A dead player dropped their bag (Loot setting mort_sac): cible is the bag left where they fell (nil: each item on the ground), nombre the lines dropped.

What the agent knows about this system¶

The text the agent reads before using this system.

Loot (butin)¶

Loot tables rolled by the host (ADR-039 § 8 to § 10). It needs the inventory. Never edit its scripts: change its settings, listen to its events, or use game.override from a script of the game.

  • Tables (tables: nom, tirages, tirages_max, rien from 0 to 1) and their rows (lignes: table, objet or sous_table, poids, or pourcentage tried apart on each roll, quantite, quantite_max, garanti once per table, conditions niveau_min, niveau_max, joueur_min, joueur_max, zone, quete). A loop between tables (a -> b -> a) is refused: nothing drops and the reason goes to the log.
  • Rarities (raretes, from the most common to the rarest; five by default, Commun to Légendaire): nom, couleur (the item's name, its glow on the ground), chance (a weight), bonus (how many random bonuses), multiplicateur, chance_gemmes (tried gemmes_max times), son, lueur. Only kinds with uniques ticked in the inventory's sortes_objets get a rarity, bonuses and a generated name; a stack stays a stack.
  • Random bonuses (bonus_hasard): valeur (a Progression value: degats, sante_max...), min, max (at item level 1), pourcent, categories (comma separated, the kind's categorie), rarete_min, nom (a piece of name) and place (apres, avant). The item level is the enemy's (event progression.niveau_ennemi) or the object setting niveau; ranges grow with it along croissance_forme and croissance_taux (the Progression's growth curve).
  • Sources: an object carrying this system with table (an enemy) drops it on sante.mort; collecte rows (a resource, a table, a chance); quetes rows (a quest, a table); butin.lacher {table, cible or position, niveau, joueur} (the Trigger's action « Lâcher un butin », a script); chests (module coffres, through butin.remplir).
  • Appearance (apparition): sol: a copy of the kind's model (modeles, else the inventory's en_main model of that kind, else modele_defaut, all set by the creator; nothing is assumed of a pack; with none, forme_sans_modele: a small engine cube or sphere in its rarity color, taille_forme metres wide (0.15, measured on the shape), set on the ground; or into the inventory), glowing in its rarity color in the world, hidden by what passes in front, by day and night, with no asset (lueur; style_lueur: faisceau, a column of light rising from it, or etincelles, sparkles around it; taille_lueur, 1 for a beam about 1.5 m high; light enough for 50 items on the ground), a floating name (nom_flottant, distance_nom), gone after disparition seconds (once empty, it shows « Plus rien. » for 1.5 s and closes by itself; fenetres.fermer closes it); a model must not carry the « inventaire » system itself; inventaire; fenetre: the body becomes usable and opens the « Tout prendre » window, delivered as a screen the user restyles in the screen editor: « Butin » (Fenêtre, Titre, Vide, the list Objets: one row per item, Image and the button Objet, Objet#2 the second; Tout prendre, Fermer; actions prendre, tout, fermer); a refusal shows « Message du butin » (Message). Never draw these windows yourself, and do not name another screen like these. The floating names follow the world and stay HUD (butin.nom.<object>).
