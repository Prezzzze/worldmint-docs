Enemies¶

What carries this system patrols, chases the player when it sees them, and hits them in reach, with its own values: an early skeleton and a boss have neither the same speed nor the same damage. They walk around walls.
Identifier ennemis
Version 6
Placed on objects yes
Also activates Health
Also needs, on the same object Health

Events¶

The descriptions come from the code.

What it announces¶

Event Fields Description
ennemi.etat cible (id), etat (text) An enemy changed state: patrouille (patrols), poursuite (chases the player), attaque (hits in reach), attente (waits its turn near the player, when enough others are already striking), retour (goes back to its spot), mort (dead, does nothing more).
combat.coup cible (id), source (id), montant (number) An attack hit something. The health module, if present, has been asked to remove the damage.
sante.blesser cible (id), montant (number), source (id?) Ask the health module to remove health from an object.

What it listens to¶

Event Fields Description
scene.change depuis (text), vers (text) Sent by the engine once the player has arrived in another scene of the game (game.go_to, or a passage). Scripts keep running across scenes: this is when a module looks at the objects of the new scene.
sante.mort cible (id), source (id?) An object's health reached zero. What happens next belongs to the game: the health module does nothing else, unless its setting says to destroy the object.
sante.change cible (id), vie (number), max (number) An object's health changed, or is announced once when the game starts.

Enemies (ennemis)¶

Objects carrying this system become characters with a state machine: they patrol around their spot, chase the player once they see them, hit them in reach, and go back home when they lose them. Placing it on an object places Health there too. Add it only if the user asked for enemies that attack.

  • Place it on each kind of enemy, best on a group: "Early skeletons" with low damage and speed, "Late skeletons" with high values, the boss alone with its own. Set them with systems_set, and the Health values (health, armor) the same way. A child can change one value for itself.
  • Several enemies at once: set the game setting attaquants_max (modules_set), usually 1 or 2. Only that many strike at the same time; the others wait their turn at distance_attente, circling the player, and step in when a place is free. With the default 0, all strike together: five skeletons in reach kill the player in seconds.
  • States: patrouille, poursuite, attaque, attente (waits its turn), retour, mort. Each change is announced by ennemi.etat (cible, etat): listen to it for a growl, an alert icon, a music change.
  • They walk around obstacles (objects with a collider) to reach the player or go back home. A closed room keeps them out. With vue on, they still have to see the player first.
  • With vue on, walls with a collider hide the player; they give up after 3 s without seeing them, or beyond abandon.
  • Their hits go through the health module, like the player's: the player's armor applies. They are announced by combat.coup. When the player dies, they all go back home. A dead enemy stops; it is removed only if its health values say so.
  • Animations: give the clip names of their model (read them with animation_clips); without them, nothing plays.
  • Enemies cloned during the game from one carrying the system join in within half a second, with its values.

Several players¶

One for the whole game: each enemy chases the nearest living player it sees and keeps them while it sees them; when that player falls or is lost, it looks for another, or goes home.
