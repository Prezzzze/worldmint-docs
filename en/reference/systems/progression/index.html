Progression¶

Experience, levels, points and characteristics (ADR-038). Tracks (the character, cooking, woodcutting...) each with its curve; XP for defeated enemies, quests, what is crafted or gathered and any free rule; rewards at each level (points, bonuses, knowledges, items); characteristics to spend points on; required levels; an XP bar, "Level 5!" and the Character window (P). The host keeps everyone's progression.
Identifier progression
Version 8
Placed on objects no

Events¶

The descriptions come from the code.

What it announces¶

Event Fields Description
progression.depenser joueur (id?), caracteristique (text) Ask the Progression module to spend points on one rank of a characteristic (its name, as in its row) for the player: the host checks the points and the maximum, then answers progression.depense or progression.refus. The Character window sends it.
progression.reinitialiser joueur (id?) Ask the Progression module to give back the points spent on characteristics (if the game allows it, for its cost). Answers progression.reinitialise or progression.refus.
progression.depense joueur (id), caracteristique (text), rang (number) A rank of a characteristic was bought: rang is its new rank. Its bonuses are already applied.
progression.refus joueur (id), raison (text) Something was refused to the player by the Progression module: a required level (an item, a door, a zone), points missing, a maximum reached. raison is the text shown, already in the player's language.
progression.recompense joueur (id), piste (text), niveau (number), sorte (text), nom (text), nombre (number) A level gave one of its rewards: sorte is points, bonus, connaissance, objet, marque or evenement; nom its kind of points, value, knowledge, item, mark or event; nombre how many.
progression.niveau_ennemi cible (id), niveau (number) The Progression module gave an enemy its level (fixed, per zone, matched to the players or growing with time): its health and damage grew with it. To show it above its head.
progression.perte joueur (id), piste (text), xp (number), recuperable (boolean) The player died and lost xp on a track (never below its level). recuperable: the XP waits where they died.
progression.recupere joueur (id), piste (text), xp (number) The player came back where they died and recovered the xp lost on a track.
progression.reinitialise joueur (id), points (number) The characteristics were reset: points is how many points came back.
savoir.donner_points points (number), joueur (id?) Ask the knowledge system to give points to the player (version 26), to spend in the knowledge window: a level gained, a book read.
savoir.apprendre connaissance (text), joueur (id?) Ask the knowledge system to teach connaissance to the player (version 26), even without what it needs first: a Trigger rule, a master in a Dialogue, a quest's reward. A game script can still refuse it (savoir.permise).
inventaire.ajouter objet (text), quantite (number), joueur (id?), fiche (number?) Ask the inventory to add items, such as a reward. Answered by inventaire.change, or inventaire.plein. With several players, joueur says whose bag; without it, the player whose action is being handled, or every player.
inventaire.retirer objet (text), quantite (number), fiche (number?) Ask the inventory to remove items, such as a payment. Answered by inventaire.change, or inventaire.manque if there are not enough. For a kind with unique copies (version 15): fiche removes that copy, otherwise the first ones; their records are destroyed.
dialogue.marquer marque (text), retirer (boolean?) Ask the dialogue system to set a mark, from a script or another system; with retirer true, to remove it. Dialogue conditions then see it.

What it listens to¶

Event Fields Description
progression.xp joueur (id?), cle (text), piste (text), gain (number), xp (number), niveau (number), source (text) Sent by the engine (ADR-038) when a player gained XP on a track (game.progress_add_xp): gain is what was given after the global multiplier and the cap, xp the track's total, niveau its level, source where it came from, cle the player's key.
progression.niveau joueur (id?), cle (text), piste (text), niveau (number), ancien (number) Sent by the engine for each level a player reached on a track (several levels at once: several events), also when an admin sets a level. The Progression module gives the rewards of that level; a Trigger rule can play a sound or open a door.
progression.depenser joueur (id?), caracteristique (text) Ask the Progression module to spend points on one rank of a characteristic (its name, as in its row) for the player: the host checks the points and the maximum, then answers progression.depense or progression.refus. The Character window sends it.
progression.depense joueur (id), caracteristique (text), rang (number) A rank of a characteristic was bought: rang is its new rank. Its bonuses are already applied.
progression.reinitialiser joueur (id?) Ask the Progression module to give back the points spent on characteristics (if the game allows it, for its cost). Answers progression.reinitialise or progression.refus.
progression.reinitialise joueur (id), points (number) The characteristics were reset: points is how many points came back.

Points a script can replace (game.override)¶

The descriptions come from the code.

  • progression.gain: function(default, joueur, piste, xp, source) -> the XP given (a number; 0: nothing). Called before each gain, after the multipliers (level gap, repetition, first time, zone; the global multiplier comes after, in the engine). piste is the track's key, source names where it comes from (ennemi:Loup, quete:Titre, fabrication:Hache, collecte:Bois, regle:event, recuperation). For double XP at night, a bonus with a ring, nothing for a track in a place.
  • progression.requis: function(default, joueur, sorte, nom, piste, niveau) -> true, or false and the text shown: whether the player may use what asks for a level. sorte: objet (hold or use an item), recette, connaissance, objet_monde (use an object of the world: a door), zone (enter a zone). The default compares the player's level on piste with niveau. To ask for a quest done, a knowledge, two tracks at once.
  • progression.texte: function(default, key) -> the text shown for that key: titre, niveau, niveau_court, gain, monte, points, caracteristiques, depenser, reinitialiser, reinit_cout, fermer, pas_de_points, max_atteint, refus_requis, perdu, recupere, rien, xp. It may hold {n}, {piste}, {xp}, {requis}, {nom}, {niveau}, {objet}, replaced afterwards (in the player's language). The default is the game's translation of the text in the original language; without one, French or English, after the player's language.

What the agent knows about this system¶

The text the agent reads before using this system.

Progression (progression)¶

Experience, levels, points to spend and characteristics (ADR-038): the « level up » of RPGs, survival and action games, as user data. Add it once with modules_add when the user asks for levels, XP, skills that rise with use, characteristics (strength, agility...) or talent points; never write an XP script. The engine keeps the progress of each player (in the world's save, under their key; only the host writes); this module decides where XP comes from, how much, and what a level gives. Change what it does through its settings, its events and its hooks (game.override), never by editing it.

Tracks (pistes)¶

Each row is a track: cle (identifier, lowercase: personnage, cuisine, bucheronnage), nom (shown, translated), icone, cachee (hidden in the window until its first XP), courbe (type curve: the XP of each level; the editor draws it, offers the presets « RPG classique », « Survie lente », « Arcade rapide », « Hardcore » and simulates the play time; its ecart sets what a stronger or weaker enemy gives), mort (aucune by default, part: lose mort_part % of the XP earned since the last level, never a level; recuperable: the same, but the XP waits where the player died, and coming back there returns it). The main track (the level others see) is the game setting progression.mainTrack (« personnage » by default). Tune a curve with progression_simulate, then modules_set.

Where XP comes from¶

  • Enemies: on each enemy group, in the ennemis system: xp (at its first level; 0: none), piste_xp (empty: the main track), its level niveau_mode = fixe (niveau), zone (random between niveau_min and niveau_max), joueurs (the players' average level + niveau_ecart, within niveau_min..niveau_max) or temps (+1 level every jours_par_niveau game days from niveau, up to niveau_plafond; a day is a sunrise of jour_nuit, or minutes_par_jour). Its level grows its health (croissance_sante, +8 % per level), damage (croissance_degats) and XP (croissance_xp), croissance_forme linear or exponential. Switch off with xp_ennemis.
  • Quests (quetes, on the quest row: xp, piste_xp), recipes (fabrication, on the recipe row: xp, piste_xp), resources (collecte, per object: xp, piste_xp); switches xp_quetes, xp_fabrication, xp_collecte. A quest of the whole group (quetes game setting groupe, its quetes.fin carries groupe: true) gives its XP to every player present, each on their own track with their own « +XP »; any other quest, to the player who finishes it.
  • Places discovered: xp_decouverte for the first entry in each zone (zone system).
  • Free rules (regles): evenement, cible (only for the object named), piste, xp, fois (toujours or une: once per player). E.g. dialogue.fin 20, savoir.appris 30 to recherche, boutique.achat 2 to commerce.
  • Anything else: emit progression.donner {xp, piste?, source?} from a script or a Trigger rule (envoyer).

How much¶

The amount is multiplied by: the level gap (ecart_actif, the curve's ecart); repetition (repetition_actif, repetition_seuil gains of the same source within repetition_fenetre seconds, then −repetition_baisse each, never below repetition_plancher); the first time (premiere_bonus, 0.5 = +50 % for the first enemy of a kind, first resource, first recipe); zones (zones: a zone's name without number, or a tag, and its multiplicateur); then the hook progression.gain; the engine applies the global multiplier (progression.xpMultiplier × a dedicated server's xp_multiplicateur) and the per-minute cap.

In a group (groupe): tous (default: each nearby player gets it all), partage (split between nearby players), degats (in proportion to the damage each dealt: Combat, Shooting and Enemies say who hit; with no damage known, the last hit gets it all), dernier (the last hit only). Nearby: within rayon_groupe metres of the enemy, or who hit it.

What a level gives (recompenses)¶

Rows: piste (empty: the main one), quand (tous, liste with niveaux « 5, 10, 20 », chaque every chaque levels from a_partir), sorte: points (points_sorte, nombre), bonus (valeur, ajout +n and/or pourcent +%, added for each such level: « +10 sante_max per level »), connaissance (nom: learnt in savoir), objet (nom, nombre: into the inventory), marque (nom: a Dialogue / quest mark), evenement (nom: an event with joueur, piste, niveau, for a Trigger rule: a sound, a door). Kinds of points: points rows (sorte, nom shown, savoir: those points go to the Knowledge system's tree, as savoir.donner_points). progression.niveau is also announced by the engine for each level.

Characteristics (caracteristiques)¶

A caracteristique row (nom, description, max rank, cout points per rank, points_sorte) followed by its effet rows (valeur, ajout per rank, pourcent per rank). No name is imposed (Force, Vigueur, Agilité are examples). The player spends in the Character window; reinitialiser allows a reset, for reinit_nombre × reinit_objet (empty: free).

Values¶

The module declares the values it drives, read by the other systems with their own setting as base: sante_max (Santé: max health; health follows when it rises), degats (Combat: the player's hits; Ennemis: an enemy's hits and shots), endurance_max, endurance_recuperation (Endurance), inventaire_places (Inventaire: slots), charge_max (Inventaire: the maximum load, when its weight poids_actif is on; « Force: +2 kg of load per point » is an effet row charge_max ajout 2, in the default characteristics), vitesse_recolte (Collecte: divides the hits a resource needs), vitesse_fabrication (Fabrication: divides a recipe's time), vitesse_deplacement (the engine: walk, run, swim; the inventory's weight adds a negative bonus from the source « poids », down to 0 when it stops the player). valeurs adds more for scripts (game.stat(player, name)). Without this module, those systems behave as before. Bonuses are given again from the saved progress at the start, when a player arrives and after a load.

Required levels (requis)¶

Rows: sorte (objet: holding or using an inventory item; recette; connaissance; objet_monde: using an object, a door, by its name without number; zone: entering a zone, the player is pushed back), nom, piste, niveau, message (with {niveau} and {piste}; empty: « Il faut le niveau 5 (Personnage). »). Checked on the host. The hook progression.requis can ask for more (a quest done, two tracks).

What the player sees¶

Above heads (noms_tetes, off by default since the engine shows no name above heads; distance_noms, 30 m): the name and main level of each other player nearby, « Léa · Niv. 5 », on every player's screen (theme role nom.joueur; the head is measured on the body). Turn it on when the user wants players to see each other's level.

A Group panel made by a game script: game.players() lists the players, game.player_name(id) their names and game.progress_level(id) their main level; both work in a guest's game too, for every player (the host sends them, protocol 17 and 24). E.g. one game.hud_text line per player, game.player_name(p) .. " · " .. game.progress_level(p), redrawn on progression.niveau and joueur.arrivee/joueur.depart. A Group panel already written by the game (Naufragés' « Groupe ») is a script of the game: to show the level there, edit that script (not a module), adding game.progress_level(p) after each player's name on the line it draws.

An XP bar of the main track with its level (barre, barre_x, barre_y, barre_largeur, barre_hauteur, barre_couleur); when the Inventory's quick bar is where the XP bar is set (at the bottom, by default), the XP bar sits just above it, as wide as it. « +45 XP » for a moment, above the bar on the right (afficher_gain, duree_gain; another track says its name: « +30 XP (Récolte) »); « Niveau 5 ! » (annoncer_niveau, son_niveau, effet_niveau, duree_niveau); the Character window (action character, key P; fenetre, pause_fenetre, largeur): one row per track (level, bar, XP), the points, the characteristics with their « Améliorer » buttons (« Maximum » at the top rank), the reset. A guest's clicks are checked by the host. Texts: hook progression.texte.

Its screens (version 8). These are screens the system delivers as data, each player seeing their own: « Barre d'expérience » (Fond, Barre, the level label Étiquette with Niveau; its place follows barre_x/barre_y/barre_largeur/barre_hauteur, or sits above the Inventory's « Barre rapide »), « Gain d'expérience » (Fond, Gain: « +45 XP », at the bar's right end), « Passage de niveau » (Annonce: « Niveau 5 ! »), « Messages de la progression » (Message, and Refus for a refusal, at the top while the window is closed) and « Fiche du personnage » (the Character window: Fenêtre, Titre, the list Pistes whose rows hold Piste with Nom, Niveau, Barre, XP, or Rien; Caractéristiques, Points, the list Liste des caractéristiques whose rows Rangée hold Caractéristique and the button Améliorer (action ameliorer), Réinitialiser (action reinitialiser), Message, Refus, Fermer (action fermer, B or Escape)). Their keys in game_run are screen/shape (Fiche du personnage/Améliorer#2, Gain d'expérience/Gain). To change their look, size, colours or texts' style, the user edits them in the screen editor (Game tab, Interface screens), or you change them with screens_set; never edit the module. largeur scales the window and its rows from the sizes of its file. Only the name above heads (progression.tete.<player>) is still drawn by the script.

Hooks¶

  • progression.gain: function(default, joueur, piste, xp, source) return xp * 2 end (double XP at night).
  • progression.requis: function(default, joueur, sorte, nom, piste, niveau) ... return false, "Finish the Old Mine first." end.
  • progression.texte: function(default, cle) if cle == "monte" then return "LEVEL {n}!" end return default(cle) end.

Presets by genre¶

  • RPG classique: one track personnage, curve « RPG classique », enemies fixe or zone, groupe tous, rewards 1 characteristic point per level, +10 sante_max per level, a talent point every 5 levels (savoir), quests 100 to 500 XP; Force +5 % degats and +2 charge_max (kg of load) per point.
  • Survie lente: tracks personnage plus recolte/fabrication (curve « Survie lente »), XP mostly from collecte and fabrication (5 to 20 each), enemies temps (+1 level every 2 days), repetition_actif, rewards vitesse_recolte +3 % per level of recolte; with the inventory's weight, +5 charge_max every 5 levels of personnage.
  • Arcade rapide: curve « Arcade rapide » (20 levels), enemies joueurs (the group's level), ecart_actif off, groupe partage, big « Niveau ! » announcements, +5 % degats per level.
  • Hardcore: curve « Hardcore », mort recuperable with mort_part 50, enemies zone, groupe dernier, repetition_actif, no reset (reinitialiser off).
