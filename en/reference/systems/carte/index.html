Map¶

A minimap in a corner of the screen, the player in the middle, and a big map of the whole scene with the M key, zoomed and moved with the mouse or the gamepad (triggers and stick). What carries this system shows on it as a marker: a shape and a color, or an image, and its name; always, once discovered up close, or only as a quest objective. Merchants, characters who talk, the objectives of quests in progress and, with several players, the other players with their names are marked by themselves. The player places their own markers, ticks what they want to see, and can follow a compass at the top of the screen.
Identifier carte
Version 12
Placed on objects yes

Events¶

The descriptions come from the code.

What it announces¶

Event Fields Description
carte.ouverte ouvert (boolean) The big map opened (ouvert true) or closed (ouvert false). The world is stopped while it is open, unless its setting says otherwise.
carte.decouvert cible (id) The player discovered a map marker (cible), one shown only once discovered: they came near enough, or carte.reveler revealed it. It now shows on the map.

What it listens to¶

Event Fields Description
carte.reveler cible (id) Ask the map to reveal the marker of cible, as if the player had come near: a character who tells where a place is, a scroll read, a quest step.
quetes.debut quete (text), groupe (boolean?) A quest started: quete is its title. groupe (Journal de quêtes 8): true for a quest of the whole group (game setting groupe), sent once for the group, joueur being who moved it on.
quetes.etape quete (text), etape (number), texte (text), groupe (boolean?) A step of a quest is done: etape is its rank in the quest (from 1), texte what it said. Sent before quetes.fin when it was the last one. groupe (Journal de quêtes 8): true for a quest of the whole group (game setting groupe), sent once for the group, joueur being who moved it on.
quetes.fin quete (text), xp (number?), piste (text?), groupe (boolean?) A quest is finished: all its steps are done. Its reward, if any, has been asked to the inventory. xp and piste (version 28): its XP for the Progression module and the track that gets it (empty: the main one). groupe (Journal de quêtes 8): true for a quest of the whole group (game setting groupe), sent once for the group, joueur being who moved it on.
quetes.echec quete (text), groupe (boolean?) A quest failed: an event, a mark, its time ran out, or quetes.echouer. It gives no reward; it may be given again if its settings allow it. groupe (Journal de quêtes 8): true for a quest of the whole group (game setting groupe), sent once for the group, joueur being who moved it on.
quetes.abandon quete (text), groupe (boolean?) The player abandoned a quest from the log (or quetes.abandonner asked for it). It gives no reward; it may be given again if its settings allow it. groupe (Journal de quêtes 8): true for a quest of the whole group (game setting groupe), sent once for the group, joueur being who moved it on.
scene.change depuis (text), vers (text) Sent by the engine once the player has arrived in another scene of the game (game.go_to, or a passage). Scripts keep running across scenes: this is when a module looks at the objects of the new scene.
menu.pause ouvert (boolean) The pause menu opened (ouvert true) or closed (ouvert false). The world is stopped while it is open.

Points a script can replace (game.override)¶

The descriptions come from the code.

  • carte.texte: function(default, key) -> the text the map shows for that key: titre, fermer, aide, decouvert, vous. It may hold {nom}, replaced afterwards. The default is the game's translation of the text in the original language (a built-in text of the map in translations_get); without one, French or English, after the player's language.
  • carte.couleur_joueur: function(default, joueur) -> the color ("#rrggbb") of that other player's arrow and name on the map, such as their team's color. The default is a color of their player number (game.player_number), the same for everyone.

What the agent knows about this system¶

The text the agent reads before using this system.

Map (carte)¶

A minimap in a corner of the screen, the player in the middle as an arrow, and a big map of the whole scene opened with the control carte (M, or ↓ on a gamepad), zoomed with the mouse wheel or with the controls carte_rapprocher (Page Up, or the right trigger RT on a gamepad) and carte_eloigner (Page Down, or the left trigger LT), held, and moved by dragging with the mouse, with the gamepad's left stick or with the walking keys. While the map has the gamepad's focus the player does not move, so LT (also run) does not make them run. Add it with modules_add when the user asks for a map, a minimap, markers or places to discover: do not build a map yourself.

The picture is the scene seen from above, taken by the engine when the scene starts (north, -Z, at the top), without the player and characters. The game setting dessin can give a drawn map instead (an image file): by default it covers the same square as the engine's view (dessin_zone "auto"); with dessin_zone "manuelle", dessin_x, dessin_z (its north-west corner) and dessin_cote (its side, in metres) say where it lies.

Markers, without code: place the system on an object (systems_attach), at its position, or on a group or folder: one marker for everything inside, sub-folders included, in the middle of the box enclosing its pieces (the folder's own position does not matter), discovered when the player comes within distance of that box. A piece inside with its own values for carte (systems_set on it) becomes its own marker. Its object settings:

  • nom: the name shown under it on the big map; empty, the name of the object or group without a trailing number. Write it in the user's language.
  • forme: rond, losange, triangle, carre, etoile, or image with image (an icon file, find one with assets_search and kind "image"); couleur ("#rrggbb", tints an image); taille (1 usual, up to 3).
  • visible: toujours; decouvert, shown once the player came within distance metres, or when your script or a declencheur rule sends carte.reveler {cible = id} (the object or group, or any piece of the group); cache, shown only while it is a quest objective.
  • quete and quete_etape: the exact title of a quetes quest (and a step number, 0 for the whole quest) for which this marker is the objective, such as the miller for "Go back to the miller".

What marks itself (game settings marchands and personnages, on by default): objects carrying boutique (a golden diamond) and dialogue (a light blue circle), named after the shop or the character, one marker per object or group they are placed on. Placing carte on one of them chooses its look instead.

Quest objectives (game setting objectifs, couleur_objectif): while a quetes step is active, the markers it names are ringed in that color, and on the minimap they stay at its edge, pointing the way. A step names objects through fin_cible (the "Loup" of "Vaincre 3 loups": every marker on an object or group named "Loup", "Loup 2"...), or a marker names its quest with quete. The quest log is not changed: the map only listens to quetes.debut, quetes.etape and quetes.fin.

Other game settings: mini_carte (shown or not), mini_coin (bas_droite by default, bas_gauche, haut_droite, haut_gauche; the inventory and the current quest step are at the top right), mini_taille (share of the screen height), mini_forme (ronde or carree), mini_etendue (metres shown from edge to edge), mini_tourne (turns with the player, otherwise north up), cadre_marge (the map tucked inside a theme frame), grande_carte (the M window), pause_fenetre (the world stops while it is open, and the big map is a screen of its own: the rest of the HUD, such as the hotbar, health or the quest tracker, is hidden with game.hud_only({"carte.", "Grande carte/"}) until it closes; a pause menu opened over it (menu.pause) still shows), noms (names on the big map), annoncer (a message when a marker is discovered).

  • Events announced: carte.ouverte (ouvert), carte.decouvert (cible: the object or group the system is placed on, once for all its pieces). Listened: carte.reveler (cible).
  • The minimap hides during a pause (a menu, a dialogue, a shop) and while the big map is open.
  • Saved games keep the places already discovered, in every scene (on_save, on_load): a loaded game shows them again without announcing them.
  • Texts follow the player's language: the map's own texts are built-in texts of the system, listed by translations_get (French and English are built in). To change a word, override carte.texte from a game script: game.override("carte.texte", function(default, key) if key == "titre" then return "Atlas" end return default(key) end). Keys: titre, fermer, aide, decouvert (with {nom}), vous; version 10: aide_repere (with {touche}), repere (with {n}), distance (with {d}), legende, quetes, s_objectifs, s_lieux, s_marchands, s_personnages, s_joueurs, s_mes_reperes, and the compass points n, ne, e, se, s, so, o, no.
  • When the world changes a lot (a bridge built, a forest burnt), game.map_refresh() from a script takes the picture again.
  • A frame from an interface pack (a ring around the minimap): not a script. Set the interface theme row {"sorte": "cadre", "role": "cadre.minicarte", "image": "<the ring>"} (cadre.carte for the big map): the engine draws it over the map, its markers and the north, under the player's arrow, and drops the dark outline of the minimap's disc. Then the game setting cadre_marge (a share of the map's size, 0 by default; 0.06 to 0.12 for a thick ring) tucks the map inside the frame so it does not show under the ring. Check it with capture_steps.
  • Its windows are screens delivered by the system (modules/carte/ecrans/, version 12), which its script shows and fills:
  • « Grande carte » (the big map): Fenêtre, Titre, Place (where the map picture goes: its height in the file is the map's height, the map stays square), Aide, Aide repère (hidden without reperes_joueur), Fermer (action fermer); on its right Légende with Titre de la légende and the list Sortes (row Sorte, a button, action legende), on its left Quêtes with Titre des quêtes and the list Liste des quêtes (row Quête, action quete). The two side panels are placed against the window by the script and hidden when empty.
  • « Lieux découverts » (top centre): Fond, the list Lignes (row Annonce, the last three).
  • « Mini-carte ronde » or « Mini-carte carrée » (after mini_forme): Fond, the dark disc or square under the map; its place and size follow mini_coin, mini_taille and cadre_marge.
  • « Boussole » or « Boussole en bas » (after boussole_position): Bande, the band; the letters, ticks and markers are drawn on it at its place and size. Change their look with screens_set (styles, not hand-made values); never draw them again with game.hud_*, and never edit the system. What follows the world at every step stays drawn by the script over them, with keys starting with carte. (no shape can carry it: the engine's picture of the map with a moving, turning part, the fog mask, the turned arrows and markers, the north, the theme frame, the scrolling graduations): do not use that prefix in a game script.
  • Testing: in game_run, read the report's hud: carte.mini.r.<n> are the minimap markers, carte.mini.o.<n> the rings of objectives, carte.mini.joueur the arrow; press carte and read Grande carte/Fenêtre, carte.g.n.<n> (the names on the big map) and carte.grande.image (its uv, the part of the map shown). A discovered place is announced in Lieux découverts/Annonce#1. wheel and mouse in a segment zoom and drag it, stick ([x, y], y down) moves it, actions carte_rapprocher or carte_eloigner held zoom it in or out. A discovery sends carte.decouvert, counted in the report's events.
  • After a saved game is loaded, the map reads the quests in progress from the quest log (shared.quetes_en_cours, version 11): the objective is back on the map, the compass and the quest list at once, for each player, guests included. A failed or abandoned quest (quetes.echec, quetes.abandon) loses its objective.

Fog of war (version 11)¶

  • brouillard (off by default): on the big map and the minimap, what the player has not approached yet stays covered in brouillard_couleur (dark by default). Walking uncovers everything within brouillard_rayon metres (25 by default) around the player. Places under the fog do not show (on the map, the minimap and the compass), except quest objectives and the player's own markers. Saved games keep it, for each player, in each scene. brouillard_groupe: what one player uncovers is uncovered for everyone; otherwise each has their own.
  • When to turn it on: exploration, survival, an adventure where discovering the world is part of the fun (Valheim, Enshrouded, Zelda style). Leave it off for a shooter, a race, a town or farm game, a small arena, or when the player must read the whole map at once.
  • The map area is cut into 32 × 32 squares: the edges are smooth curves, about one square wide; on a very large scene (more than about 1 km), the uncovered area grows by bigger steps. It costs one HUD element per map (an image @mask: the engine computes), whatever is uncovered: sent to guests like any image.
  • Testing: the report's hud has carte.mini.brouillard (minimap) and, map open, carte.g.brouillard (big map); their text is the mask.

Compass, the player's markers, legend and quest list (version 10)¶

  • Compass (game setting boussole, off by default; boussole_position haut or bas, in the middle): a band that scrolls as the camera turns, with N, NE, E… every 45°, degrees every 30° and ticks every 15°, a fixed mark in the middle. On it, at their heading: quest objectives (held at the band's edge when behind), the player's markers, and the places visible on the map within boussole_portee metres; the distance of the nearest objective is written under it. Style panneau.boussole for its background (the shape Bande). It hides during a pause and while the big map is open. Only what fits in the band is created (about 20 HUD elements).
  • Compass or minimap? A survival game in the manner of Rust (find your way by the sun and landmarks) sets boussole on and mini_carte off: the big map (M) stays for planning. An adventure or RPG game with towns, merchants and quests keeps the minimap, and may add the compass for the quest objective's direction (Valheim, WoW style). With both, put the minimap in a bottom corner (mini_coin) so the top stays clear.
  • The player's markers (reperes_joueur, on by default): on the big map, the control carte_repere (right click, or Y on a gamepad at the crosshair in the middle of the view) places "Marker 1", "Marker 2"… at that spot, or removes the one there; a left click without dragging on one removes it too. They show on the big map, the minimap and the compass, in couleur_reperes, at most reperes_max. Saved games keep them, for each player, in each scene. reperes_groupe: shared with all the players (anyone can remove one); otherwise each has their own.
  • Legend (legende, on by default): on the right of the big map, one button per kind of marker present (objectives, places, merchants, characters, players, my markers); a click hides or shows that kind on the big map and the minimap, kept for the player by saved games. Its rows (Grande carte/Sorte#<row>) have the style coche.carte, selected while shown.
  • Quest list (liste_quetes, on by default, only with the quetes system): on the left of the big map, the quests in progress; a click centers the map on its objective. It does not change the quest log.

Several players¶

One copy per player: each player has their own minimap, map window, discovered places, compass, legend choices and markers (unless reperes_groupe shares the markers).

The other players show by themselves (game setting joueurs: partout by default, on the minimap and the big map; grande, only on the big map; aucun, nowhere, for hide and seek): an arrow turned where they look, in a color of their player number (the same for everyone; a game script gives another one, such as a team's, with game.override("carte.couleur_joueur", function(default, joueur) ... end)), and their name (game.player_name: their character's name or nickname), on the guests' maps as on the host's. Do not place carte on the player's character for that: the markers would all carry the copied character's look. Placed there anyway, it chooses the look of the others' markers, which are named after each player (never after the object), and nobody sees one on themselves.
