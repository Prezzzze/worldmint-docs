Getting started¶

This page takes you from creating a project to a running game. Each step links to the page that details it.

1. Create a project¶

File > New project...: choose where to put it and its name ("My game" is suggested). A project is a folder that holds your whole game: its scenes, the models it uses, its settings. See Projects and scenes.

Right after, the Creation assistant offers two choices:

  • With the assistant: it asks you a few questions about your game (the story, the mood, days and nights going by, the places, the character, what one does, people to talk to, merchants, quests, a title screen...), one step at a time. Each choice tells the agent which system brings it: ticking "Buy and sell at merchants" asks it for the Shop. Next moves on, Skip skips a question, Back returns. At the end, it prepares Your request to the agent: read it, change it if you like, then Launch sends it to the agent, which first presents its plan and waits for your go-ahead before building. Save and close keeps your answers for later.
  • Start from scratch: you build yourself, or by talking directly to the agent.

The assistant reopens at any time with Game > Creation assistant...; your answers are kept in the project.

2. The window¶

  • At the top: the menus (File, Edit, Select, Object, View, Game, Agent, Help), a few buttons in the middle (gizmo axes, snapping, four views, undo, redo), and on the right the Preferences (gear) and the Play button.
  • On the left: three tabs. Scene lists what is in the scene, Game holds the game's controls and systems, Scripts its scripts.
  • In the middle: the 3D view, with its tool column on the left.
  • At the bottom: the Library, your models and sounds.
  • On the right: the Properties of what is selected, and the Agent panel. Each folds with its chevron; a handle between them shares the height.
  • At the very bottom, the status bar: Library opens or closes the library, the editor's last message (a click opens the Console), and on the right the agent's state, the time of the last automatic backup, the number of selected objects and the number of objects in the scene.

Each part has its page: see The editor.

3. Add models¶

  1. Add a folder of models to the library: Add a folder, then Browse or paste its path. You can also drag a folder from File Explorer onto the window.
  2. In the library, double-click a model to add it to the scene, or drag it into the 3D view, where you want it.
  3. For a simple shape: Object > Create (Cube, Sphere, Cylinder, Capsule, Plane), or right-click in the list of the Scene tab.

Double-clicked, the model arrives at the center of the view; dragged, it arrives where you drop it. The Place on ground when adding preference, on by default, then sets it on the ground. The model's file is copied into the project: your library never changes.

4. Place objects¶

  • Select: click the object, in the view or in the list. Ctrl + click adds to the selection; dragging in the view draws a rectangle.
  • Move, rotate, scale: keys W, E, R, or the tool column on the left of the view, then pull the handles (the gizmo). Or type the values in the Properties, Position and size section.
  • Place on ground: End key, or the Place on ground button in the properties.
  • Look around: hold the right mouse button to orbit, the middle button to pan, the wheel to zoom, F to frame the selection.

See The 3D view.

5. Choose the player¶

The game needs a character to play. Select a model (an animated character, preferably), then click Set as player in its Properties. Its properties then show its Movement, its Animations and the Player camera: see The properties.

6. Play¶

F5, or the Play button at the top right. The game opens in its own window; the editor stays open beside it. While the game runs, the player settings you change in the properties apply live. F5 or Stop closes the game.

In the game, the player moves with the game controls: by default W A S D on a QWERTY keyboard (Z Q S D on AZERTY), Left Shift to run, Space to jump, the mouse to turn the camera. Esc frees the mouse.

7. Undo¶

Everything can be undone, your changes and the agent's: Ctrl+Z undoes, Ctrl+Y redoes. The same commands are in the Edit menu and as arrows in the middle of the top bar. Under each answer of the agent, Undo these changes undoes everything it did during that turn.

8. Save¶

Ctrl+S saves the scene. Every 60 seconds, an automatic backup keeps a separate copy, without touching your file. If the editor closes before you saved, it offers to Recover your work on the next launch.

9. Ask the agent¶

In the Agent panel, describe what you want to build and press Enter. The agent works in the scene before your eyes. See The agent.
