Interface¶

L'allure des menus, des boutons, des listes et des champs de texte du jeu : les couleurs des fonds, du texte et de ce qui est sélectionné, la taille du texte, l'arrondi des coins. Tout ce que le jeu affiche les prend, sauf ce qu'un script choisit lui-même.
Identifiant interface
Version 1
Se pose sur des objets non
Géré par le moteur oui : le moteur le fait vivre lui-même, sans script

Interface (interface)¶

The look of everything the game shows on screen: panels, buttons, text fields, texts. The system has no script: the engine applies it to the HUD. Add it once with modules_add; it is for the whole game, not placed on objects.

  • Its settings (modules_set) are the default look of every HUD element: fond_panneaux (panel background), fond_boutons (button and field background), accent (hovered button, selected field, scroll bar), texte (text color) as #rrggbb colors; taille_texte (text height, a share of the screen height, 0.03 by default); arrondi (corner rounding, a share of the screen height, 0 for square corners); opacite (background opacity, 0 to 1).
  • A value a script gives (color, alpha, size, text_color) wins over the theme. To let the user restyle the whole interface from one place, do not give colors in scripts: use the theme.
  • When the user asks for a style ("dark and gold", "like a parchment", "bigger text"), change these settings instead of every script.
  • Without the system, the HUD keeps its former look (texts slightly bigger than buttons).
  • Build menus with game.hud_panel and parent (see scripts_reference): the engine places the elements, wraps texts, scrolls long lists, and announces hud.clic and hud.valider.
  • Check a menu in game_run: the report's hud gives each element's rect as the engine placed it, hidden for what is outside a scrolled panel's frame, and lines for wrapped texts. Then look at it with capture_steps.
