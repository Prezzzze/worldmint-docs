Besoins¶

Ce dont le joueur a besoin pour vivre : la faim, la soif, la fatigue, ou ce que le jeu veut. Chaque besoin baisse avec le temps ; à zéro, le joueur perd de la vie. Il se remonte en mangeant ou en buvant ce que porte l'inventaire, à une source du monde (un puits, un lit) qui porte ce système, ou par une règle du Déclencheur. Chaque besoin s'éteint sans perdre ses réglages.

Événements¶

Les descriptions viennent du code et sont en anglais, la langue de travail de l'agent.

Ce qu'il annonce¶

Événement Champs Description
besoins.change besoin (text), valeur (number), max (number) A need of the player (hunger, thirst, tiredness...) changed by a whole point, or is announced once when the game starts. besoin is its name as written in the needs list (original language).
besoins.bas besoin (text), valeur (number), max (number) A need went below the warning level of the besoins system (its bar blinks): a sound, a message, a quest hint.
besoins.vide besoin (text) A need reached zero: from now on the player loses health each second (and maybe walks slower or cannot run), until it goes back up.
besoins.consomme objet (text) The player ate or drank one item of the inventory (its foods list): it has been taken from the inventory and its needs raised.
besoins.source cible (id), besoin (text), montant (number) The player used a source of the world carrying the besoins system (a well, a river, a bed): besoin went up by montant.
sante.blesser cible (id), montant (number), source (id?) Ask the health module to remove health from an object.
sante.soigner cible (id), montant (number) Ask the health module to give health back, up to its maximum.
inventaire.retirer objet (text), quantite (number) Ask the inventory to remove items, such as a payment. Answered by inventaire.change, or inventaire.manque if there are not enough.
jour_nuit.avancer heures (number) Ask the day and night system to move the clock on by heures (a night in a bed: 8). Every hour, sunrise and sunset crossed are announced in order.

Ce qu'il écoute¶

Événement Champs Description
besoins.remplir besoin (text), montant (number) Ask the besoins system to raise a need by its name, without using anything up: a meal at an inn, a Trigger rule, a script.
besoins.consommer objet (text) Ask the besoins system to eat or drink one item of the inventory, as a click on its slot would: nothing happens if it is not a food or the player has none.
inventaire.change objet (text), quantite (number), total (number), chargement (boolean?) The quantity of an item changed. total is the new quantity. chargement is true when a saved game gives the items back (game.load): nothing was picked up.
inventaire.choisi objet (text), total (number) The player clicked a slot of the inventory window: objet is the kind of item, total how many they carry. What it does belongs to the game: eat, equip, read.
interaction.utiliser cible (id), joueur (id) The player used the object in reach: a door, a merchant, an item to pick up. Whoever owns that object decides what it does.
sante.change cible (id), vie (number), max (number) An object's health changed, or is announced once when the game starts.
langue.change langue (text) Sent by the engine when the player changes the game's language (game.set_language, the Menu options). A module redraws what it already shows: its texts go through game.t and game.language.

Points qu'un script peut remplacer (game.override)¶

Les descriptions viennent du code et sont en anglais, la langue de travail de l'agent.

  • besoins.baisse : function(default, besoin, par_minute) -> how much this need goes down per minute right now (number). The default is its baisse setting, plus its course setting while the player runs. To make thirst fall faster in the desert or at noon, hunger slower while sleeping.

Needs (besoins)¶

Hunger, thirst, tiredness, or any need the game wants: each one goes down with the time of play, and at zero the player loses health. Add it once with modules_add (it needs sante: put sante on the player, and barre_de_vie to show the health). Use it whenever the user talks about eating, drinking, starving, sleeping or survival: never write a hunger script.

  • The needs (game setting besoins, modules_set): a list, one row per need, in the user's language. Hunger and thirst by default; add « Fatigue » or anything else, remove what the user did not ask for. Each row: nom (shown next to its bar, and how foods and sources name it), actif (true by default; false switches the need off without losing its settings: no bar, it does not fall, never hurts, and its foods and sources do nothing, saying so; a row without it is on), max (the full reserve, the value at the start), baisse (lost per minute of play), course (lost on top per minute of running), degats (health lost per second at zero; 0: it never kills), vide (also at zero: rien, lent (the player walks at lenteur of their speed), sans_course (no running nor jumping: tiredness)), couleur (its bar).
  • Tiredness (« Fatigue ») is a row of its own, like hunger: add it only when the user wants sleep to matter (baisse low or 0, course for running, vide sans_course, degats 0 so it never kills), and set actif false when they want to try the game without it, rather than deleting the row. Ask, or offer, how energy comes back; any mix works: sleep in a bed (a source: source_besoin « Fatigue », source_verbe « Dormir », source_heures 8 with jour_nuit), a food or drink (an aliments row: « Café », besoin « Fatigue »), or anything else through a declencheur rule with the action remplir_besoin (besoin « Fatigue », montant): resting by a campfire (a zone around it, quand entree, fois chaque), sitting on a bench (quand utilise), a prayer at an altar. Never a script for that.
  • Foods (game setting aliments): which inventory items raise which need, by how much (objet, besoin written as the need's nom, montant), and vie (health given too). One row per need an item raises: a watermelon feeds and quenches. The player eats with a click on its slot in the inventory window (I), as long as manger_fenetre is on; rien_si_plein keeps a food when its needs are full. The items themselves come from inventaire (picked up), collecte (gathered: berries from a bush), fabrication (cooked at a campfire) or boutique.
  • Sources in the world: place besoins on an object (systems_attach; interaction comes with it) and set source_besoin (the need's nom), source_montant, source_verbe (the prompt: « Boire », « Dormir »), source_recharge (seconds before it works again), source_heures (hours that pass, with jour_nuit: 8 for a bed). A well, a river bank, a bed, a spring.
  • Dosage: with baisse 2 per minute and max 100, hunger reaches zero after 50 minutes of play. Say the numbers to the user and check them with game_run (a long segment doing nothing): the player must have time to find food, and doing nothing must end badly if the user asked for a survival game.
  • Game settings: alerte (% under which the bar blinks and besoins.bas is sent), soin_rassasie and seuil_rassasie (health regained per second while every need is above that %), afficher and the bars' place (x, y, largeur, hauteur, ecart: they line up under the health bar by default).
  • Events announced: besoins.change (besoin, valeur, max), besoins.bas, besoins.vide (besoin), besoins.consomme (objet), besoins.source (cible, besoin, montant). Send besoins.remplir (besoin, montant) to raise a need without using anything (a meal at an inn; from a declencheur rule, use its action remplir_besoin, which sends it with these fields), besoins.consommer (objet) to eat an item from a script or a declencheur rule.
  • Change how fast a need falls from your own script, never the module's: game.override("besoins.baisse", function(default, besoin, par_minute) if besoin == "Soif" and game.has_tag(game.ground(game.player()) or "", "desert") then return par_minute * 2 end return default(besoin, par_minute) end).
  • Saved games: game.save keeps every need and the sources' waiting time; game.load gives them back. Nothing to write.
  • HUD keys start with besoins.: do not use that prefix in a game script. The bars are numbered among the needs that are on: besoins.1 is the first bar shown.
  • Testing: in game_run, read the report's hud (besoins.1, besoins.1.nom...) and the besoins.* events; a script listening to sante.change shows the health lost at zero.

Several players¶

One copy per player: each player has their own needs, bars, food and penalties; a source (a well, a bed) refills the player who uses it. A bed that moves the clock on (jour_nuit.avancer) moves it on for everyone: the day is shared.
