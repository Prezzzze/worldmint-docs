Commerces¶

Les commerces de la ville deviennent des boutiques : un vendeur, pris parmi vos personnages selon la sorte, se tient derrière le comptoir de chaque commerce visitable proche du joueur, et vend les articles de sa sorte par la Boutique. Sans personnage réglé, pas de vendeur.
Identifiant commerces
Version 1
Se pose sur des objets non
Active aussi Boutique

Événements¶

Les descriptions viennent du code et sont en anglais, la langue de travail de l'agent.

Ce qu'il annonce¶

Événement Champs Description
commerce.achat lieu (id), vendeur (id), objet (text), quantite (number), prix (number), monnaie (text), joueur (id?) The player bought an item from the clerk of a shop of the city (the Commerces system): lieu is the shop's place, vendeur the clerk, quantite how many they received, prix what they paid, in monnaie. Follows boutique.achat; the inventory and the shop's stock have already changed.
boutique.ouvrir cible (id), onglet (text?), joueur (id?) Ask the shop system to open the shop of cible, as if the player had used it: from a dialogue answer, a trigger, a script. onglet opens it on another module's tab (equipement.atelier: the services). It opens for joueur only; without joueur, for the host only (never for every player).
boutique.acheter cible (id), objet (text), joueur (id?) Ask the shop system to buy objet at the merchant cible for the player, without the window, exactly as the Buy button (money taken, item given, stock counted): the Commerces system's shared.commerces.acheter sends it. Refused while another merchant's window is open. boutique.achat follows when it is done. For joueur only; without joueur, for the host only.

Ce qu'il écoute¶

Événement Champs Description
boutique.achat cible (id), objet (text), quantite (number), prix (number), monnaie (text) The player bought an item in a shop: cible is the merchant, quantite how many they received, prix what they paid, in monnaie (a kind of inventory item). The inventory has already changed.

Points qu'un script peut remplacer (game.override)¶

Les descriptions viennent du code et sont en anglais, la langue de travail de l'agent.

  • commerces.vendeur : function(default, infos) -> the clerk of a shop: {personnage = the hidden scene object copied (a character), animation = a clip of that character (empty: its first idle clip)}, or nil for no clerk. infos: lieu (the shop's place), donnees (game.data of the place: sorte, nom, batiment, vendeur...). The default takes the rows of the setting vendeurs for this shop (commerce: its name, building or place) or else for its kind, one of them chosen by the place.
  • commerces.articles : function(default, infos) -> the list of what the shop sells, rows of the Shop ({objet, quantite, prix, stock, rachat}). infos: lieu, donnees. The default: the rows of the setting articles for this shop if it has some, else those of its kind.
  • commerces.ouvert : function(default, infos) -> true if the shop has its clerk now (infos: lieu, donnees). The default is true: override it to follow the hours (donnees.horaires) with a day clock of your game, or to close a shop after a robbery.

Shops of the city (commerces)¶

The shops of a generated city become merchants without code: near the player, each visitable shop gets a clerk standing behind its counter (the place its interior recipe left for them, facing the room), who sells the items of its kind through the Shop (boutique). Activating it activates boutique (and so interaction and inventaire). Use it for every "the grocery sells sodas", "a clerk in each café"; never write a script that clones clerks or a shop window yourself, and never edit the system: set it, listen to its events, override its hooks.

Set it with modules_set (game-wide settings; nothing is placed on objects):

  • vendeurs: who keeps each kind, one row each: sorte (the kind as the city names it: superette, cafe, banque, vetements, pharmacie… city_get lists the shops and their kinds), commerce (empty: the whole kind; a shop's name, building id or place id: that shop only, instead of its kind's rows), personnage (a character of the scene, usually hidden: it is copied, with its animations), animation (a clip of that character, looped; empty or missing: its first clip whose name holds "idle", else its usual animation; never invent a pose). Several rows for a kind: each shop takes one of them, always the same. No row, or a character missing from the scene: no clerk, without error. Never pick a model of a pack by default: ask the user which characters keep the shops.
  • articles: what each kind sells, in the window's order: sorte, commerce (a named shop's rows replace its kind's rows for it), objet (the inventory kind, in the user's language), quantite, prix, stock (per shop; 0: no limit).
  • monnaie (the inventory kind that pays, "Or" by default: set the game's money, such as "Dollar"), texte (the prompt near the clerk, "Acheter"), distance (m, 40: clerks appear closer than this to the player and go back to the pool beyond it; lower it if the game slows down).

Example: {"vendeurs": [{"sorte": "superette", "personnage": "Vendeuse"}], "articles": [{"sorte": "superette", "objet": "Soda", "prix": 2, "stock": 12}, {"sorte": "superette", "objet": "Sandwich", "prix": 5}], "monnaie": "Dollar"}.

How it plays: the player walks in (commerce.entree), the clerk faces them; using the clerk (E) opens the Shop window titled with the shop's name. Buying is the Shop's (money taken, item given, clear messages); the stock belongs to the shop, not to the clerk: it is kept when the clerk leaves and comes back, and by the saved game.

Ports for scripts (shared.commerces, from any game script):

  • shared.commerces.articles(lieu) -> the list of what the shop sells (objet, quantite, prix, stock, and reste when the stock is limited).
  • shared.commerces.vendeur(lieu) -> its clerk now (tagged commerce.vendeur), or nil (far away, closed, no character set); shared.commerces.lieu(clerk) -> the shop of a clerk.
  • shared.commerces.ouvrir(lieu) opens its shop window; shared.commerces.acheter(lieu, objet) buys one purchase of objet without the window (the Shop's own purchase, through the event boutique.acheter). Both need the clerk there (false otherwise).
  • Event commerce.achat {lieu, vendeur, objet, quantite, prix, monnaie, joueur}: after a purchase from a clerk; a quest step, a declencheur rule, a sound. boutique.achat still comes first, with the clerk as cible.
  • The place of a shop: game.near(game.player(), 2000, "commerce:superette", {asleep = true})[1]; game.data(lieu) has sorte, nom, porte, vendeur {position, regard} and arrets (customer stops).

Hooks (game.override from a game script, never an edit of the system):

  • commerces.vendeur function(default, infos) -> {personnage, animation} or nil (infos.lieu, infos.donnees): a special clerk for one shop, none at night.
  • commerces.articles function(default, infos) -> a list of Shop rows: prices that change with the story, a sold-out shop.
  • commerces.ouvert function(default, infos) -> false to have no clerk now (after a robbery; or by infos.donnees.horaires with the game's own clock). The default is always open.

Cost: one animated character per visitable shop within distance (2 to 10 at once in a dense street), copied from the pool. Testing: in game_run, give money (inventaire.ajouter), walk into the shop, press interact near the clerk, click Boutique/Acheter#1; or call shared.commerces.acheter from the test script and read commerce.achat in the logs.

Several players¶

One copy, on the host: the clerks are the same for everyone. Each player buys through their own Shop window and inventory; a shop's limited stock is shared.
