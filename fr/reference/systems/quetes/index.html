Journal de quêtes¶

Les quêtes du jeu et leurs étapes, qui avancent toutes seules : sur un événement (une règle du Déclencheur, une réponse d'un Dialogue, un ennemi vaincu), une marque du Dialogue, des objets portés ou des objets remis. Une quête finie peut donner une récompense ; une quête peut aussi échouer (un événement, une marque, un temps écoulé) ou être abandonnée depuis le journal, puis être reprise. Un journal s'ouvre avec J, et une notification annonce chaque nouvelle quête, chaque étape et chaque fin.

Événements¶

Les descriptions viennent du code et sont en anglais, la langue de travail de l'agent.

Ce qu'il annonce¶

Événement Champs Description
quetes.debut quete (text) A quest started: quete is its title.
quetes.etape quete (text), etape (number), texte (text) A step of a quest is done: etape is its rank in the quest (from 1), texte what it said. Sent before quetes.fin when it was the last one.
quetes.fin quete (text) A quest is finished: all its steps are done. Its reward, if any, has been asked to the inventory.
quetes.echec quete (text) A quest failed: an event, a mark, its time ran out, or quetes.echouer. It gives no reward; it may be given again if its settings allow it.
quetes.abandon quete (text) The player abandoned a quest from the log (or quetes.abandonner asked for it). It gives no reward; it may be given again if its settings allow it.
inventaire.ajouter objet (text), quantite (number), joueur (id?) Ask the inventory to add items, such as a reward. Answered by inventaire.change, or inventaire.plein. With several players, joueur says whose bag; without it, the player whose action is being handled, or every player.
inventaire.retirer objet (text), quantite (number) Ask the inventory to remove items, such as a payment. Answered by inventaire.change, or inventaire.manque if there are not enough.
dialogue.marquer marque (text), retirer (boolean?) Ask the dialogue system to set a mark, from a script or another system; with retirer true, to remove it. Dialogue conditions then see it.

Ce qu'il écoute¶

Événement Champs Description
quetes.demarrer quete (text) Ask the quest log to give a quest to the player, by its title: from a script, a dialogue or a trigger. Nothing happens if it was already given.
quetes.echouer quete (text) Ask the quest log to make a quest in progress fail, by its title: from a script or a trigger. Nothing happens if it is not in progress.
quetes.abandonner quete (text) Ask the quest log to abandon a quest in progress, by its title, as the player does from the log. Nothing happens if it is not in progress.
inventaire.change objet (text), quantite (number), total (number), chargement (boolean?) The quantity of an item changed. total is the new quantity. chargement is true when a saved game gives the items back (game.load): nothing was picked up.
dialogue.marque marque (text) A dialogue set a mark: a word the game remembers (a line shown, an answer chosen). Every dialogue's conditions can use it.
hud.clic cible (text), joueur (id?) A button of the HUD was clicked: pressed, then released on it; or chosen with A (Enter) while it has the gamepad or keyboard focus; or the window's back button (back = true), with B (Escape). cible is the button's key (game.hud_button), joueur the player who clicked (a guest clicks on their own computer; the host plays the click). Announced by the engine, before the scripts' on_update of that step.

Points qu'un script peut remplacer (game.override)¶

Les descriptions viennent du code et sont en anglais, la langue de travail de l'agent.

  • quetes.texte : function(default, key) -> the text the quest log shows for that key: titre, aucune, en_cours, terminees, fermer, fait, nouvelle, etape, finie, recompense, echouee, abandonnee, ratees, echouee_court, abandonnee_court, abandonner, confirmer, reste, manque. It may hold {quete}, {etape}, {objet}, {n} and {total}, replaced afterwards (in the player's language). The default is the game's translation of the text in the original language (translations_get lists them as built-in texts of the quest log); without one, French or English, after the player's language.

Quest log (quetes)¶

The game's quests and their steps, written in its settings and moved on by events, without code. A notification announces each new quest, step done and quest finished; the current step shows on the right of the screen; a log window opens with the control journal (J, or → on a gamepad). Add it once with modules_add; it is for the whole game, not placed on objects. Use it whenever the user talks about quests, missions, objectives or tasks to do: do not write a quest script.

Its game setting quetes (modules_set) is a list of rows, in order. A quete row starts a quest; the etape rows under it are its steps.

  • quete: titre (shown, and names the quest in events), description, depart (when the player gets it):
  • debut: when the game starts;
  • evenement: on the event depart_evenement, such as an answer of a dialogue with annonce: "loups.accepte", or a declencheur rule "send an event";
  • marque: when the Dialogue mark depart_marque is set (at once if it already is);
  • quete: when the quest titled depart_quete ends: a chain of quests. Also dans_l_ordre (on: one step after the other, the next ones hidden in the log; off: all at once), recompense and recompense_quantite (items the inventory receives at the end; needs inventaire). Failing, echec (what makes the quest in progress fail; no reward then):
  • aucun: never (the default);
  • evenement: the event echec_evenement happens while the quest is in progress, about an object named echec_cible if given (sante.mort with "Marchand": the merchant to escort died);
  • marque: the Dialogue mark echec_marque is set (at once if it already is);
  • temps: echec_delai seconds of play pass after its start (time waits during a pause; the tracker and the log show what is left, such as "Reste 0:42"). abandon (on by default: the log shows an Abandonner button under it, pressed twice), reprendre (on by default: what gives the quest gives it again, from the start, after it failed or was abandoned; a quest that starts with the game is only given again by « quetes.demarrer »), marque_echec and annonce_echec (a mark and an event when it fails or is abandoned: the miller complains).
  • etape: texte (what to do, as the log writes it), and fin (what ends it):
  • evenement: the event fin_evenement happens fin_fois times while the step is active; fin_cible keeps only events about an object of that name ("Loup" counts "Loup 1", "Loup 2"). For example sante.mort with fin_cible "Loup" and fin_fois 5 (five wolves defeated), dialogue.fin with fin_cible "Meunier" (a talk with the miller finished), zone.entree with the zone's name (a place reached), boutique.achat, collecte.recolte, or an event the game names itself;
  • marque: the Dialogue mark fin_marque is set (at once if it already is);
  • objet: the player carries fin_quantite of fin_objet at the same time (from the inventory; done at once if they already do);
  • remettre: on the event fin_evenement (for fin_cible if given, such as dialogue.fin with "Meunier"), if the player carries fin_quantite of fin_objet, the inventory takes them back and the step is done; otherwise an announcement says how many are missing, and the step waits for the next time. Use it for "Bring 3 pelts to the miller".
  • On both: marque (a mark set when it ends, which every dialogue's tests and answers see) and annonce (an event sent when it ends, for a declencheur rule: a bridge appears, a door opens).

A quest is finished when all its steps are done. Write every title and step in the user's language, and keep steps short.

A miller who asks to hunt wolves, then thanks the player:

  1. Miller's dialogue: an answer "I'll do it" with annonce: "loups.accepte", marque: "loups_acceptes"; a test on the mark loups_chasses leads to a thank-you line with an answer "Here they are" whose annonce is loups.rendus.
  2. quetes: {"sorte": "quete", "titre": "Les loups du moulin", "depart": "evenement", "depart_evenement": "loups.accepte", "recompense": "Or", "recompense_quantite": 50}, then {"sorte": "etape", "texte": "Vaincre 3 loups", "fin": "evenement", "fin_evenement": "sante.mort", "fin_cible": "Loup", "fin_fois": 3, "marque": "loups_chasses"}, then {"sorte": "etape", "texte": "Revenir voir le meunier", "fin": "evenement", "fin_evenement": "loups.rendus"}.

Other details:

  • Events announced: quetes.debut (quete: its title), quetes.etape (quete, etape: its rank, texte), quetes.fin (quete), quetes.echec (quete) and quetes.abandon (quete). A declencheur rule or a script can react to them; for one particular quest, prefer its annonce or annonce_echec.
  • Give a quest from a script: game.emit("quetes.demarrer", {quete = "Les loups du moulin"}); make it fail or abandon it: game.emit("quetes.echouer", {quete = ...}), game.emit("quetes.abandonner", {quete = ...}). A step can also end on an event your script sends.
  • Game settings: fenetre (the log with J), pause_fenetre (the world stops while it is open), notifications and duree_notification (seconds), suivi (the current step on the right of the screen, with its progress such as 2/5). Notifications and the step on screen hide during a pause (a menu, a dialogue, a shop).
  • Texts follow the player's language. The log's own texts (Quest log, New quest...) are built-in texts of the system, listed by translations_get with the game's texts: translate them there for every language the game offers (French and English are built in). Quest titles, descriptions, step texts and rewards are translated with the game's tables too; events keep the original title, so depart_quete and quetes.demarrer name a quest by its original title. To change a word, override quetes.texte from a game script: game.override("quetes.texte", function(default, key) if key == "titre" then return "Missions" end return default(key) end). Keys and their {quete}, {etape}, {objet}, {n} placeholders are in modules_info.
  • Saved games: game.save keeps every quest's state (failed and abandoned ones included), its steps done, its counts (2 wolves out of 3), the time left to a timed quest and the marks; game.load gives them back, without announcing the quests again. Nothing to write.
  • Gamepad and keyboard: B or Escape closes the log (quetes.fermer is its back button), like A or Enter on Close. The Abandonner buttons (quetes.abandonner.<n>, n the quest's rank among the quest rows) are reached like any other button.
  • HUD keys start with quetes.: do not use that prefix in a game script.
  • Testing: in game_run, do what each step asks, then press journal and read the report's hud (quetes.l.<n>, quetes.suivi.titre, quetes.suivi.reste); game.log in a script listening to quetes.etape shows the order.

Several players¶

One copy per player: each player has their own quests, steps, journal and rewards (into their own bag).
