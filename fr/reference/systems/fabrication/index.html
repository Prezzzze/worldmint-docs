Fabrication¶

Des recettes qui changent ce que porte le joueur en autre chose : trois bois et deux pierres font une hache. On fabrique à mains nues (C) ou à une station : un objet du monde, un modèle importé compris, à qui l'on donne une fonction que le jeu définit (Établi, Feu de camp, Four, Forge...), avec ses niveaux à améliorer, son combustible et sa file d'attente.
Identifiant fabrication
Version 12
Se pose sur des objets oui
Active aussi Inventaire, Interaction
Demande aussi, sur le même objet Interaction

Événements¶

Les descriptions viennent du code et sont en anglais, la langue de travail de l'agent.

Ce qu'il annonce¶

Événement Champs Description
fabrication.fabrique objet (text), quantite (number), etabli (text), cible (text?), xp (number?), piste (text?) The player crafted a recipe: its ingredients left the inventory and objet (quantite of it) came in, at once or at the end of its time in the queue. etabli is the kind of station where it was made (empty bare-handed), cible the station object (missing bare-handed).
fabrication.manque objet (text), manque (text?), etabli (text?), niveau (number?), combustible (text?) A recipe could not be crafted: manque lists what is missing, in the player's language ("2 Wood, 1 Stone"), or etabli names the stations it needs (with niveau, the minimum level, when the station is too low), or combustible the fuel a station out of fire needs.
fabrication.commencee objet (text), quantite (number), temps (number), etabli (text), cible (text?) A recipe with a time joined the player's queue: its ingredients left the inventory; objet (quantite of it) comes in after temps seconds. etabli is the kind of station, cible the station object (missing bare-handed).
fabrication.finie objet (text), quantite (number), etabli (text), cible (text?) A recipe of the queue is done: objet (quantite of it) came into the inventory. fabrication.fabrique is announced too.
fabrication.station_amelioree cible (text), etabli (text), niveau (number), nom (text), ancienne (text?) A station (cible, of the kind etabli) was upgraded to the level niveau, named nom (empty if the level has no name); its cost left the inventory. With a model for that level, cible is the new copy, and ancienne the object it replaced.
fabrication.allumee cible (text), etabli (text) A station that burns fuel (cible, of the kind etabli) starts burning. Its particles effect (the particules system) is switched on with it.
fabrication.combustible_vide cible (text), etabli (text), combustible (text) A station (cible, of the kind etabli) burnt its last fuel (combustible): it stops crafting until fuel is added, and its particles effect is switched off.
fabrication.onglet joueur (id), onglet (text), cible (id?), etabli (text?), parent (text?), largeur (number?) The crafting window opened another module's tab at a station (onglet, its key; parent, the empty panel to fill; largeur, the window's width), or left it (onglet empty). The module whose tab it is fills the panel.
inventaire.ajouter objet (text), quantite (number), joueur (id?), fiche (number?) Ask the inventory to add items, such as a reward. Answered by inventaire.change, or inventaire.plein. With several players, joueur says whose bag; without it, the player whose action is being handled, or every player.
inventaire.retirer objet (text), quantite (number), fiche (number?) Ask the inventory to remove items, such as a payment. Answered by inventaire.change, or inventaire.manque if there are not enough. For a kind with unique copies (version 15): fiche removes that copy, otherwise the first ones; their records are destroyed.

Ce qu'il écoute¶

Événement Champs Description
fabrication.ouvrir etabli (text?), cible (text?), onglet (text?), joueur (id?) Ask the crafting system to open its window, with the recipes of the station object cible, or of the kind of station named etabli (empty or missing: bare-handed): a Trigger rule, a button of a drawn screen. onglet opens it on another module's tab (equipement.atelier: the workshop). It opens for joueur only; without joueur, for the host only (never for every player).
fabrication.fabriquer objet (text), etabli (text?), cible (text?) Ask the crafting system to craft the recipe that gives objet, as its button would, at the station object cible, or at the kind of station named etabli (the nearest one of that kind within reach, else as if at a level 1 station), empty or missing: bare-handed. Refused, with fabrication.manque, if something is missing. A recipe with a time joins the queue.
fabrication.ameliorer cible (text) Ask the crafting system to upgrade the station cible to its next level, as its Upgrade button would: the player pays the cost. Refused, with a message in the window, if something is missing.
fabrication.alimenter cible (text), quantite (number?) Ask the crafting system to put quantite (1 if missing) of the fuel of the station cible into it, taken from the player's inventory, as its fuel button would.
interaction.utiliser cible (id), joueur (id) The player used the object in reach: a door, a merchant, an item to pick up. Whoever owns that object decides what it does.
inventaire.change objet (text), quantite (number), total (number), chargement (boolean?), fiche (number?), jete (boolean?) The quantity of an item changed. total is the new quantity. chargement is true when a saved game gives the items back (game.load): nothing was picked up. For a unique copy (version 15), objet is its kind, fiche its record's number, total how many of that kind. With several stacks of a kind (version 21), total counts all of them; jete is true when a slot was dropped on the ground.
inventaire.plein objet (text), fiche (number?), reste (number?) An item could not be added: every slot holds another kind of item (or another unique copy), or (version 21) every stack of its kind is full. reste: picked up in part, how many stayed on the ground.
inventaire.manque objet (text), quantite (number), fiche (number?) Items could not be removed: there are not enough.

Points qu'un script peut remplacer (game.override)¶

Les descriptions viennent du code et sont en anglais, la langue de travail de l'agent.

  • fabrication.onglets : function(default, infos) -> a list of {cle, libelle}: the tabs other modules add to this station's window (infos: cible, the station; etabli, its kind; joueur). The default is an empty list: the window shows its recipes alone. With a tab, the window shows a row of tabs (the recipes first, labelled by the text onglet); the open tab of another module is the shape "Fabrication/Onglet ouvert" of the screen "Fabrication", which the module fills when fabrication.onglet announces it (its parent field). A station without recipes, fuel or level to gain opens on the first other tab. Equipment adds « Atelier » (repair, upgrade, set gems) at the stations its settings name. Call default(infos) and add yours to its list.
  • fabrication.permise : function(default, recette, infos) -> true, or false and the reason shown (text): whether this recipe can be crafted now. recette is its title (the product's original name). infos (version 3) is a table: objet, quantite, etabli (the stations it asks for, as written), niveau (the minimum station level), temps (seconds), connaissance (the knowledge it asks for, as written; empty: none). The default is true. To unlock a recipe with a quest, a book read, a level, a knowledge learnt (the connaissance field). Call default(recette, infos) for the other cases.
  • fabrication.texte : function(default, key) -> the text the crafting window shows for that key: titre, titre_etabli, aucune, fabriquer, fabriquer_n, tout, autres, categorie, recherche, temps, ingredient, garde, plus, moins, tout_max, choisir, introuvable, fermer, fabrique, manque, place, il_faut, il_faut_niveau, refus, niveau, feu, eteint, alimenter, ameliorer, ameliore, max, en_cours, attente_feu, trop_loin, annuler, commence, sans_combustible, disparue, onglet (the label of the recipes tab, shown when another module adds a tab). It may hold {objet}, {n}, {etabli}, {liste}, {secondes}, {combustible}, {nom}, {part}, {a} (how many the player has) and {raison}, replaced afterwards (in the player's language). The default is the game's translation of the text in the original language (translations_get lists them as built-in texts of the crafting window); without one, French or English, after the player's language.

Ce que l'agent sait de ce système¶

Le texte que l'agent lit avant de se servir de ce système (en anglais, la langue de travail de l'agent).

Crafting (fabrication)¶

Recipes that turn items of the inventory into another item: three wood and two stones make an axe; raw meat cooked at a campfire. Add it once with modules_add (with inventaire and interaction). Use it whenever the user talks about crafting, making, cooking, smelting, forging, a workbench, an oven, a forge or a kitchen: never write a crafting script. Version 3 (ADR-035): stations defined by the game, with levels, fuel and a queue. Version 4: a station's window shows only its own recipes. Version 8: a detailed window (disposition_fenetre), with categories, slots and a details panel.

It has two game settings (modules_set), each a list of rows, in order, and two values per object.

The kinds of stations (stations)¶

A station row starts a kind of station; the niveau rows under it are its levels, in order; the cout rows under a level are what upgrading to that level costs. Nothing is hard-coded: the default rows (Établi with 3 levels, Feu de camp burning wood, Four, Forge) are examples the user changes or deletes. Any game logic fits: Valheim (workbench radius, levels), Rust (furnace with fuel), Enshrouded, ARK...

The default survival progression (the rows of a new game, matching the default knowledges of savoir): bare-handed, a stone axe (« Hache »: 3 Bois, 2 Pierre); at the Établi level 1, « Pioche » and « Lance »; at level 2 (10 Bois, 4 Pierre), « Arc », « Hache de fer » and « Pioche de fer » (2 Bois, 3 Fer); level 3 costs 20 Bois and 5 Fer. The Feu de camp (burns Bois, 30 s a log) cooks « Viande cuite », « Poisson grillé », « Baies grillées »; the Four (burns Bois only while cooking) also does « Pain » and « Ragoût »; the Forge smelts « Fer » from 2 « Minerai de fer ». A campfire is a cooking station, not a workbench: never put tools on it. Rename everything in the user's words, and drop what their game does not have.

  • station: nom (the name recipes ask for, in the user's language: « Établi », « Forge », « Table d'alchimie »), usage (interaction: the player stands at it and presses E, or C right next to it; rayon: its recipes are allowed anywhere within rayon metres, in the window C), file (on: a recipe with a time joins a queue; off: everything is instant there), combustible (the item it burns, an inventory kind; empty: no fuel), duree_combustible (seconds of fire per unit), allumage (toujours: burns while fuel remains, a campfire; fabrication: burns only while crafting, an oven).
  • niveau: nom_niveau (« Établi simple »), modele (optional: a hidden object of the scene carrying fabrication, used as a mould: at this level a copy replaces the station, same place and turn, keeping its level and fire). The first level is the starting one; its cost is not used. A station without niveau rows has one level.
  • cout: objet, nombre: the price of the level above.
  • Example, a forge that burns coal and has two levels: {"sorte": "station", "nom": "Forge", "usage": "interaction", "combustible": "Charbon", "duree_combustible": 90, "allumage": "fabrication"}, {"sorte": "niveau", "nom_niveau": "Forge en pierre"}, {"sorte": "niveau", "nom_niveau": "Forge en fer"}, {"sorte": "cout", "objet": "Fer", "nombre": 10}.

The recipes (recettes)¶

A recette row starts a recipe; the ingredient rows under it are what it needs.

  • recette: objet (the item it gives, an inventory kind, in the user's language; it also names the recipe), categorie (version 8: its group in the detailed window, « Outils », « Nourriture »; empty: « Autres »), quantite, etabli (the kinds of stations where it is made, by their nom, several separated by commas: « Feu de camp, Four »; empty: bare-handed, anywhere), niveau_min (minimum station level), temps (seconds; 0: at once), connaissance (a knowledge name the player must have learnt; this module does not check it: a knowledge system or a game script does, through fabrication.permise; empty: none).
  • ingredient: ingredient (an inventory kind), nombre, garde (on: the player must have it but keeps it, a tool such as a hammer).
  • Cooking is only stations: a campfire, an oven, a kitchen are kinds of stations; any food is a recipe: {"sorte": "recette", "objet": "Poisson grillé", "etabli": "Feu de camp, Four", "temps": 5}, {"sorte": "ingredient", "ingredient": "Poisson cru", "nombre": 1}. Bread and stew at the oven only.
  • A recipe at a level too low shows in grey with « Il faut : Établi niveau 2 »; a recipe of another station does not show.

Giving an object a station function (values per object)¶

Attach the system to the object (systems_attach; interaction comes with it) and set nom_etabli, shown as « Fonction de cet objet »: one of the station names (a list in the inspector). Any object works, an imported model included: an imported oven model with nom_etabli « Four » is an oven. Empty: its name without trailing number (a « Feu de camp 3 » is a Feu de camp). niveau: its starting level. combustible_depart: units of fuel at the start (0: out).

  • A station with fuel crafts only while it has fire; the window shows « Feu : 45 s (Bois) » or « Éteint » and a button « Ajouter : Bois » that takes one unit from the inventory. The queue stops when the fire dies and resumes when fed. If the object carries particules (flames, smoke), its effect is switched on and off with the fire.
  • Upgrade: the window of a station with a next level has « Améliorer (10 Bois, 4 Pierre) »; it takes the cost, raises the level (kept per object, in the save, the same for all players).
  • A piece the player builds with construction keeps its model's systems: an oven model carrying fabrication becomes an oven wherever it is built.
  • A kind of station used by an object but missing from stations behaves like version 2: one level, no fuel, used with E.

The window and the queue¶

  • The control craft (C, or the left stick pressed) opens the window with the bare-handed recipes, plus those of the nearest station in reach (its radius, or the hand's reach); using a station (E) opens it with only that station's recipes (version 4: the campfire does not show the axe; the bare-handed ones stay in the window C), unless mains_nues_station is ticked (off by default; a game made before version 4 keeps it on, as it was). Close with C again or Close (B / Escape). montrer_impossibles shows what cannot be made yet, in grey. pause_fenetre (off by default) stops the world while it is open, and the queue with it.
  • A recipe with a time takes its ingredients at once and joins the player's queue, shown in the window with its progress and a Cancel button (the ingredients come back); window closed, the current one shows at the right of the screen. The item reaches the inventory at the end; if it does not fit, it waits for room. rester_pres on: the queue waits while the player is away from the station.
  • Two looks (version 8, disposition_fenetre): liste (default, and kept by older games): one row per recipe with its Craft button. detaillee: the wider window of survival games (Rust, Valheim, Enshrouded): at the top the station, its level, its fire (Add) and Upgrade, and a Search field; on the left the categories (« Tout » first, each with how many of its recipes can be made now; a recipe without categorie goes in « Autres », last; one category only: no column); in the middle the items as slots (the icon of the item kind of the inventory if it has one, else its name; impossible ones in grey); on the right the chosen recipe: its name, the item's description, the ingredients as « 2 / 3 Bois » (missing ones in the alert colour), the time, « Il faut : Établi niveau 2 », a quantity (− 1 +, Max: as many as the ingredients allow) and Craft (greyed when impossible); the queue at the bottom. Choose detaillee when the game has many recipes (more than about eight), for survival and role-playing games; give each recipe a categorie in the user's words (« Outils », « Armes », « Nourriture »). Keep liste for a handful of recipes. It is the screen « Fabrication détaillée » (see below); its keys: Fabrication détaillée/Recherche (the field), Fabrication détaillée/Catégorie#N (1: Tout), Fabrication détaillée/Case#N (Case rare#N for a rare item kind), Fabrication détaillée/Nom, Ingrédients, Il faut, Nombre, Moins / Plus / Max, Fabriquer. The Atelier tab of Equipment keeps its place in both looks (it opens in the screen « Fabrication »).
  • Spell every item exactly as the inventory stores it: the same kind as what collecte gives (ressource) and what the player picks up.

Events and hooks¶

  • Announced: fabrication.fabrique (objet, quantite, etabli, cible?; at once or at the end of the queue), fabrication.manque (objet, manque or etabli with niveau or combustible), fabrication.commencee (objet, quantite, temps, etabli, cible?), fabrication.finie, fabrication.station_amelioree (cible, etabli, niveau, nom, ancienne?), fabrication.allumee and fabrication.combustible_vide (cible, etabli).
  • Listened: fabrication.ouvrir (cible? or etabli?), fabrication.fabriquer (objet, cible? or etabli?), fabrication.ameliorer (cible), fabrication.alimenter (cible, quantite?): from a declencheur rule (« envoyer »), a drawn screen's button or a script. A Trigger rule « quand l'événement fabrication.station_amelioree » can play a sound or show a message.
  • Reserve a recipe from your own script, never the module's: game.override("fabrication.permise", function(default, recette, infos) if infos.connaissance == "Métallurgie" and not appris then return false, game.t("Il faut d'abord apprendre la métallurgie.") end return default(recette, infos) end). infos holds objet, quantite, etabli, niveau, temps, connaissance. A refused recipe is not shown.
  • Texts follow the player's language; its own texts are built-in texts listed by translations_get. Change one with game.override("fabrication.texte", ...).
  • Saved games: the player's queue, and each station's level and fire (the host keeps them). An upgraded station replaced by its level's model is kept by the engine (copied objects).
  • Tabs from other modules (hook fabrication.onglets): at a station, another module may add a tab to this window instead of opening its own; the Equipment adds « Atelier » (repair, upgrade, set gems) at the stations its settings name. The window then shows « Fabrication » and « Atelier » under its title (Fabrication/Onglet#1, Fabrication/Onglet#2..., rows of the list Onglets); the other tab is the shape Fabrication/Onglet ouvert, which its module fills when the event fabrication.onglet announces it (its parent field). A station without recipes, fuel or level to gain opens on that tab. fabrication.ouvrir {cible, onglet} opens a precise tab. With several players, fabrication.ouvrir opens for its joueur only; without joueur (a rule, a script of the game), for the host only.
  • The window is made of screens delivered by the system: « Fabrication » (modules/fabrication/ecrans/fenetre.json, the look liste, and another module's tab in both looks): Fenêtre, Titre, the list Onglets (row Onglet), Onglet ouvert, Station, Actions de la station (Alimenter, Améliorer), Vide, the list Recettes (row Recette: Image, Nom, Fabriquer), the list File (row Ligne de la file: En cours, Annuler), Message, Fermer. « Fabrication détaillée » (ecrans/detaillee.json): Voile, Fenêtre, Entête (Titre, Recherche), Onglets, Ligne de la station, Corps with the list Catégories, Cases (Vide, the grid list Grille, row Place: Case, Case rare) and Fiche (Image, Nom, Choisir, Description, Ingrédients, Temps, Il faut, Quantité, Fabriquer), the list File (with the gauge Progression), Message, Fermer. Their buttons send the system's actions (onglet, alimenter, ameliorer, fabriquer, annuler, fermer, and in the detailed one categorie, case, moins, plus, max). The script sets a few sizes from what it shows, as before the screens: the name beside an image, the list Recettes five rows high with more than five recipes, and in the detailed screen the height of Corps (0.36 with a queue, else 0.44), the width of Cases, the columns of Grille and the width of each Place (wider without categories). It reads the sizes the shapes have on screen (Fenêtre, Message, Fiche, Catégories): widen Fenêtre (or narrow Fiche) in the screen and the slots take the room left. Change their look, places and sizes with screens_set (styles, not hand-made values); never draw a crafting window with game.hud_*, and never edit the system. largeur is no longer used (kept for old games): resize the shape Fenêtre.
  • Gamepad and keyboard: the window's buttons get the focus like every window (the detailed one starts on its first slot); B or Escape closes it (Fabrication/Fermer). Keys of a row: Fabrication/Fabriquer#N, Fabrication/Annuler#N, Fabrication/En cours#N (N counts the rows shown). The line of the crafting under way, window closed, is the screen « Fabrication en cours » delivered by the system (modules/fabrication/ecrans/encours.json, its shape Ligne, key Fabrication en cours/Ligne): move it or change its look with screens_set, never by drawing it again.
  • Testing: in game_run, give the ingredients with inventaire.ajouter in a test script, then send fabrication.fabriquer with cible (the station's id), fabrication.alimenter, fabrication.ameliorer, or click Fabrication/Fabriquer#N; read the events and the inventory, and Fabrication/Message, in the report's hud.

Several players¶

One copy per player: each player has their own window, queue and ingredients (their own inventory). The stations are common: their level and fire are the same for everyone, burnt once per step.
