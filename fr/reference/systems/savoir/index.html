Savoir¶

Ce que le joueur sait faire, et comment il l'apprend : des connaissances qui débloquent des recettes de la Fabrication, des pièces de la Construction ou ce que le jeu veut. Chacune s'apprend d'une ou plusieurs façons : connue dès le départ, en obtenant un objet, en étudiant un objet à une table de recherche, avec des points dans un arbre, en lisant un plan, auprès d'un personnage ou d'une quête, en analysant des objets du monde, ou à force de fabriquer. Une fenêtre (K) montre ce qui est connu, ce qui peut s'apprendre et comment.

Événements¶

Les descriptions viennent du code et sont en anglais, la langue de travail de l'agent.

Ce qu'il annonce¶

Événement Champs Description
savoir.appris connaissance (text), moyen (text), categorie (text), etiquettes (text) The player learned a knowledge (version 26): connaissance is its name (original language), moyen how (depart, objet, etude, points, plan, evenement, marque, analyse, fabrication, apprendre), categorie its category, etiquettes the tags it unlocks, separated by commas. Each tag is also set as a Dialogue mark of the same word.
savoir.points points (number), gain (number) The player's knowledge points changed (version 26): points is the new total, gain what was added (negative when spent in the tree).
savoir.analyse connaissance (text), compte (number), besoin (number), cible (id) The player analysed an object of the world (version 26): cible, which counts for the knowledge connaissance; compte analyses done, besoin needed.
savoir.refus connaissance (text), raison (text) A knowledge could not be learned (version 26): raison says why, in the player's language (what is needed first, missing points or items, a game script's refusal).
dialogue.marquer marque (text), retirer (boolean?) Ask the dialogue system to set a mark, from a script or another system; with retirer true, to remove it. Dialogue conditions then see it.
inventaire.retirer objet (text), quantite (number), fiche (number?) Ask the inventory to remove items, such as a payment. Answered by inventaire.change, or inventaire.manque if there are not enough. For a kind with unique copies (version 15): fiche removes that copy, otherwise the first ones; their records are destroyed.
inventaire.ajouter objet (text), quantite (number), joueur (id?), fiche (number?) Ask the inventory to add items, such as a reward. Answered by inventaire.change, or inventaire.plein. With several players, joueur says whose bag; without it, the player whose action is being handled, or every player.

Ce qu'il écoute¶

Événement Champs Description
savoir.apprendre connaissance (text), joueur (id?) Ask the knowledge system to teach connaissance to the player (version 26), even without what it needs first: a Trigger rule, a master in a Dialogue, a quest's reward. A game script can still refuse it (savoir.permise).
savoir.donner_points points (number), joueur (id?) Ask the knowledge system to give points to the player (version 26), to spend in the knowledge window: a level gained, a book read.
savoir.ouvrir table (text?) Ask the knowledge system to open its window (version 26); with table, the study of that research table's items.
savoir.etudier objet (text), table (text?) Ask the knowledge system to study objet as at the research table named table (empty or missing: any table), as its button would (version 26). Refused, with savoir.refus, if something is missing.
inventaire.change objet (text), quantite (number), total (number), chargement (boolean?), fiche (number?), jete (boolean?) The quantity of an item changed. total is the new quantity. chargement is true when a saved game gives the items back (game.load): nothing was picked up. For a unique copy (version 15), objet is its kind, fiche its record's number, total how many of that kind. With several stacks of a kind (version 21), total counts all of them; jete is true when a slot was dropped on the ground.
inventaire.choisi objet (text), total (number), fiche (number?), double (boolean?), case (text?) The player clicked a slot of the inventory window: objet is the kind of item, total how many they carry. What it does belongs to the game: eat, equip, read. case (version 21) is the slot's key ("Wood~2" for a second stack).
inventaire.manque objet (text), quantite (number), fiche (number?) Items could not be removed: there are not enough.
inventaire.plein objet (text), fiche (number?), reste (number?) An item could not be added: every slot holds another kind of item (or another unique copy), or (version 21) every stack of its kind is full. reste: picked up in part, how many stayed on the ground.
fabrication.fabrique objet (text), quantite (number), etabli (text), cible (text?), xp (number?), piste (text?) The player crafted a recipe: its ingredients left the inventory and objet (quantite of it) came in, at once or at the end of its time in the queue. etabli is the kind of station where it was made (empty bare-handed), cible the station object (missing bare-handed).
interaction.utiliser cible (id), joueur (id) The player used the object in reach: a door, a merchant, an item to pick up. Whoever owns that object decides what it does.
dialogue.marque marque (text) A dialogue set a mark: a word the game remembers (a line shown, an answer chosen). Every dialogue's conditions can use it.
langue.change langue (text) Sent by the engine when the player changes the game's language (game.set_language, the Menu options). A module redraws what it already shows: its texts go through game.t and game.language.

Points qu'un script peut remplacer (game.override)¶

Les descriptions viennent du code et sont en anglais, la langue de travail de l'agent.

  • savoir.permise : function(default, connaissance, moyen) -> true, or false and the reason shown (text): whether this knowledge can be learned now. connaissance is its name (original language), moyen the way (depart, objet, etude, points, plan, evenement, marque, analyse, fabrication, apprendre). The default is true. To ask for a level, a place, a time of day.
  • savoir.texte : function(default, key) -> the text the knowledge window shows for that key: titre, titre_table, points, aucune, fermer, tout, connu, a_apprendre, verrouille, il_faut, apprendre, etudier, appris, deja, pas_assez, manque, refus, etude_rien, analyse, comment, moyen_depart, moyen_objet, moyen_etude, moyen_etude_table, moyen_cout, moyen_points, moyen_plan, moyen_evenement, moyen_marque, moyen_analyse, moyen_fabrication, moyen_autre, a_apprendre_raison, invite_table, invite_analyse. It may hold {connaissance}, {objet}, {n}, {total}, {table}, {liste} and {raison}, replaced afterwards (in the player's language). The default is the game's translation of the text in the original language; without one, French or English, after the player's language.

Knowledge (savoir)¶

What the player knows how to do, and how they learn it: the research / tech tree / blueprint / recipe-discovery system of survival games, in user data. Activating it activates inventory and interaction; placing it on an object places interaction there too.

  • Knowledges: the game setting connaissances, a list of rows. A connaissance row (nom, description, categorie: a tab of the window, icone: an image, secrete: hidden until learned) is followed by its own rows:
  • prerequis (prerequis: another knowledge's name): needed first, whatever the way. Several rows: all needed. The window draws them as a tree.
  • debloque (debloque: recette | piece | etiquette, debloque_nom): a Crafting recipe (the name of what it gives) or a Building piece (its name) that stays closed until the knowledge is learned (game setting verrouiller, on); or a free tag, which the Trigger condition, scripts and Dialogue tests can look at (learning it also sets a Dialogue mark of the same word).
  • moyen (moyen, then its fields): one way to learn it. Add several rows for several ways; the first met is enough.
    • depart: known from the start.
    • objet + objet: when the player obtains that kind of item (picked up, crafted, given). Learned later if what it needs first is missing then.
    • etude + objet, nombre (used up), cout and cout_nombre (optional extra cost, used up too), table (a research table's name, empty: any): studied at a research table.
    • points + nombre: bought with points in the window (K).
    • plan + objet: reading a plan; a click on its slot in the inventory window uses one up (not if already known).
    • evenement + evenement: an event name; send it from a Dialogue answer (annonce), a quest's end (annonce), a Trigger rule (envoyer) or a script.
    • marque + marque: a Dialogue or quest mark.
    • analyse + nombre: analysing that many objects of the world (see below).
    • fabrication + objet, nombre: crafting that recipe that many times (learning by doing).
  • Research table: any object (an imported model) with this system (systems_attach), object setting role table (the default), nom_table (empty: its name without number). Using it (E) opens the study of what can be learned there.
  • Objects to analyse: object setting role analyse, analyse = the knowledge's name, analyse_retirer to remove it once analysed. Each object counts once. Tag many objects (wrecks, plants) the same way.
  • Points: points_depart; the list gains (evenement, cible: only for the object named, points), e.g. fabrication.fabrique 1, sante.mort with Wolf 2, quetes.fin 5; the Trigger action donner_points; the event savoir.donner_points.
  • Trigger: actions apprendre (connaissance; ignores what is needed first) and donner_points (points); every rule has condition (aucune, connait, ignore) and condition_valeur (a knowledge or a tag): a door only an initiate opens.
  • Events: it announces savoir.appris (connaissance, moyen, categorie, etiquettes), savoir.points, savoir.analyse, savoir.refus; it listens to savoir.apprendre (without connaissance, since version 4: the first one the player does not know yet), savoir.donner_points, savoir.ouvrir (table optional), savoir.etudier.
  • Hooks: savoir.permise (refuse learning now: a level, a place), savoir.texte (every text of the window). It answers fabrication.permise, construction.permise, declencheur.condition and interaction.texte itself: do not override those to lock recipes, write a knowledge instead.
  • A recipe of Crafting can also name its own knowledge (passed to fabrication.permise as an extra argument): it is closed until that knowledge or tag is known.
  • Window: control knowledge (K) (fenetre), pause_fenetre. largeur (old) is no longer used: widen the window by its screen.
  • Its window and its announcement are two screens delivered by the system (modules/savoir/ecrans/), which its script shows and fills:
  • « Savoir »: Fenêtre, Titre, Points, the list Onglets (row Onglet: Tout, then each category; the system's action onglet), the list Liste (it scrolls past the height given in its file), whose row Ligne holds Retrait (one step of the tree per level: its width in the file), Icône, Nom (the knowledge, its state, how to learn it), Apprendre (a button, the action apprendre: with points, or Étudier at a research table) and Vide (nothing to learn, alone on its row); then Message and Fermer (action fermer).
  • « Annonce du savoir » (top centre): Annonce (« Appris : Arc », an analysis). Change their look, places and sizes with screens_set (styles, not hand-made values): the rows' text takes what the window's width leaves, so widen Fenêtre and the rows follow. Never draw them again with game.hud_*, and never edit the system.
  • Gamepad and keyboard: B or Escape closes the window (Savoir/Fermer is its back button), like A or Enter on Close; the tabs and the Apprendre buttons are reached like any other button.
  • Testing: in game_run, press knowledge and read the report's hud: Savoir/Titre, Savoir/Points, the rows Savoir/Nom#<row> and Savoir/Apprendre#<row>, the tabs Savoir/Onglet#<n>, Savoir/Message; the announcement Annonce du savoir/Annonce.
  • Saved games keep what is learned, the points, the analyses and the counters.

Recipes¶

Write names in the user's language. Recipes and pieces must already exist in fabrication / construction.

  • The default (a new game, the logic Khalil chose for survival): the default rows match the default recipes of fabrication: « Outils de pierre » (Hache, Pioche, Lance) and « Cuisine au feu » (Viande cuite, Poisson grillé, Baies grillées) known from the start (moyen depart); « Travail du fer » (Fer, Hache de fer, Pioche de fer) learned by obtaining « Minerai de fer » (moyen objet); « Boulangerie » (Pain, Ragoût) by obtaining « Farine », after « Cuisine au feu »; « Arc » studied at a research table (moyen etude, objet « Arc », the bow found is used up). So for « un jeu de survie », keep this logic (learn by obtaining an ingredient, study at a research table, a few known from the start), rename it in the user's words, and place one research table (any model, role table) near the workbench. Switch to points (ARK) or another style below only if the user asks.
  • Like Rust (research table + scrap, then a tech tree): a research table (any bench model, role table, nom_table "Research table"). Each weapon or tool: a knowledge debloque recette, with moyen etude (objet the item itself, cout "Scrap", cout_nombre 75). The tree: knowledges with prerequis and moyen points; gains give points for collecte.recolte or crafting. Shared tech for a team: commun on.
  • Like Valheim (discover by holding an ingredient; station level): each recipe's knowledge with moyen objet = its key ingredient ("Copper" teaches "Bronze axe"); chain them with prerequis (bronze needs copper smelting). The station level belongs to Crafting (its workbench name/level); the knowledge just opens the recipe. No window needed: fenetre can stay on as a codex.
  • Like Enshrouded (craftsmen unlock recipes): a character with a Dialogue whose answer sends an event (annonce "blacksmith.rescued"); knowledges with moyen evenement on it; or a quest's end (annonce); or a Trigger rule apprendre on the rescued NPC. Categories per craftsman ("Blacksmith", "Alchemist").
  • Like ARK (engrams with level points): every knowledge moyen points (nombre = its cost) and prerequis; gains on the level-up event of the game (or quetes.fin, sante.mort with cible); points_depart for the first ones.
  • Like Subnautica (scan fragments): fragments are objects with role analyse and analyse_retirer; the knowledge moyen analyse nombre 3.
  • Blueprints: an item "Forge blueprint" to pick up (inventaire), and a knowledge moyen plan objet "Forge blueprint".
  • Learning by doing: moyen fabrication objet "Arrow" nombre 20 teaches "Iron arrows".

Several players¶

One copy per player: each learns for themselves, with their own points (their copy answers the recipe and piece locks for them only), and sees their own window and announcements. The game setting commun ("One knowledge for the whole group") shares what is learned and the points; the host's save keeps it.
