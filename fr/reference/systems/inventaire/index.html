Ce qu'il annonce¶

Événement Champs Description
inventaire.change objet (text), quantite (number), total (number), chargement (boolean?) The quantity of an item changed. total is the new quantity. chargement is true when a saved game gives the items back (game.load): nothing was picked up.
inventaire.plein objet (text) An item could not be added: every slot holds another kind of item.
inventaire.manque objet (text), quantite (number) Items could not be removed: there are not enough.
inventaire.choisi objet (text), total (number) The player clicked a slot of the inventory window: objet is the kind of item, total how many they carry. What it does belongs to the game: eat, equip, read.
inventaire.en_main objet (text), avant (text), place (number), chargement (boolean?), joueur (id?) What the player holds in hand changed (version 24): the slot chosen in the inventory's bar (keys 1 to 9, wheel), an item used up, a load (chargement true). objet is the kind held, "" for empty hands; avant the one held before; place the bar slot (0: nothing). Gathering checks the tool held; a game script equips a sword with it.

Ce qu'il écoute¶

Événement Champs Description
interaction.utiliser cible (id), joueur (id) The player used the object in reach: a door, a merchant, an item to pick up. Whoever owns that object decides what it does.
inventaire.ajouter objet (text), quantite (number), joueur (id?) Ask the inventory to add items, such as a reward. Answered by inventaire.change, or inventaire.plein. With several players, joueur says whose bag; without it, the player whose action is being handled, or every player.
inventaire.retirer objet (text), quantite (number) Ask the inventory to remove items, such as a payment. Answered by inventaire.change, or inventaire.manque if there are not enough.
inventaire.tenir objet (text?), place (number?), joueur (id?) Ask the inventory to hold something in hand (version 24): a bar slot (place), or a kind of item (objet; one outside the bar takes the chosen slot). objet "" puts the item away: empty hands.
joueur.occupe occupe (boolean), par (text) The player's hands are taken by something else than fighting (version 23): a building mode where the click places a piece, a tool being aimed. occupe true when it starts, false when it ends; par names who says it ("construction"), so several can overlap. Fighting modules do not strike nor block while any of them keeps the player busy.
hud.clic cible (text), joueur (id?) A button of the HUD was clicked: pressed, then released on it; or chosen with A (Enter) while it has the gamepad or keyboard focus; or the window's back button (back = true), with B (Escape). cible is the button's key (game.hud_button), joueur the player who clicked (a guest clicks on their own computer; the host plays the click). Announced by the engine, before the scripts' on_update of that step.
langue.change langue (text) Sent by the engine when the player changes the game's language (game.set_language, the Menu options). A module redraws what it already shows: its texts go through game.t and game.language.

Inventory (inventaire)¶

Keeps what the player carries, grouped by kind. Activating it activates the interaction module; placing it on an object places interaction there too, so the player can use it.

  • An item to pick up: an object carrying this system (systems_attach), or every child of a group carrying it. Its kind is nom if set (systems_set), otherwise its name without a trailing number: "Apple 3" is an "Apple". quantite is how many one pickup gives: a bag of 50 coins. Name items in the user's language.
  • Picking up: the player uses the item (E by default), or walks over it if the game setting au_contact is on. The item leaves the world.
  • Give or take items from your scripts, such as a reward or a payment: game.emit("inventaire.ajouter", {objet = "Apple", quantite = 2}), game.emit("inventaire.retirer", {objet = "Gold", quantite = 10}).
  • Know what the player has: listen to inventaire.change (objet, quantite, total). A shop checks total before selling, or listens to inventaire.manque, sent when there are not enough.
  • inventaire.plein is sent when every slot holds another kind of item (game setting places).
  • The content shows at the top right with the HUD key inventaire.
  • The window: the control inventory (I, or Back on a gamepad) opens a grid with one slot per kind, and closes it (game setting fenetre). It scrolls beyond four rows; colonnes sets the slots per row; pause_fenetre stops the world while it is open. Its HUD keys start with inventaire..
  • Gamepad and keyboard: the first slot has the focus, the d-pad or the arrows move it, A or Enter chooses it, B or Escape closes the window (inventaire.fermer is its back button).
  • Saved games: game.save keeps what the player carries, and game.load gives it back; then the inventory announces each kind again with inventaire.change and chargement = true (nothing was picked up), so that every system knows the totals. Do not re-add items after a load.
  • The item in hand (game setting barre, on by default): a bar of cases_barre slots (5) at the bottom of the screen (place_barre; afficher_barre off if the game draws its own). What is picked up fills the first free slot; keys 1 to 9, the wheel (item_next, item_previous) and the d-pad left (item_next: it goes round the slots; every other gamepad button already belongs to a system) choose the slot, and its key again puts the item away. What is held is announced by inventaire.en_main (objet, "" for empty hands, avant, place): collecte gathers with the tool held, a game script equips a sword with it. Choose it from a script with game.emit("inventaire.tenir", {objet = "Axe"}) (or place, or objet = ""). In the window, a number key puts the hovered slot at that place of the bar. The bar, the chosen slot and what is held are saved; each player has their own.
  • Its model in the hand (montrer_en_main, on): a copy of its model follows the character's hand bone, found by measuring the skeleton (game.hand_bone: a bone made for holding under the right hand, else the right hand; os_main overrides it). The model comes from the list en_main (a row per kind: objet, modele, an object of the scene, usually hidden, and an offset and rotation along the bone), else from the first item of that kind picked up. Best: set the model in the character's hand in the editor (entities_create with attach_bone), check it with a capture, hide it, and name it in en_main: its exact place is reused. Do not leave a visible model in the hand, nor write a script that shows or hides one.
  • A click on a slot sends inventaire.choisi (objet, total). What it does belongs to the game: eat an apple (inventaire.retirer, then sante.soigner), equip a sword, read a letter. Write that in a game script, or a declencheur rule on the event.

Several players¶

One copy per player: each player has their own bag by default. The game setting sac_commun ("One bag for the whole group") gives everyone the same bag. The host's saved game keeps each guest's bag under their player key: they find it again when they come back.
