Carte¶

Une mini-carte dans un coin de l'écran, le joueur au centre, et une grande carte de toute la scène avec la touche M, qu'on zoome et qu'on déplace à la souris ou à la manette (gâchettes et stick). Ce qui porte ce système y apparaît comme un repère : une forme et une couleur, ou une image, et son nom ; toujours, une fois découvert de près, ou seulement comme objectif d'une quête. Les marchands, les personnages qui parlent, les objectifs des quêtes en cours et, à plusieurs, les autres joueurs avec leur nom se repèrent tout seuls. Le joueur pose ses propres repères, coche ce qu'il veut voir, et peut suivre une boussole en haut de l'écran.
Identifiant carte
Version 15
Se pose sur des objets oui

Événements¶

Les descriptions viennent du code et sont en anglais, la langue de travail de l'agent.

Ce qu'il annonce¶

Événement Champs Description
carte.ouverte ouvert (boolean) The big map opened (ouvert true) or closed (ouvert false). The world is stopped while it is open, unless its setting says otherwise.
carte.decouvert cible (id) The player discovered a map marker (cible), one shown only once discovered: they came near enough, or carte.reveler revealed it. It now shows on the map.

Ce qu'il écoute¶

Événement Champs Description
carte.reveler cible (id) Ask the map to reveal the marker of cible, as if the player had come near: a character who tells where a place is, a scroll read, a quest step.
quetes.debut quete (text), groupe (boolean?) A quest started: quete is its title. groupe (Journal de quêtes 8): true for a quest of the whole group (game setting groupe), sent once for the group, joueur being who moved it on.
quetes.etape_debut quete (text), etape (number), texte (text), groupe (boolean?) A step of a quest starts (Journal de quêtes 11, events version 33): it becomes one to do now, its other ways to end it too. etape is its rank in the quest (from 1), texte what it says. Sent after quetes.debut for the first steps, then after the quetes.etape that opened it, and again when the step starts over (an "Also fails if" row set to recommence) or the quest resumes at it (quetes.reprise); its own annonce_debut event follows. Not sent again when a saved game is loaded. groupe (Journal de quêtes 8): true for a quest of the whole group.
quetes.etape quete (text), etape (number), texte (text), issue (number?), suite (text?), groupe (boolean?) A step of a quest is done: etape is its rank in the quest (from 1), texte what it said. Sent before quetes.fin when it was the last one. issue (Journal de quêtes 11): the rank (from 1) of the other way to end the step that did it, absent when the step itself did; texte is then that way's text. suite (Journal de quêtes 11): the label the quest goes to next (fin, echec or a step's label), absent when it simply goes on to the next row. groupe (Journal de quêtes 8): true for a quest of the whole group (game setting groupe), sent once for the group, joueur being who moved it on.
quetes.etape_echec quete (text), etape (number), alors (text), manquee (boolean?), groupe (boolean?) A step of a quest failed (Journal de quêtes 11, events version 33): one of its "Also fails if" rows happened. etape is its rank in the quest (from 1), alors what that row does: echoue (the quest fails, quetes.echec follows), recommence (the step starts again from zero) or etiquette (the quest goes to the row's label). Also sent for an "Also fails if" row under the quest itself that does not make it fail (recommence: the quest starts again; etiquette), etape being then the step it was waiting for (0: none). For an optional step, manquee is true: the step is only missed, the quest goes on without it. groupe: true for a quest of the whole group.
quetes.reprise quete (text), etape (number), groupe (boolean?) A quest resumes (Journal de quêtes 11, events version 33): tried again after a failure (the log's Try again button, reessayer auto, quetes.reessayer, or given again when it does not start over), or started again by an "Also fails if" row of the quest set to recommence. etape is the step it resumes at (from 1; 0: none). The steps before it stay done; marks stay set and the world is not put back as it was: a Trigger rule on the step's annonce_debut does that. groupe: true for a quest of the whole group.
quetes.fin quete (text), xp (number?), piste (text?), groupe (boolean?), facultatives (table?) A quest is finished: all its steps are done. Its reward, if any, has been asked to the inventory. xp and piste (version 28): its XP for the Progression module and the track that gets it (empty: the main one). facultatives (Journal de quêtes 11): {faites, total}, how many of its optional steps were done, out of how many; absent when it has none. groupe (Journal de quêtes 8): true for a quest of the whole group (game setting groupe), sent once for the group, joueur being who moved it on.
quetes.echec quete (text), groupe (boolean?) A quest failed: an event, a mark, its time ran out, or quetes.echouer. It gives no reward; it may be given again if its settings allow it. groupe (Journal de quêtes 8): true for a quest of the whole group (game setting groupe), sent once for the group, joueur being who moved it on.
quetes.abandon quete (text), groupe (boolean?) The player abandoned a quest from the log (or quetes.abandonner asked for it). It gives no reward; it may be given again if its settings allow it. groupe (Journal de quêtes 8): true for a quest of the whole group (game setting groupe), sent once for the group, joueur being who moved it on.
scene.change depuis (text), vers (text) Sent by the engine once the player has arrived in another scene of the game (game.go_to, or a passage). Scripts keep running across scenes: this is when a module looks at the objects of the new scene.
menu.pause ouvert (boolean) The pause menu opened (ouvert true) or closed (ouvert false). The world is stopped while it is open.

Points qu'un script peut remplacer (game.override)¶

Les descriptions viennent du code et sont en anglais, la langue de travail de l'agent.

  • carte.texte : function(default, key) -> the text the map shows for that key: titre, fermer, aide, decouvert, vous. It may hold {nom}, replaced afterwards. The default is the game's translation of the text in the original language (a built-in text of the map in translations_get); without one, French or English, after the player's language.
  • carte.couleur_joueur : function(default, joueur) -> the color ("#rrggbb") of that other player's arrow and name on the map, such as their team's color. The default is a color of their player number (game.player_number), the same for everyone.

Ce que l'agent sait de ce système¶

Le texte que l'agent lit avant de se servir de ce système (en anglais, la langue de travail de l'agent).

Map (carte)¶

A minimap in a corner of the screen, the player in the middle as an arrow, and a big map of the whole scene opened with the control carte (M, or ↓ on a gamepad), zoomed with the mouse wheel or with the controls carte_rapprocher (Page Up, or the right trigger RT on a gamepad) and carte_eloigner (Page Down, or the left trigger LT), held, and moved by dragging with the mouse, with the gamepad's left stick or with the walking keys. While the map has the gamepad's focus the player does not move, so LT (also run) does not make them run. Add it with modules_add when the user asks for a map, a minimap, markers or places to discover: do not build a map yourself.

The picture is the scene seen from above, taken by the engine when the scene starts (north, -Z, at the top), without the player and characters. The game setting dessin can give a drawn map instead (an image file): by default it covers the same square as the engine's view (dessin_zone "auto"); with dessin_zone "manuelle", dessin_x, dessin_z (its north-west corner) and dessin_cote (its side, in metres) say where it lies.

Markers, without code: place the system on an object (systems_attach), at its position, or on a group or folder: one marker for everything inside, sub-folders included, in the middle of the box enclosing its pieces (the folder's own position does not matter), discovered when the player comes within distance of that box. A piece inside with its own values for carte (systems_set on it) becomes its own marker. Its object settings:

  • nom: the name shown under it on the big map; empty, the name of the object or group without a trailing number. Write it in the user's language.
  • forme: rond, losange, triangle, carre, etoile, or image with image (an icon file, find one with assets_search and kind "image"); couleur ("#rrggbb", tints an image); taille (1 usual, up to 3).
  • visible: toujours; decouvert, shown once the player came within distance metres, or when your script or a declencheur rule sends carte.reveler {cible = id} (the object or group, or any piece of the group); cache, shown only while it is a quest objective.
  • quete and quete_etape: the exact title of a quetes quest (and a step number, 0 for the whole quest) for which this marker is the objective, such as the miller for "Go back to the miller".

What marks itself (game settings marchands and personnages, on by default): objects carrying boutique (a golden diamond) and dialogue (a light blue circle), named after the shop or the character, one marker per object or group they are placed on. Placing carte on one of them chooses its look instead.

Quest objectives (game setting objectifs, couleur_objectif): while a quetes step is active, the markers it names are ringed in that color, and on the minimap they stay at its edge, pointing the way. A step names objects through fin_cible (the "Loup" of "Vaincre 3 loups": every marker on an object or group named "Loup", "Loup 2"...) or its fin_lieu (a lieu or tenir step), and so do the other ways to end it (issue rows: both doors of a branch are objectives until one is taken); or a marker names its quest with quete. The quest log is not changed: the map reads what it publishes at each quetes.* event (version 13, below).

Other game settings: mini_carte (shown or not), mini_coin (bas_droite by default, bas_gauche, haut_droite, haut_gauche; the inventory and the current quest step are at the top right), mini_taille (share of the screen height), mini_forme (ronde or carree), mini_etendue (metres shown from edge to edge), mini_tourne (turns with the player, otherwise north up), cadre_marge (the map tucked inside a theme frame), grande_carte (the M window), pause_fenetre (the world stops while it is open, and the big map is a screen of its own: the rest of the HUD, such as the hotbar, health or the quest tracker, is hidden with game.hud_only({"carte.", "Grande carte/"}) until it closes; a pause menu opened over it (menu.pause) still shows), noms (names on the big map), annoncer (a message when a marker is discovered).

  • Events announced: carte.ouverte (ouvert), carte.decouvert (cible: the object or group the system is placed on, once for all its pieces). Listened: carte.reveler (cible).
  • The minimap hides during a pause (a menu, a dialogue, a shop) and while the big map is open.
  • Saved games keep the places already discovered, in every scene (on_save, on_load): a loaded game shows them again without announcing them.
  • Texts follow the player's language: the map's own texts are built-in texts of the system, listed by translations_get (French and English are built in). To change a word, override carte.texte from a game script: game.override("carte.texte", function(default, key) if key == "titre" then return "Atlas" end return default(key) end). Keys: titre, fermer, aide, decouvert (with {nom}), vous; version 10: aide_repere (with {touche}), repere (with {n}), distance (with {d}), legende, quetes, s_objectifs, s_lieux, s_marchands, s_personnages, s_joueurs, s_mes_reperes, and the compass points n, ne, e, se, s, so, o, no; version 14: donne ("!") and rendre ("?").

Fog of war (version 11)¶

  • brouillard (off by default): on the big map and the minimap, what the player has not approached yet stays covered in brouillard_couleur (dark by default). Walking uncovers everything within brouillard_rayon metres (25 by default) around the player. Places under the fog do not show (on the map, the minimap and the compass), except quest objectives and the player's own markers. Saved games keep it, for each player, in each scene. brouillard_groupe: what one player uncovers is uncovered for everyone; otherwise each has their own.
  • When to turn it on: exploration, survival, an adventure where discovering the world is part of the fun (Valheim, Enshrouded, Zelda style). Leave it off for a shooter, a race, a town or farm game, a small arena, or when the player must read the whole map at once.
  • The map area is cut into 32 × 32 squares: the edges are smooth curves, about one square wide; on a very large scene (more than about 1 km), the uncovered area grows by bigger steps. It costs one HUD element per map (an image @mask: the engine computes), whatever is uncovered: sent to guests like any image.

Compass, the player's markers, legend and quest list (version 10)¶

  • Compass (game setting boussole, off by default; boussole_position haut or bas, in the middle): a band that scrolls as the camera turns, with N, NE, E… every 45°, degrees every 30° and ticks every 15°, a fixed mark in the middle. On it, at their heading: quest objectives (held at the band's edge when behind), the player's markers, and the places visible on the map within boussole_portee metres; the distance of the nearest objective is written under it. Style panneau.boussole for its background (the shape Bande). It hides during a pause and while the big map is open. Only what fits in the band is created (about 20 HUD elements).
  • Compass or minimap? A survival game in the manner of Rust (find your way by the sun and landmarks) sets boussole on and mini_carte off: the big map (M) stays for planning. An adventure or RPG game with towns, merchants and quests keeps the minimap, and may add the compass for the quest objective's direction (Valheim, WoW style). With both, put the minimap in a bottom corner (mini_coin) so the top stays clear.
  • The player's markers (reperes_joueur, on by default): on the big map, the control carte_repere (right click, or Y on a gamepad at the crosshair in the middle of the view) places "Marker 1", "Marker 2"… at that spot, or removes the one there; a left click without dragging on one removes it too. They show on the big map, the minimap and the compass, in couleur_reperes, at most reperes_max. Saved games keep them, for each player, in each scene. reperes_groupe: shared with all the players (anyone can remove one); otherwise each has their own.
  • Legend (legende, on by default): on the right of the big map, one button per kind of marker present (objectives, places, merchants, characters, players, my markers); a click hides or shows that kind on the big map and the minimap, kept for the player by saved games. Its rows (Grande carte/Sorte#<row>) have the style coche.carte, selected while shown.
  • Quest list (liste_quetes, on by default, only with the quetes system): on the left of the big map, the quests in progress; a click centers the map on its objective. It does not change the quest log.

Several players¶

One copy per player: each player has their own minimap, map window, discovered places, compass, legend choices and markers (unless reperes_groupe shares the markers).

The other players show by themselves (game setting joueurs: partout by default, on the minimap and the big map; grande, only on the big map; aucun, nowhere, for hide and seek): an arrow turned where they look, in a color of their player number (the same for everyone; a game script gives another one, such as a team's, with game.override("carte.couleur_joueur", function(default, joueur) ... end)), and their name (game.player_name: their character's name or nickname), on the guests' maps as on the host's. Do not place carte on the player's character for that: the markers would all carry the copied character's look. Placed there anyway, it chooses the look of the others' markers, which are named after each player (never after the object), and nobody sees one on themselves. Testing: with game_run « players », a guest's hud has carte.mini.j.<n> (the arrows), carte.mini.jn.<n> and, map open, carte.g.jn.<n> (the names).

GPS (version 15)¶

  • gps (aucun by default, so a game without it is unchanged; auto: by vehicle while the player drives (game.driving()), on foot otherwise; a_pied; vehicule): a line on the minimap and the big map from the player to the objective of the quest highest in the quest log (the nearest of its objectives), or to the player's last marker (gps_cible: auto the last marker placed, otherwise the objective; objectif; repere). The way comes from game.route: in a generated city it follows the streets (by vehicle in the driving direction, through roundabouts by their ring and the left-turn lanes; on foot both ways), elsewhere the walkable areas (game.path). The map never computes a way itself.
  • By vehicle the route may change to the next lane of the same direction (gps_changement_voie, 15 m added) and turns back only at the end of a dead end (gps_demi_tour, 300 m added).
  • It is redone when the target changes or moves more than gps_ecart metres (15), when the player strays more than gps_ecart from the line, or gets in or out of a vehicle (auto); checked a few times a second. It is one smooth thick line (a curve through the points of game.route with axe = true: the middle of the streets, not the shifted lane), round ends, a glow: gps_couleur (#3d8bff), gps_epaisseur (pixels for a 1080 high screen, 7), gps_halo (pixels, 5), gps_halo_couleur (empty: the line's), gps_halo_opacite (0.3). The destination is a diamond of the line's color (image_destination: an image of the user's). mini_distance writes the distance along the route under the minimap; panneau_destination shows on the big map the destination's name, district and distance.
  • When to turn it on: a town or open-world game with quests or deliveries (GTA, a taxi or delivery game), or a large world where the way is not obvious. Leave it off for a small level, an arena, or an exploration game where finding the way is the point.
  • Testing: the report's hud has carte.mini.gps (an image @trace:..., laid like the map image), carte.mini.destination, carte.mini.distance and, map open, carte.g.gps, carte.g.destination, carte.g.dest.nom, carte.g.dest.lieu, carte.g.echelle.texte.

The map's look (version 15)¶

  • fond_carte (plan by default): the map is a vector plan made once from the scene's data (game.map_plan: the streets of a generated city by importance, water, parks, squares, buildings, the relief and water of the terrain) and drawn in the game's colors, with the district and town names in capitals (plan_noms, mini_noms). vue: the photo of the scene seen from above, as before. A drawn map (dessin) comes before both.
  • Theme, by hand in the system's settings or with modules_configure: plan_fond, plan_grandes_rues, plan_rues, plan_petites_rues, plan_chemins, plan_largeur_rues (times the real width, 0.5), plan_eau, plan_parcs, plan_places, plan_batiments + plan_couleur_batiments, plan_relief, plan_couleur_noms. To match a reference image (a dark slate plan with light streets and a bright route): set the background, the four street colors, water and parks from it, then gps_couleur, gps_halo.
  • Minimap: mini_forme arrondie by default (a rounded rectangle, mini_arrondi, with a thin border mini_bordure, mini_bordure_couleur, mini_bordure_opacite), ronde or carree; mini_coin, mini_taille, mini_tourne (turns with the player, or north fixed), a "N" badge. image_cadre lays an image over it (a decorated ring).
  • Images of the user's: image_joueur (the player's arrow, pointing up), image_destination, image_cadre, and one icon per kind of marker: picto_marchands, picto_personnages, picto_objectifs, picto_lieux, picto_mes_reperes (in their own colors; a marker's own image wins).
  • Names: in capitals with spaced letters (plan_noms_espacement: aucun by default, fin a thin space if the font has one, large a full space), in plan_police (a shipped font such as manrope, or projet:<id>; empty: the game's), on a dark rounded background (plan_noms_fond, its opacity). A name never covers the route, the player or the destination: it moves to the nearest free place, or hides (game.map_labels, recomputed when the route or the zoom changes).
  • Plan content from a generated city: the street axes (cut at a roundabout's island), the ring and island of each roundabout, parks (free ground in grass) and squares (paved free ground), building footprints, district and town names; the terrain adds its sea and lakes.
  • Destination: destination_seule (on): the diamond at the end of the line replaces the objective's marker on the maps. The line starts and ends on the street: the walk from the street to an off-street target is not drawn.
  • Big map: zoom and drag as before, the destination panel, a scale bar (echelle, "200 m"), the legend with the icon of each kind (its picto_*, or its shape and color), placing a marker.
  • Never copy a game's name, sprites or texts into another: the user gives their own images in these settings.
