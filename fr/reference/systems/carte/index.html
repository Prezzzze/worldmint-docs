Carte¶

Une mini-carte dans un coin de l'écran, le joueur au centre, et une grande carte de toute la scène avec la touche M, qu'on zoome et qu'on déplace à la souris ou à la manette (gâchettes et stick). Ce qui porte ce système y apparaît comme un repère : une forme et une couleur, ou une image, et son nom ; toujours, une fois découvert de près, ou seulement comme objectif d'une quête. Les marchands, les personnages qui parlent et les objectifs des quêtes en cours se repèrent tout seuls.
Identifiant carte
Version 6
Se pose sur des objets oui

Événements¶

Les descriptions viennent du code et sont en anglais, la langue de travail de l'agent.

Ce qu'il annonce¶

Événement Champs Description
carte.ouverte ouvert (boolean) The big map opened (ouvert true) or closed (ouvert false). The world is stopped while it is open, unless its setting says otherwise.
carte.decouvert cible (id) The player discovered a map marker (cible), one shown only once discovered: they came near enough, or carte.reveler revealed it. It now shows on the map.

Ce qu'il écoute¶

Événement Champs Description
carte.reveler cible (id) Ask the map to reveal the marker of cible, as if the player had come near: a character who tells where a place is, a scroll read, a quest step.
quetes.debut quete (text) A quest started: quete is its title.
quetes.etape quete (text), etape (number), texte (text) A step of a quest is done: etape is its rank in the quest (from 1), texte what it said. Sent before quetes.fin when it was the last one.
quetes.fin quete (text) A quest is finished: all its steps are done. Its reward, if any, has been asked to the inventory.
scene.change depuis (text), vers (text) Sent by the engine once the player has arrived in another scene of the game (game.go_to, or a passage). Scripts keep running across scenes: this is when a module looks at the objects of the new scene.
hud.clic cible (text), joueur (id?) A button of the HUD was clicked: pressed, then released on it; or chosen with A (Enter) while it has the gamepad or keyboard focus; or the window's back button (back = true), with B (Escape). cible is the button's key (game.hud_button), joueur the player who clicked (a guest clicks on their own computer; the host plays the click). Announced by the engine, before the scripts' on_update of that step.

Points qu'un script peut remplacer (game.override)¶

Les descriptions viennent du code et sont en anglais, la langue de travail de l'agent.

  • carte.texte : function(default, key) -> the text the map shows for that key: titre, fermer, aide, decouvert, vous. It may hold {nom}, replaced afterwards. The default is the game's translation of the text in the original language (a built-in text of the map in translations_get); without one, French or English, after the player's language.

Map (carte)¶

A minimap in a corner of the screen, the player in the middle as an arrow, and a big map of the whole scene opened with the control carte (M, or ↓ on a gamepad), zoomed with the mouse wheel or with the controls carte_rapprocher (Page Up, or the right trigger RT on a gamepad) and carte_eloigner (Page Down, or the left trigger LT), held, and moved by dragging with the mouse, with the gamepad's left stick or with the walking keys. While the map has the gamepad's focus the player does not move, so LT (also run) does not make them run. Add it with modules_add when the user asks for a map, a minimap, markers or places to discover: do not build a map yourself.

The picture is the scene seen from above, taken by the engine when the scene starts (north, -Z, at the top), without the player and characters. The game setting dessin can give a drawn map instead (an image file): by default it covers the same square as the engine's view (dessin_zone "auto"); with dessin_zone "manuelle", dessin_x, dessin_z (its north-west corner) and dessin_cote (its side, in metres) say where it lies.

Markers, without code: place the system on an object (systems_attach), at its position, or on a group or folder: one marker for everything inside, sub-folders included, in the middle of the box enclosing its pieces (the folder's own position does not matter), discovered when the player comes within distance of that box. A piece inside with its own values for carte (systems_set on it) becomes its own marker. Its object settings:

  • nom: the name shown under it on the big map; empty, the name of the object or group without a trailing number. Write it in the user's language.
  • forme: rond, losange, triangle, carre, etoile, or image with image (an icon file, find one with assets_search and kind "image"); couleur ("#rrggbb", tints an image); taille (1 usual, up to 3).
  • visible: toujours; decouvert, shown once the player came within distance metres, or when your script or a declencheur rule sends carte.reveler {cible = id} (the object or group, or any piece of the group); cache, shown only while it is a quest objective.
  • quete and quete_etape: the exact title of a quetes quest (and a step number, 0 for the whole quest) for which this marker is the objective, such as the miller for "Go back to the miller".

What marks itself (game settings marchands and personnages, on by default): objects carrying boutique (a golden diamond) and dialogue (a light blue circle), named after the shop or the character, one marker per object or group they are placed on. Placing carte on one of them chooses its look instead.

Quest objectives (game setting objectifs, couleur_objectif): while a quetes step is active, the markers it names are ringed in that color, and on the minimap they stay at its edge, pointing the way. A step names objects through fin_cible (the "Loup" of "Vaincre 3 loups": every marker on an object or group named "Loup", "Loup 2"...), or a marker names its quest with quete. The quest log is not changed: the map only listens to quetes.debut, quetes.etape and quetes.fin.

Other game settings: mini_carte (shown or not), mini_coin (bas_droite by default, bas_gauche, haut_droite, haut_gauche; the inventory and the current quest step are at the top right), mini_taille (share of the screen height), mini_forme (ronde or carree), mini_etendue (metres shown from edge to edge), mini_tourne (turns with the player, otherwise north up), grande_carte (the M window), pause_fenetre (the world stops while it is open), noms (names on the big map), annoncer (a message when a marker is discovered).

  • Events announced: carte.ouverte (ouvert), carte.decouvert (cible: the object or group the system is placed on, once for all its pieces). Listened: carte.reveler (cible).
  • The minimap hides during a pause (a menu, a dialogue, a shop) and while the big map is open.
  • Saved games keep the places already discovered, in every scene (on_save, on_load): a loaded game shows them again without announcing them.
  • Texts follow the player's language: the map's own texts are built-in texts of the system, listed by translations_get (French and English are built in). To change a word, override carte.texte from a game script: game.override("carte.texte", function(default, key) if key == "titre" then return "Atlas" end return default(key) end). Keys: titre, fermer, aide, decouvert (with {nom}), vous.
  • When the world changes a lot (a bridge built, a forest burnt), game.map_refresh() from a script takes the picture again.
  • HUD keys start with carte.: do not use that prefix in a game script.
  • Testing: in game_run, read the report's hud: carte.mini.r.<n> are the minimap markers, carte.mini.o.<n> the rings of objectives, carte.mini.joueur the arrow; press carte and read carte.g.n.<n> (the names on the big map) and carte.grande.image (its uv, the part of the map shown). wheel and mouse in a segment zoom and drag it, stick ([x, y], y down) moves it, actions carte_rapprocher or carte_eloigner held zoom it in or out. A discovery sends carte.decouvert, counted in the report's events.
  • Not yet: a fog of war (the parts of the map not yet walked hidden in black).
