Sons¶

Les bruits du jeu, posés sur ce qui les fait : pas des personnages (calés sur leur animation, et selon le sol), coups portés et reçus, morts, portes et coffres qu'on utilise, ambiance d'un feu ou d'une rivière, cri d'alerte d'un ennemi. Plus, pour tout le jeu : musique, musique de combat, ramassage. Plusieurs fichiers séparés par des virgules : un au hasard à chaque fois.

Événements¶

Les descriptions viennent du code et sont en anglais, la langue de travail de l'agent.

Ce qu'il écoute¶

Événement Champs Description
scene.change depuis (text), vers (text) Sent by the engine once the player has arrived in another scene of the game (game.go_to, or a passage). Scripts keep running across scenes: this is when a module looks at the objects of the new scene.
combat.attaque source (id) Someone struck, whether it hit or not: the stamina module counts it.
combat.coup cible (id), source (id), montant (number) An attack hit something. The health module, if present, has been asked to remove the damage.
sante.mort cible (id), source (id?) An object's health reached zero. What happens next belongs to the game: the health module does nothing else, unless its setting says to destroy the object.
interaction.utiliser cible (id), joueur (id) The player used the object in reach: a door, a merchant, an item to pick up. Whoever owns that object decides what it does.
collecte.coup cible (id), coups (number), restants (number) The player hit a resource (a use of something carrying collecte). restants is how many hits are left before it is used up.
collecte.epuise cible (id) A resource is used up and is about to disappear (destroyed, or hidden until it grows back): it still exists while this is sent.
inventaire.change objet (text), quantite (number), total (number), chargement (boolean?) The quantity of an item changed. total is the new quantity. chargement is true when a saved game gives the items back (game.load): nothing was picked up.
inventaire.plein objet (text) An item could not be added: every slot holds another kind of item.
ennemi.etat cible (id), etat (text) An enemy changed state: patrouille (patrols), poursuite (chases the player), attaque (hits in reach), attente (waits its turn near the player, when enough others are already striking), retour (goes back to its spot), mort (dead, does nothing more).
endurance.change valeur (number), max (number), epuise (boolean) The player's stamina changed. epuise is true from zero until they regain a share of it: meanwhile they cannot run, jump or strike.
sante.pare cible (id), source (id?), montant (number) A hit was blocked by a guard: montant is the damage it stopped. For a clang, sparks, a stamina cost.
soins.soin cible (id), montant (number), objet (text) The healing module gave health back: a potion drunk, a spring touched. objet is the kind of potion or the name of what healed.

Points qu'un script peut remplacer (game.override)¶

Les descriptions viennent du code et sont en anglais, la langue de travail de l'agent.

  • sons.pas : function(default, id, sol, fichiers) -> the files of this footstep (text, commas allowed), or "" for silence. sol: the object under the feet or nil; fichiers: what the module would play (the ground's surface, or the walker's steps).

Ce que l'agent sait de ce système¶

Le texte que l'agent lit avant de se servir de ce système (en anglais, la langue de travail de l'agent).

Sounds (sons)¶

The game's noises, placed on what makes them, like the other systems: on the player, on a folder of skeletons, on a door, on a campfire. Each object or folder has its own sounds. Only with sound files the user has: find them with assets_search and kind sound.

  • Placed on objects (systems_attach, then systems_set): pas (footsteps: for animated characters, each one falls when a foot touches the ground in the walk or run animation, at any speed; foulee in metres only serves objects without animation), surface (placed on a floor, a bridge, a carpet: whoever walks on it makes these steps instead of their own), saut and atterrissage (the player's jump and landing), attaque (the swing of its weapon, hit or miss), coup_recu, parade (a hit it blocked), mort, alerte (an enemy starts chasing), utiliser (a door, a chest, a lever used through the interaction module), ambiance (a loop coming from it: fire, river, forge; only the nearest play), plus volume, portee (beyond it, not heard), foulee, volume_pas.
  • For the whole game (modules_set): musique, musique_combat (while an enemy chases the player), volume_musique, ramassage, inventaire_plein, soin (a heal from the module soins), essoufflement, variation (a slightly different pitch each time, so twenty identical sounds do not sound mechanical).
  • Sound mixer groups: its noises (steps, hits, deaths, doors, gathering, heals) go to groupe_bruits (effects), the looping ambiences to groupe_ambiance (ambience), pickups and a full inventory to groupe_interface (ui), the music to music. On an object, groupe sends all its sounds to one group (voice for a talking character, ambience for a radio); empty: by kind of sound. Balance whole groups with mixer_set, not each object's volume.
  • Several files separated by commas give a random one each time: give footsteps and hits two to four files when the pack has them.
  • It only listens to what the other modules announce: hits and swings come from combat.basique and ennemis, deaths from sante, uses from interaction, pickups from inventaire. A sound the game needs elsewhere (a lever in a puzzle, a cutscene) is a game.sound in that script.
  • Different grounds: place the module on each kind of floor (or its folder) with surface: grass, stone, wood. The floor needs a collider. A character still needs the module on itself to make steps at all.
  • In game_run nothing is heard: the report's sounds lists what played.

Several players¶

One for the whole game, what the host hears: sounds placed somewhere (steps, hits, jumps of every player) are heard from everyone; sounds with no place (picking up, running out of breath) are the host's only. Guests do not hear the game's sounds yet.

Several players¶

Its sounds reach the guests: the sounds placed somewhere (steps, hits, jumps of every player) and the music are heard by everyone, each from their own place; a sound without a place for one player (picking up, getting out of breath) is heard by that player only.
