Mort et réapparition¶

Ce qui arrive au joueur à zéro point de vie : il tombe (une animation de son personnage, au choix), ne commande plus rien, voit un écran de mort, puis réapparaît (au départ, au dernier point de réapparition ou sur place) ou la partie se termine. Placé sur un objet, il en fait un point de réapparition. Demande la Santé.

Événements¶

Les descriptions viennent du code et sont en anglais, la langue de travail de l'agent.

Ce qu'il annonce¶

Événement Champs Description
joueur.mort joueur (id), cible (id), source (id?), position (vector) A player died (death and respawn module): they control nothing until they respawn. Other systems hook here: drop the bag, a grave, a counter.
joueur.reapparu joueur (id), cible (id), position (vector) A dead player stands up again (death and respawn module): respawned, or brought back by sante.ranimer.
mort.point joueur (id), cible (id) A player reached a respawn point (an object carrying the death and respawn system): they will respawn there.
sante.ranimer cible (id), part (number?), montant (number?), invulnerabilite (number?) Ask the health module to bring an object back to life, even dead: part (0 to 1 of its maximum, all by default) or montant (points), then invulnerabilite seconds without losing health. Sent by the death and respawn module.
fenetres.fermer joueur (id?), raison (text?) Close every window a player has open (loot, inventory, equipment, workshop...), sent by the Death module when they die; any script may send it (a cutscene). joueur: that player (missing: everyone). Each module with a window closes its own.

Ce qu'il écoute¶

Événement Champs Description
sante.mort cible (id), source (id?) An object's health reached zero. What happens next belongs to the game: the health module does nothing else, unless its setting says to destroy the object.
sante.change cible (id), vie (number), max (number) An object's health changed, or is announced once when the game starts.
mort.reapparaitre joueur (id) Ask the death and respawn module to respawn this dead player now (a button of the game's own, a teammate).
joueur.arrivee joueur (id), numero (number), nom (text) Sent by the engine, in a game with several players (the game setting Multiplayer), on the computer hosting the game, when a guest joins: joueur is their character (a copy of the player's), numero their place (1 to 7, the host being 0), nom the name they chose. game.players() lists them all. Scripts only run on the host.
joueur.depart joueur (id), numero (number) Sent by the engine on the host when a guest leaves the game (they quit, or their connection was lost and they did not come back in time, the game setting Place kept after a drop): their character is removed right after. A module forgets what it kept for them.
langue.change langue (text) Sent by the engine when the player changes the game's language (game.set_language, the Menu options). A module redraws what it already shows: its texts go through game.t and game.language.

Death and respawn (mort)¶

What happens to a player at zero health (sante.mort), chosen by the creator: they fall, control nothing, see a death screen, then respawn or the game ends. Needs sante on the player. Only add it if the user wants deaths handled (a game where the player can die). Never script a death or a respawn yourself when this module fits.

  • The fall: animation is a clip of the player's own character, picked from its clips (player_get lists them), whatever the pack; never assume a name. It plays once and the body stays on its last frame until respawn (the engine adds it to the player's animation graph as the state mort; a graph that already has a state mort keeps it). Empty (the default): the player just stops where they stand, standing; say so to the user and offer to pick a clip.
  • Controls: a dead player does not move, jump or act (only pause still reaches the scripts), whatever their machine sends: game.set_player_dead. The host decides; every player sees them dead.
  • After death (apres): bouton (a "Respawn" button after delai seconds), auto (respawn after delai seconds, counted on screen), or fin (a "Game over" screen; the game stops delai seconds after no player is left alive, with game.finish("lost")).
  • Where (lieu): point (the last respawn point reached, else where the player entered the game), depart, or sur_place. A respawn point is an object carrying this system (systems_attach, value rayon: how close the player must come); objects cloned from it during the game (a sleeping bag the player builds) are points too. Announced by mort.point (joueur, cible).
  • Health back: vie_mode part (vie in % of the maximum) or nombre (vie points), then invulnerabilite seconds without losing health. Done by sante.ranimer (cible, part or montant, invulnerabilite), which a script can also send to bring someone back where they are.
  • Screen: texte_mort, texte_fin (empty: "You died", "Game over" in the player's language), voile (how dark). What it shows is delivered as screens, which the user restyles in the screen editor: « Écran de mort » (Voile, Fenêtre, Titre, Compte: the seconds before an automatic respawn, Réapparaître: the button, action reapparaitre) and « Fin de partie » (Voile, Fenêtre, Titre). Never draw a death screen yourself, and do not name another screen like these. Other texts: override mort.texte.
  • Events: joueur.mort (joueur, cible, source, position) when a player dies, joueur.reapparu (joueur, cible, position) when they stand up again. Hook other systems on them: dropping the bag (loot), a grave, a death counter. Ask for a respawn from a script: game.emit("mort.reapparaitre", {joueur = id}). At death it also sends fenetres.fermer (joueur, raison mort): the windows of the modules (loot, inventory, equipment, workshop) close, so the death screen covers none; a game's own window script should listen to it too.
  • Save: a game saved while a player is dead comes back with them dead (lying, no controls, their screen), for the time left, then they respawn as set.
  • Dropping the bag: the butin module's setting mort_sac (off by default): all or part of the bag, in a bag left where they fell or on the ground, reserved for them for a while. Never script it yourself.
  • Losses: the XP lost at death is the progression module's (each track's mort, mort_part): it already listens to sante.mort, nothing to add here.
  • Enemies and other characters: unchanged; this module only handles players.

Several players¶

One for the whole game, on the host. A dead guest is seen dead and lying on every machine, then respawns everywhere; they cannot move by cheating, since the host moves their body. Each player sees their own death screen and button.
