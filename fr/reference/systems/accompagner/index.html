Accompagner¶

Le personnage qui porte ce système suit le joueur (ou un objet) à la distance réglée, rattrape en courant, va vers un lieu en attendant le joueur, fuit, ou attend sur place. Un compagnon, un animal, un membre de l'équipe, une escorte de quête, un otage, un fuyard. Il contourne les murs et marche avec ses propres animations.

accompagner.arrive cible (id), mode (text), lieu (id?), qui (id?) A Companion character arrived (Companion module): mode aller (it reached its place, lieu), suivre (it reached its destination lieu while following qui: the end of an escort) or fuir (it is far enough from what it fled, qui).

Points qu'un script peut remplacer (game.override)¶

Les descriptions viennent du code et sont en anglais, la langue de travail de l'agent.

  • accompagner.place : function(default, infos) -> the point (a vector) where a following character stands near the one it follows. infos: cible (the character), chef (the one followed), rang (its rank among the characters following that one, from 1), nombre (how many follow it), reglages (its settings). Ce que l'agent sait de ce système¶

Le texte que l'agent lit avant de se servir de ce système (en anglais, la langue de travail de l'agent).

Companion (accompagner)¶

Placed on a character (systems_attach): it follows the player or an object, walks to a place while waiting for the player, flees, or waits where it is. One system for every character who goes along: a pet, a team member, an escorted merchant, a hostage, a runaway. Use it whenever a character must follow, be escorted, lead the way or run away: never write that walking in a script. Never edit the system: set its values (systems_set), send its events, or override its hook with game.override.

How it walks¶

  • It walks with game.walk_to (the walkable areas, around walls, stepping aside from the other walkers) and plays its own idle, walk and run animations: animation_repos, animation_marche, animation_course (clip names of its model, read with animation_clips); empty, the clip whose name says idle, walk or run, if the model has one. Set them when the names say nothing. Never make up a pose.
  • Following: it stays at distance (2.5 m) behind the one it follows, walks at vitesse_marche, runs at vitesse_course when it is further than distance + rattrapage or when the leader runs, and stops close by. Several companions of the same player share the places without walking into each other: placement derriere (spread by ecart degrees) or cote (right, then left, then further back). A game rule of your own: override accompagner.place (returns the point; infos: cible, chef, rang, nombre, reglages).
  • Very far (teleport_distance, 50 m) or stuck with no way (teleport_bloque, 6 s), it reappears at its place near the leader (teleportation, on by default; untick it for an escort the player can abandon).
  • Vehicles: with vehicule monter (default) it gets in with the player it follows when they drive off, and gets out next to them. It sits at a free passenger seat of the car or motorbike (game.seat), visible, playing animation_assis (a clip of its model; empty: the looping clip whose name says sit or seat, not the one where it sits down or stands up). With no sitting clip, in a boat, with the vehicle's « conducteur » off or no free seat, it rides hidden: never a made-up pose. The seats are the vehicle's settings (passagers, passager_avant, passager_hauteur, passager_cote): move them there when it sinks in or floats, after a screenshot. a_pied: it stays outside and runs after them, or reappears near them.
  • Other scenes: autres_scenes compagnon (default: when suit_des_le_debut is on), oui (whenever it follows the player) or non. When the player it follows goes to another scene (game.go_to), it enters with them (game.bring_along) and reappears at its place near them, still following, with its orders. One it does not take along stays in its scene with its state, and is found there on coming back; its copy in a scene it has left is hidden. A scene already visited without it gets it too, with its children and its state; it is never there twice.
  • Dead (sante.mort on it), it does nothing until its health comes back.

Orders (events)¶

Send them from a quest step's annonce_debut, a declencheur rule (action envoyer: the rule's object is the cible, the player who triggered it the qui), a dialogue answer's annonce, or a script. cible is the character (id or name); without cible, every character carrying the system in the scene obeys (a step's annonce_debut sends none: fine with one companion in the scene; with several, put a declencheur rule on the character, quand evenement, action envoyer accompagner.suivre).

  • accompagner.suivre {cible, qui, lieu?}: follow qui (a player or an object; missing: the one it followed last, else the host). With lieu (or its setting destination), it announces accompagner.arrive (mode suivre) once it gets there.
  • accompagner.attendre {cible}: "Wait here"; accompagner.suivre without qui is "Follow me" again. Bind them to a key (a control and a script line if game.pressed("wait") then game.emit("accompagner.attendre", {cible = ...}) end) or to dialogue answers (annonce).
  • accompagner.aller {cible, lieu?, qui?}: walk to lieu (an object, its name or a point; missing: destination), stopping to wait, facing the player, while qui is further than attente (10 m); within rayon_arrivee it announces accompagner.arrive (mode aller) and waits there.
  • accompagner.fuir {cible, de?, distance?}: run away from de (missing: qui, else the nearest player) until distance (missing: distance_fuite); safe, it announces accompagner.arrive (mode fuir), and runs again if approached.
  • accompagner.arreter {cible}: stop and forget the leader.
  • suit_des_le_debut on: it follows the host from the start, with no event (a pet, a team member).
  • accompagner.perdu {cible, qui, mode, distance}: further than perdu_distance (30 m) from the one it follows or waits for during perdu_apres seconds (10; 0 never). Once, until it is close again.

Recipes¶

  • An escort quest: on the merchant, the system with teleportation off and destination the village. The step {"sorte": "etape", "texte": "Escort the merchant to the village", "fin": "evenement", "fin_evenement": "accompagner.arrive", "fin_cible": "Merchant", "annonce_debut": "accompagner.suivre"} (or fin lieu on the village: then only the player needs to get there), under it {"sorte": "echec", "echec": "eloigne", "echec_lieu": "<merchant id>", "echec_rayon": 25} (abandoned) and {"sorte": "echec", "echec": "evenement", "echec_evenement": "sante.mort", "echec_cible": "Merchant"} (killed); the merchant carries sante. Keep echec_rayon below teleport_distance if teleportation stays on. Everything else is the Quest log's (resume, try again).
  • A guide who leads the way: annonce_debut accompagner.aller, the step fin evenement accompagner.arrive with fin_cible the guide.
  • A pet: suit_des_le_debut on, placement cote, its own clips; "Sit" and "Come" as accompagner.attendre and accompagner.suivre on two controls or dialogue answers.
  • A runaway thief: a declencheur rule quand entree on a zone around him, action envoyer accompagner.fuir; a quest step sante.mort or dialogue.fin on him.
  • Fighting at the player's side is not part of this system: a companion does not attack. (Planned: the ennemis system as an ally.)

Several players, saves, tests¶

  • One copy for the whole game: each character follows the player it is told (qui: the Trigger and quests give the player who caused it), the host otherwise.
  • Saves keep who follows whom, who waits, where it goes or what it flees, and in which scene it is; the engine keeps its position. Nothing to write.
  • Test with game_run: move the player, then read the character's position (game.position) and log accompagner.arrive, accompagner.perdu and the quetes.* events.
