Véhicule¶

Fait d'un modèle une voiture, une moto ou un bateau qu'on conduit. Une voiture : ses roues (choisies parmi ses enfants, trouvées par leur nom, ou détachées d'un modèle d'un seul tenant) tournent, braquent et suivent la suspension. Une moto : elle tient debout, penche dans les virages et peut tomber. Un bateau : sa coque flotte, mesurée sur son modèle, un moteur la pousse et un gouvernail la tourne ; il s'échoue sur la terre. Le joueur monte et descend avec la commande Utiliser, conduit avec les commandes du jeu, au clavier ou à la manette, et la caméra le suit.

Vehicle (vehicule)¶

Turns a model into a car, a motorbike or a boat the player drives: wheels with suspension, engine, brakes and steering. The engine runs it, without a script. The player gets in and out with the interact control (E, Y on a gamepad) next to it, drives with the movement controls (forward and back accelerate or brake then reverse, left and right steer), jump is the hand brake; the camera follows behind it.

  • systems_attach it on the car object itself (or on the folder that holds the body and the wheels), placed on the ground, not on each part.
  • Wheels: the car's children (any depth) whose name contains roue, wheel, tire, tyre or pneu are found by themselves; otherwise list them in roues ([{"objet": "<wheel id>"}, ...]). Their size and place come from their models. Never assume a pack's names: look at the children (entities_list) and list them if their names say nothing.
  • A model made in one piece (no wheel among its children): with « roues_du_modele » on (the default), the engine looks for the wheels inside the model itself, the round pieces at its bottom seen from the side; in the game they come off the body and turn like real wheels. The editor circles them when the vehicle is selected. When none is found (the wheels are welded to the body), invisible wheels carry it: four at a car's corners, two in the middle of a motorbike, and the model's own wheels do not turn.
  • avant: which side of the model is the front (+z most models, -z, +x, -x): the wheels on that side steer. Check it in a screenshot: an arrow shows it when the vehicle is selected.
  • Per object: masse (kg, car 1200, kart 150, motorbike 200), puissance (engine torque N·m: car 600, kart or motorbike 150, truck 3000), vitesse_max (km/h), adherence (1 dry road, 0.4 snow), suspension (travel in m), raideur (1.5 soft, 3 firm), braquage (degrees), freins. Game-wide: distance to get in.
  • A motorbike: set « sorte » to « moto ». Two wheels one behind the other (found or listed like a car's, the front one steers, the rear one drives). It stands upright by itself, parked or ridden, and leans into turns. Per object: « inclinaison » (the most it leans in a turn, degrees: 45 road bike, 55 racing, 25 scooter), « equilibre » (how hard it straightens, 1 ordinary), « maniabilite » (how fast the handlebars turn, 1 ordinary), « choc » (a sideways hit faster than this, in m/s, unbalances it for a moment: 4 ordinary, 0 never), « chute » (can fall over, on by default). Knocked over (a crash, a hard hit), it falls on its side and its rider is thrown off (a rider with the chiffon system falls limp); getting back on with interact or game.drive sets it upright. Set « masse » and « puissance » for a bike: the defaults are a car's.
  • Driving feel: « aide » (driving assist, 0 to 1, default 1) makes a car or a motorbike arcade-friendly, as in GTA or Forza Horizon: it steers less at speed (full lock standing, a third at 80 km/h), the steering moves smoothly even on the keyboard, and the vehicle follows where its wheels point, within its grip, instead of spinning out or sliding sideways (the hand brake still drifts). 0 is physics alone, like a simulation. When the user says a vehicle is hard to drive, check « aide » is 1 before touching anything else; a car that turns too little at speed: raise « braquage »; a motorbike that leans too little: raise « inclinaison ».
  • The driver stays visible (« conducteur », on by default): seated astride the motorbike, hands on the handlebars, leaning with it, or in the car's driver seat (left side), hands on the wheel, seen through the windows. The seat is measured on the model (a motorbike: the top of the model between the wheels; a car: driver side, a bit over a third of its height); « siege_avant », « siege_hauteur », « siege_cote » (meters) move it when the rider sinks into the saddle or floats above it: check a screenshot while driving. « animation_conduite »: a clip of the player's character looped while driving; empty, a clip whose name says drive, ride, sit or seat is used if the model has one (read animation_clips), otherwise a seated pose is made from the character's own. « conducteur » off hides the driver while driving (tinted windows). A boat's driver stays hidden.
  • Copies: game.clone(id, pos) of a vehicle gives another vehicle you can drive, with its wheels; a vehicle hidden then shown again (game.set_visible) is a vehicle again.
  • Scripts: game.drive(id) puts the player in, game.leave_vehicle(), game.driving() (the vehicle driven or nil), game.vehicle(id) (speed in km/h, driven, flipped, motorbike, fallen, lean in degrees). Events vehicule.monte and vehicule.descendu (cible, qui): a race starts when the player gets in; vehicule.tombe (cible, qui if someone rode it) when a motorbike falls over.
  • A zone detects the driven vehicle as the player: a finish line works as for a runner.
  • Check with game_run and inputs: press interact next to it, then hold move_forward; vehicles reports « kind » (car, motorbike, boat), top_speed_kmh, travelled_m, flipped (it turned over during the run) and driven_s, wheels, invisible_wheels and « model_wheels » (taken out of a one-piece model). A motorbike adds « max_lean_deg » (how far it leaned), « falls » and « fallen_now ». A vehicle that does not move: check its wheels are found and that it stands on the ground. A motorbike that falls at once: lower « maniabilite » or raise « equilibre ».
  • A boat: set « sorte » to « bateau » and place it on the water (a lake or the sea, its bottom at the surface). No wheels: its hull is measured on its model (length, width slice by slice, bottom), how deep it sits comes from masse, its center of gravity is at the waterline so a mast does not capsize it. Per object: masse (motorboat 1200, rowing boat 150), « poussee » (engine thrust in N: 4000 for a 1200 kg motorboat, 800 for a rowing boat), « vitesse_max » (km/h, it levels off there at full thrust), « virage » (degrees per second at full rudder, once moving: 40 motorboat, 15 big boat). Driven like a car; the engine only pushes in water, so it runs aground on land and stays there. It rocks with the waves and drifts with moving water.
  • For a boat, game_run's vehicles adds « kind » « boat », « afloat_s », « aground_now », « aground_s », « draft_m » (how deep its hull sits) and « float_points » (where the water carries it, measured on the model; 4 or more). game.vehicle(id) has boat and aground. A boat that does not move: check it is on the water (« afloat_s » above 0) and not « aground_now ».

Several players¶

The engine runs it once for the whole game: any player gets in and out with interact, drives with their own controls, and nobody gets into a vehicle another player drives (vehicule.monte and vehicule.descendu carry joueur). Every player sees the others seated on the vehicles they drive.
