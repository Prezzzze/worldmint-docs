Articulation¶

Relie cet objet à un autre, ou au monde : une porte sur ses gonds, un pont-levis, une roue, un tiroir, un ascenseur, un lustre, une planche qui cède sous un poids, une corde, un ressort. Avec des butées, un moteur qui l'ouvre et le ferme, une force de rupture. L'objet porte aussi Physique.

Joint (articulation)¶

Links an object to another one, or to the world, with a real physics joint: a door on its hinges, a drawbridge, a wheel on a cart, a drawer, an elevator, a chandelier, a pendulum, a plank that gives way, a rope, a spring. The engine runs it, without a script. The object also carries physique (added with it): give it a masse.

  • systems_attach it on the moving part (the door, not the wall), then systems_set:
  • type: charniere (turns around an axis), glissiere (slides along an axis), boule (turns every way around a point), fixe (holds until it breaks), corde (never further than a length), ressort (comes back to its length).
  • autre: the object that holds it (the cart of a wheel, the furniture of a drawer); empty: the world, where it stands.
  • pivot: where it is held on its model's box: centre, gauche (−X), droite (+X), bas, haut, arriere (−Z), avant (+Z). No coordinates: a door is held on one side, a drawbridge at the bottom, a chandelier at the top, a wheel in the middle.
  • axe (charniere, glissiere): vertical (a door, an elevator), cote (X: a drawbridge, a wheel), avant (Z: a drawer).
  • Stops: angle_min (−180 to 0) and angle_max (0 to 180) in degrees for a hinge, from where it starts (−180 and 180: it turns endlessly); angle_max is the swing cone of a boule (180: free); course_min / course_max in meters for a slider. longueur for a rope or spring (0: the starting distance; attached to the world, it hangs from that far above the pivot). rigidite: 0 hard stops, above: springy (a spring's stiffness).
  • Motor (charniere, glissiere): moteur aucun (moves when pushed), position (holds closed, goes to its far stop when opened), vitesse (turns or slides all the time at vitesse, degrees or meters per second); force its strength (500 for a door, 20000 for an elevator that carries the player); ouvert starts it open.
  • rupture: the force in newtons beyond which it breaks (800: a rotten plank the player breaks by landing on it; 0: never).
  • Open and close without code: a declencheur rule with action ouvrir or fermer and cible the door (a lever with interaction, a zone in front of the door). Action casser breaks it. A rule quand: casse reacts when it breaks.
  • Scripts: game.joint_open(id), game.joint_close(id), game.joint_break(id), game.joint(id) (kind, position: degrees or meters, broken). The engine announces articulation.cassee (cible, autre).
  • Examples: a door {"type": "charniere", "pivot": "gauche", "axe": "vertical", "angle_max": 100, "moteur": "position", "force": 800} with masse 30; a drawbridge {"type": "charniere", "pivot": "bas", "axe": "cote", "angle_min": -90, "angle_max": 0, "moteur": "position", "ouvert": true}; a chandelier {"type": "boule", "pivot": "haut", "angle_max": 30}; an elevator {"type": "glissiere", "axe": "vertical", "course_max": 5, "moteur": "position", "force": 20000} with masse 200.
  • Check with game_run: joints gives each one's angle or position now and the range it reached, and whether and when it broke.

Several players¶

The engine runs it once for the whole game: joints are shared.
