Butin¶

Des tables de butin, tirées quand un ennemi meurt, quand un coffre s'ouvre, à la récolte, à la fin d'une quête ou par le Déclencheur : ce qui tombe, avec sa rareté (couleur, lueur), ses bonus au hasard et son nom généré pour les exemplaires uniques. Au sol, dans l'inventaire, ou dans une fenêtre « Tout prendre ».
Identifiant butin
Version 6
Se pose sur des objets oui
Active aussi Inventaire

Nom Le nom de la table, comme « Loup » ou « Coffre de la grotte ». nom Texte (vide)
Tirages Combien de fois la table tire une ligne (au poids) à chaque fois qu'elle sert. tirages Nombre 1 de 0 à 100, en nombre entier

Événements¶

Les descriptions viennent du code et sont en anglais, la langue de travail de l'agent.

Ce qu'il annonce¶

Événement Champs Description
butin.tombe table (text), nombre (number), cible (text?), joueur (id?) Loot was rolled and placed (on the ground, in the inventory or in a content): how many lines dropped.
butin.ramasse objet (text), quantite (number), fiche (number?), joueur (id) A player took a piece of loot (from the ground or a Take all window). fiche: the unique item's record, if it is one.
butin.refus cible (text), joueur (id), raison (text) The host refused a player taking loot reserved for someone else (sharing personal, killer or in turn).
butin.ouvrir cible (text), joueur (id?) Open the Take all window of a content (a body, a chest) for a player.
butin.contenu cible (text), nombre (number) A content (a body, a chest) changed: its Take all windows redraw.
butin.prendre cible (text), numero (number?), joueur (id) A player asks the host to take from a content: one line (numero) or all they may take.
inventaire.ajouter objet (text), quantite (number), joueur (id?), fiche (number?) Ask the inventory to add items, such as a reward. Answered by inventaire.change, or inventaire.plein. With several players, joueur says whose bag; without it, the player whose action is being handled, or every player.
inventaire.retirer objet (text), quantite (number), fiche (number?) Ask the inventory to remove items, such as a payment. Answered by inventaire.change, or inventaire.manque if there are not enough. For a kind with unique copies (version 15): fiche removes that copy, otherwise the first ones; their records are destroyed.
inventaire.sortir objet (text), fiche (number?), case (text?), joueur (id?) Ask the inventory to take one item out of the bag without destroying it (version 16): objet is its kind, fiche a unique copy (it keeps its record; whoever takes it says its new holder). The Equipment module takes what it makes the player wear. A stack gives one from its smallest stack, or from case (version 21), a slot's key.
butin.sac joueur (id), cible (id?), nombre (number), position (vector) A dead player dropped their bag (Loot setting mort_sac): cible is the bag left where they fell (nil: each item on the ground), nombre the lines dropped.

Ce qu'il écoute¶

Événement Champs Description
sante.mort cible (id), source (id?) An object's health reached zero. What happens next belongs to the game: the health module does nothing else, unless its setting says to destroy the object.
progression.niveau_ennemi cible (id), niveau (number) The Progression module gave an enemy its level (fixed, per zone, matched to the players or growing with time): its health and damage grew with it. To show it above its head.
collecte.recolte cible (id), ressource (text), quantite (number), joueur (id?), xp (number?), piste (text?) A resource was used up and gave what it holds. The inventory, if present, has been asked to add it.
quetes.debut quete (text), groupe (boolean?) A quest started: quete is its title. groupe (Journal de quêtes 8): true for a quest of the whole group (game setting groupe), sent once for the group, joueur being who moved it on.
quetes.fin quete (text), xp (number?), piste (text?), groupe (boolean?) A quest is finished: all its steps are done. Its reward, if any, has been asked to the inventory. xp and piste (version 28): its XP for the Progression module and the track that gets it (empty: the main one). groupe (Journal de quêtes 8): true for a quest of the whole group (game setting groupe), sent once for the group, joueur being who moved it on.
quetes.abandon quete (text), groupe (boolean?) The player abandoned a quest from the log (or quetes.abandonner asked for it). It gives no reward; it may be given again if its settings allow it. groupe (Journal de quêtes 8): true for a quest of the whole group (game setting groupe), sent once for the group, joueur being who moved it on.

Loot (butin)¶

Loot tables rolled by the host (ADR-039 § 8 to § 10). It needs the inventory. Never edit its scripts: change its settings, listen to its events, or use game.override from a script of the game.

  • Tables (tables: nom, tirages, tirages_max, rien from 0 to 1) and their rows (lignes: table, objet or sous_table, poids, or pourcentage tried apart on each roll, quantite, quantite_max, garanti once per table, conditions niveau_min, niveau_max, joueur_min, joueur_max, zone, quete). A loop between tables (a -> b -> a) is refused: nothing drops and the reason goes to the log.
  • Rarities (raretes, from the most common to the rarest; five by default, Commun to Légendaire): nom, couleur (the item's name, its glow on the ground), chance (a weight), bonus (how many random bonuses), multiplicateur, chance_gemmes (tried gemmes_max times), son, lueur. Only kinds with uniques ticked in the inventory's sortes_objets get a rarity, bonuses and a generated name; a stack stays a stack.
  • Random bonuses (bonus_hasard): valeur (a Progression value: degats, sante_max...), min, max (at item level 1), pourcent, categories (comma separated, the kind's categorie), rarete_min, nom (a piece of name) and place (apres, avant). The item level is the enemy's (event progression.niveau_ennemi) or the object setting niveau; ranges grow with it along croissance_forme and croissance_taux (the Progression's growth curve).
  • Sources: an object carrying this system with table (an enemy) drops it on sante.mort; collecte rows (a resource, a table, a chance); quetes rows (a quest, a table); butin.lacher {table, cible or position, niveau, joueur} (the Trigger's action « Lâcher un butin », a script); chests (module coffres, through butin.remplir).
  • Appearance (apparition): sol: a copy of the kind's model (modeles, else the inventory's en_main model of that kind, else modele_defaut, all set by the creator; nothing is assumed of a pack; with none, forme_sans_modele: a small engine cube or sphere in its rarity color, taille_forme metres wide (0.15, measured on the shape), set on the ground; or into the inventory), glowing in its rarity color in the world, hidden by what passes in front, by day and night, with no asset (lueur; style_lueur: faisceau, a column of light rising from it, or etincelles, sparkles around it; taille_lueur, 1 for a beam about 1.5 m high; light enough for 50 items on the ground), a floating name (nom_flottant, distance_nom), gone after disparition seconds (once empty, it shows « Plus rien. » for 1.5 s and closes by itself; fenetres.fermer closes it); a model must not carry the « inventaire » system itself; inventaire; fenetre: the body becomes usable and opens the « Tout prendre » window, delivered as a screen the user restyles in the screen editor: « Butin » (Fenêtre, Titre, Vide, the list Objets: one row per item, Image and the button Objet, Objet#2 the second; Tout prendre, Fermer; actions prendre, tout, fermer); a refusal shows « Message du butin » (Message). Never draw these windows yourself, and do not name another screen like these. The floating names follow the world and stay HUD (butin.nom.<object>). With the Inventory's disposition_fenetre trois_parties (version 3), that window stays closed: the content shows in the inventory's right column, and a bag slot dragged onto a chest's content is put in it with butin.ranger {cible, objet, fiche?, quantite, joueur?} (chests only, coffre/..., with their sharing rules; checked by the host; without joueur, the host's player).
  • Dropping the bag on death (mort_sac, off by default: rien): with the module mort (its event joueur.mort), tout or part (mort_part %, of each stack and of the unique items, the first in the bag) of the dead player's bag leaves it (inventaire.retirer, inventaire.sortir: a unique item keeps its record), left where they fell: mort_forme sac (a bag to open with « Tout prendre », a copy of mort_modele_sac, an object chosen by the creator, else a small engine cube; it disappears when empty) or sol (each item on the ground, like loot). Reserved for them mort_reserve seconds (0: anyone), then anyone. Announced by butin.sac {joueur, cible, nombre, position}. Nothing is dropped when the Inventory's bag is shared by the group. Saved with the game (the bag comes back where it was).
  • Luck: the value chance_butin (base chance_base) multiplies the chance of every rarity but the first and divides the chance of nothing. protection_active, protection_rang, protection_hausse: the bad-luck protection, counted per player on the host.
  • Online: an item set on the ground is seen by every guest, a copy of its model or the engine shape in its rarity color, at the same place and size (protocol 25), and leaves when picked up.
  • Several players (partage): libre, personnel (one roll per player, never on the ground where others would see it: in that player's own « Tout prendre » window on the body, or straight into their bag; the records of one player's share are never sent to the others' machines), tueur (delai_tueur seconds), tour. Only the host rolls and checks each pickup; a refusal sends butin.refus (raison reserve) and shows a message to that player.
  • A full bag never loses an item: what does not fit stays on the ground or in the window (butin.refus with raison plein, and a message); given straight to the bag, it drops at the player's feet.
  • Conditions receive the player's zone (zone.entree: the zone's name without its number), their quests in progress (quetes.debut until quetes.fin, quetes.abandon, quetes.echec) and their level; a zone object carrying this system with niveau gives the item level of what drops while the player is in it (when the source has no level).
  • Save: loot on the ground (when garder_au_sol, with its time left; a unique item keeps its record), the contents of bodies and chests (a body opens its window again after a load; identical stacks are merged), and each player's bad-luck count.
  • Events: emits butin.tombe, butin.ramasse, butin.refus, butin.ouvrir, butin.contenu, butin.sac, inventaire.ajouter, inventaire.retirer, inventaire.sortir; listens to butin.lacher, butin.remplir, butin.prendre, sante.mort, joueur.mort, collecte.recolte, quetes.fin, progression.niveau_ennemi, interaction.utiliser.
  • Scripts: game.loot_roll(table, {niveau, niveau_joueur, zone, quetes, chance, joueur}) returns the list rolled without creating anything; game.loot_simulate(table, n) rolls n times (the editor's simulator). Under the module's settings, the editor offers the genre presets (Survie, Jeu de rôle, Tir) and « Tirer cette table N fois ».
