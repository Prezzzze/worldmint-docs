Physique¶

Les objets qui le portent tombent, roulent, glissent, et le joueur les pousse en marchant dedans : des caisses, des tonneaux, un ballon, des rochers. Ceux qui viennent sur lui le poussent aussi, selon leur masse : un rocher le bouscule, un ballon rebondit. Dans l'eau, ils flottent, coulent ou restent entre deux eaux, et dérivent avec elle. Leur forme entoure le modèle, ou suit sa silhouette. Posé sur un dossier, il vaut pour tout ce qu'il contient.

Physics (physique)¶

The objects carrying this system are simulated by the engine: they fall, roll, slide, bounce, and the player and characters push them by walking into them. Crates, barrels, balls, rocks, a cart. The system has no script: the engine does it all.

  • Make objects movable: modules_add it once, then systems_attach on each object, or on a group so that all its children move. An object carrying it does not need a collider: it collides by itself.
  • Per object (systems_set): forme, masse in kg (a crate 10 to 30, a barrel 40, a rock 200: the heavier, the harder to push), frottement (0 slides as on ice, 0.6 by default), rebond (0 to 1).
  • forme: boite (a crate, furniture: slides and tips over), boule (a ball, a round rock: rolls every way), cylindre (a barrel, a column: rolls on its side), capsule (a cylinder with rounded ends: a bottle, a log, a dummy; rolls on its side and tips over gently), all four at the size of the model; or enveloppe, the model's own outline without its hollows (a jagged rock that rolls and catches on its edges, a statue standing on its base, an L-shaped piece of furniture). enveloppe costs a little more: use it when the shape changes how the object moves, not for crates and balls. A flat model (a sign, a sheet) has no outline and becomes a boite.
  • A moving object hits the player and the characters as two bodies do, by their masses (a character weighs 70 kg): a 200 kg rock rolling down a slope knocks the player back, a 40 kg barrel shoves them a little, a light ball bounces off them. It never goes through them. To make a rolling trap dangerous, give it mass and speed (game.push); add damage with a zone on its path or a script, not by expecting the shove to hurt.
  • In water (a lake, the sea): « eau » says what it does. « flotte » (the default: half sunk, a crate, a barrel, a ball), « entre_deux » (stays at the depth where it is let go), « coule » (sinks slowly to the bottom: a stone, an anchor), or « densite » with densite in kg per m³ (below 1000 it floats, higher the lighter: cork 200, wood 500, ice 900; above, it sinks: stone 2500). « freinage_eau » (1 ordinary, 0 glides, 3 sticks to the water). The water slows it, rocks it with the waves and carries it when it moves; its pieces, if it breaks, float or sink like it.
  • For the whole game: force_du_joueur, the force in newtons with which the player and characters push (300 by default; 0: they never push anything).
  • Place them resting on the ground or on each other (entities_ground): one placed in the air falls when the game starts.
  • Scripts move them with the physics: game.push(id, vec(...)) throws one (a change of speed in m/s), game.velocity(id) reads its speed, game.set_position puts one back at rest. One that falls out of the world disappears.
  • To know when something reaches a place (a ball in a goal, a crate on a plate), use the zone system.
  • game_run reports moving_objects: how far each one travelled, and whether it fell out of the world. Check there that what should move did. Its « water » part lists each object that touched the water: « state » at the end (« floating », « underwater », « out_of_water »), « submerged_pct », « below_surface_m », « in_water_s » and « drift_m » (how far the water carried it); « floating_debris » counts broken pieces that floated.

Several players¶

The engine runs it once for the whole game: moving objects are shared and seen by every player.
