Volume de brouillard¶

Un brouillard local, limité à une zone : la brume d'une vallée, d'un sous-bois, d'un marais, la fumée d'une grotte. Créer > Volume de brouillard pose une boîte invisible qui le porte : il remplit la boîte (ou la sphère qu'elle contient) et s'efface vers ses bords. Avec le brouillard volumétrique (qualité Haute ou Moyenne), il prend les rayons et les ombres du soleil. Sa sorte « Air lumineux (rayons seulement) » (Créer > Air lumineux) est un air presque invisible qui ne montre que les rayons du soleil, comme la poussière d'une forêt.

Géré par le moteur oui : le moteur le fait vivre lui-même, sans script

Fog volume (brouillard)¶

An object carrying this system fills its box with a local fog: the mist of a valley or an undergrowth, a marsh, the smoke of a cave, a cloud of dust. The scene fog (world_set fogDistance) covers the whole map; a fog volume stays where you put it. The system has no script: the renderer draws it. Several volumes can overlap: their fog adds up.

  • Make one: create a box (builtin:cube) with visible: false and no collider, then systems_attach this system on it. Scale the box to the area (20 × 6 × 30 for a valley floor, 4 × 3 × 4 for a room): the fog fills it, or the ball inside it (forme "sphere"). Place it so that its bottom is a little under the ground.
  • Per object (systems_set): forme (boite, sphere), distance (m: at this distance inside the fog, 95% is hidden; 5 to 15 for a thick mist, 40 to 100 for a haze; the box must be larger than this distance to hide everything behind it), couleur ("#rrggbb", its color in full light), bords (0 to 1: how much of the box, from its edges, fades out), degrade (0 to 1: 1 is thick at the bottom and nothing at the top, a mist that stays on the ground).
  • With volumetric fog on (world_set volumetricFog true, and High or Medium quality), the volume catches the sun's rays and the shadows of the trees: a fog volume in a forest shows sun shafts there only, without fogging the rest of the map. Raise volumetricAir for brighter shafts.
  • Two kinds (sorte): brouillard (the default, a visible fog) and air_lumineux ("Glowing air (rays only)"), an almost invisible air that only shows the sun's rays where they cross it, like dust in a forest. Pick by what the user describes: "sun rays in the forest", "light shafts", "god rays through the trees or a window", "dust in the light" -> air_lumineux; "mist in the valley", "a foggy marsh", "smoke in a cave", "the forest is misty" -> brouillard. The air has its own settings: distance_air (m, 200 by default; 100 for strong rays, 400 for a light trace) and bords_air (1 by default, keep it); distance, couleur and bords do not apply to it (it has no colour: it takes the sun's). It needs volumetric fog on (world_set volumetricFog true; turn it on if it is off) and a sun low enough to pass between trees or walls; on Low quality it does not show at all. Make its box large and tall enough to hold the rays from the treetops to the ground (30 × 12 × 30 for a clearing). Keep fogDistance 0 if the user wants no mist.
  • In Low quality (and on a small graphics card), it is still drawn, without shadows or rays. Far from the camera it is also drawn without shadows: it never stops at a distance.
  • Do not make its box visible: that would show the box. It moves with its object (a cloud that drifts, smoke that follows a fire).

Several players¶

The engine draws it once for the whole game: every player sees it.
