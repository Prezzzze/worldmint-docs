Poupée de chiffon¶

Un personnage animé qui tombe inerte, comme une poupée de chiffon : à sa mort, sous un choc fort, ou quand le Déclencheur le demande. Son corps est construit seul, depuis son squelette. Il peut se relever, et son animation reprend en douceur.
Identifiant chiffon
Version 2
Se pose sur des objets oui

Réglages pour tout le jeu¶

Dans l'onglet Jeu, section Systèmes, en ouvrant la carte du système.

Aucun.

Dans les Propriétés d'un objet ou d'un dossier qui porte le système, section Systèmes. Un dossier transmet ses valeurs à ce qu'il contient ; un objet peut changer une valeur pour lui seul.

Ragdoll (chiffon)¶

An animated character falls limp like a rag doll, then can get back up and its animation resumes smoothly. Its body is built by the engine from its skeleton (capsules measured on its bones, joints with reasonable limits): nothing to set per model, any skeleton works.

  • systems_attach it on the characters (the player, enemies, villagers) or their folder. Per object: a_la_mort (falls when its sante reaches zero, and stays down), choc (a hit from a moving object faster than this, in m/s, knocks it down; 0 never), se_relever and delai (after a hit, it gets back up once still for that many seconds), masse (kg), raideur (how far each limb bends, in degrees: 45 natural, 20 stiff).
  • In water, the fallen body floats, head and shoulders just above the surface, and drifts; set « flotte » to false to make it sink (a knight in armor). The game_run « water » part lists it.
  • The character must be animated (a model with bones and animations): a static model cannot fall as a ragdoll.
  • Without code: a declencheur rule with action chiffon (falls) or relever (gets up), cible the character.
  • Scripts: game.ragdoll(id, true) makes it fall (an optional third argument, a vec, throws it), game.ragdoll(id, false) gets it back up, game.ragdolled(id) tells if it is down. Events chiffon.tombe and chiffon.releve (cible).
  • While down, the player does not move and the camera follows the body; a character is not walked by its scripts.
  • Check with game_run: ragdolls gives each one's falls, get_ups, and whether it is down at the end.

Several players¶

One for the whole game: any character carrying it falls on death, guests' characters included (on the host; guests see their place, not the limp pose).
