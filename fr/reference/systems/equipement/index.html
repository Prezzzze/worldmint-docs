Équipement¶

Le joueur porte des objets dans des emplacements (tête, torse, arme, anneaux...) que vous inventez : leurs bonus s'ajoutent à ses caractéristiques, et ils peuvent se voir sur le personnage. Il porte en double-cliquant un objet de l'inventaire ou en le glissant sur un emplacement.
Identifiant equipement
Version 8
Se pose sur des objets non
Active aussi Inventaire

Ce que l'agent sait de ce système¶

Le texte que l'agent lit avant de se servir de ce système (en anglais, la langue de travail de l'agent).

Equipment (equipement)¶

The player wears items in slots (head, chest, weapon, two rings...): their bonuses add to the player's values, and they can show on the character. It requires the Inventory: the items worn come from the bag and go back to it. Prefer it to your own script; adapt it through its settings, its events and game.override, never by editing it.

  • Slots: the list emplacements (a row per slot: nom, icone, categories (the Inventory's categories it accepts, separated by commas), nombre (2 for two rings), visible, partie (the body part where what shows is attached: its bone is found by measuring the character's skeleton, game.body_bone), arme (holds the weapon in hand)). Empty: the preset prereglage (jeu_de_role: head, chest, hands, legs, feet, weapon, off hand, two rings, amulet, back; survie: head, chest, legs, feet, bag, tool; tir: helmet, vest, main weapon, secondary weapon). A slot's key in scripts is its name in lowercase without accents (« Main secondaire » -> main_secondaire, two rings -> anneau_1, anneau_2).
  • Wearable kinds: give the kind a categorie in the Inventory's sortes_objets (« casque »): it then goes to a slot accepting it. A row in sortes_equipables adds: emplacement (a precise slot), deux_emplacements with autre_emplacement (a two-handed weapon takes « Arme » and « Main secondaire »), conditions (niveau_requis on piste, carac_requise with carac_min; the Progression's required levels apply too), effet (with the Temporary effects module: set while worn, source equipement), and what shows (below). Fixed bonuses: bonus_sortes (a row per kind and value: valeur, add, pct in %), added to the record's bonuses of a unique copy.
  • Wearing: a double click on an inventory slot (A twice on a gamepad), or dragging it onto a slot of the equipment window (control equipment, O; it also opens beside the inventory, avec_inventaire; with the Inventory's disposition_fenetre trois_parties, the slots are drawn in the inventory's left column instead, through shared.equipement.places() and shared.equipement.placesPour(kind), version 6). A click on a worn slot takes it off. Scripts: game.emit("equipement.porter", {objet = "Casque", fiche = 12, emplacement = "tete", joueur = id}) (fiche for a unique copy, emplacement optional) and game.emit("equipement.retirer", {emplacement = "tete", joueur = id}). Read what is worn: shared.equipement.porte(player); what shows for a kind on the player's body: shared.equipement.apparence(kind) -> {[slot key] = {objet, fiche, nom, double?}} (double: the second slot of a two-slot item, naming the main one).
  • What happens: the item leaves the bag (inventaire.sortir); a unique copy's record gets the holder equipement:<player entity>, and inventaire:<player entity> again when taken off. Bonuses go through game.stat_bonus, source equipement:<slot key>, and are removed exactly. Events equipement.porte and equipement.retire {joueur, emplacement, objet, fiche}; equipement.refus {joueur, raison}. The hook equipement.avant refuses: game.override("equipement.avant", function(default, infos) if infos.objet == "Armure lourde" then return false, "Pas pour un mage" end return default(infos) end) (infos: joueur, emplacement, objet, fiche, second).
  • Effective bonuses: one function, shared.equipement.bonusEffectifs(record, kind) -> {{valeur, add, pct}}: the record's bonuses plus the kind's, raised by its upgrade level, plus its gems; a broken record (usure_max > 0 and usure == 0) gives nothing. When a worn record changes (objet.change), its bonuses are computed again.
  • What shows (visible on the kind's row): modele (a copy of an object attached to a bone: best, set the model on the character in the editor — the helmet on the head —, check it with a capture, hide it, and choose it in modele: its exact place is reused; os (a bone chosen in the character's skeleton; empty: the slot's measured body part), the offsets and echelle_x/y/z (sizes fitted to the measured head or body) override it), pieces (objects of the character shown or hidden while worn: pieces_montrer, pieces_cacher, names separated by commas, chosen in the model), les_deux, or rien. By body: a row in visible_corps (objet, corps, then another model, bone, offset, size or pieces) is used when the player's profile field champ_corps (« corps ») has that value. Never assume a bone, a model, a clip or a size, and never write a pack's bone name: use the slot's body part (measured), or a bone chosen in the character's skeleton, and measure the head before setting sizes. The equipment's models are the creator's, kind by kind: there is no default model.
  • The weapon in hand (arme_en_main): barre (the Inventory's hotbar, as before), emplacement (the weapon worn in the slot that holds the weapon is handed to the Inventory, inventaire.tenir_porte: held, shown and announced exactly like the bar's, with its row of en_main, whose prise can be the measured weapon hold), or lies (both: the weapon worn comes back in hand when the bar holds nothing). Equipment never attaches the weapon in hand itself. With the hook equipement.corps (another body following the player), the Inventory holds the weapon in that body's hand too (its hook inventaire.porteur, whose default follows equipement.corps).
  • The window: one line per slot (its name, then what is worn; a slot in use takes the theme's selected state) and the totals of the values worn and of the list valeurs; control equipment (O, Y on a gamepad); styles fenetre.equipement, titre.equipement, case.equipement (case.rare for a rare item worn), texte.equipement, bouton.fermer. The tooltip of an inventory item compares it with what is worn at the same place (comparer): green what goes up, red what goes down. One piece of information per bonus: an upgraded or set item shows its effective total (« +9 Dégâts », green) instead of the record's raw bonus; a broken item shows its raw bonuses greyed, followed by « (sans effet) ». Tier and comparison colors are made readable on the theme's image by the engine (hudReadableTint).
  • Durability (ADR-039 § 6), only when the user asks for wear: give the kind's row in sortes_equipables a usure_max (0: never wears; a new copy's record gets it), usee_par (attaque: each hit, combat.attaque, or shot, tir.tir, while held; coup_recu: each hit taken while worn, combat.coup or tir.touche on the player; recolte: each harvest while held, collecte.recolte) and perte (lost each time). At zero, a_zero: casse (kept, greyed, « Cassé » in the tooltip, no bonuses until repaired: bonusEffectifs gives nothing) or detruit (taken off and destroyed). Below seuil_usure % (20), a message and the slot turns red (equipement.usure_alerte). Events equipement.casse, equipement.detruit.
  • Repair, each path optional: at a station whose name is station_reparation (Crafting's station kind, « Établi »: its Crafting window gets a workshop tab, « Atelier », costs in couts_station rows with action reparer), at a merchant if boutique_reparation (the Shop window gets a « Services » tab) (price prix_reparation for an item at zero, in proportion to what is lost; money monnaie, else the merchant's), or a kit (kit_reparation, an Inventory kind: a double click on it repairs the most worn item worn or held; kit_montant 0: all). baisse_reparation lowers the maximum at each repair. Script: game.emit("equipement.reparer", {fiche = n, par = "station"|"boutique"|"kit", cible = station or merchant}); announces equipement.repare.
  • Sets (§ 7): ensembles (nom, pieces: kinds separated by commas) and ensembles_paliers (ensemble, nombre of pieces worn, valeur, add, pct in %): set and removed by themselves, source ensemble:<name key>; a broken piece does not count. The tooltip shows « Loup (2/4) » and each tier, green with « (actif) » when reached, grey with « (encore 2) » (the pieces still missing) otherwise: a written mark, not only a color (texts palier_atteint, palier_manque).
  • Upgrade (§ 7): the kind's row niveau_max (0: none) and amelioration_pct (each level adds that % of the item's bonuses), plus fixed bonuses per level in amelioration_bonus. Where: station_amelioration (« Forge »; costs couts_station with action ameliorer, niveau the level aimed for or 0) or merchants (boutique_amelioration, price per level in amelioration_niveaux). amelioration_niveaux (niveau, chance %, prix): 100 % without a row; the roll is made on the host. On failure, echec_amelioration: rien, niveau_perdu or casse. The name shows « Épée de fer +2 ». Script: equipement.ameliorer {fiche, par, cible}; announces equipement.ameliore {reussi, niveau, echec}.
  • Gems (§ 7): a kind whose Inventory categorie is categorie_gemme (« gemme »); its bonuses in gemmes_bonus (gemme, categorie of the item receiving it, empty for any other, valeur, add, pct). Sockets: the kind's row emplacements_gemmes, or rolled by Loot. Set at station_sertissage or merchants (boutique_sertissage, prix_sertissage); retrait_gemme: impossible, detruite, or rendue (for prix_retrait, or couts_station retirer_gemme). Scripts: equipement.sertir {fiche, gemme or gemme_fiche, par, cible}, equipement.desertir {fiche, index, par, cible}; announce equipement.serti, equipement.desserti.
  • The workshop: one window at a station or a merchant, never two. At a station named in station_reparation, station_amelioration or station_sertissage, the Crafting window has two tabs, « Fabrication » (its recipes) and « Atelier »; at a merchant with a service ticked, the Shop window has « Marchandise » and « Services » (hooks fabrication.onglets, boutique.onglets). The workshop tab lists the player's unique items with Repair, Upgrade, Set and Remove buttons and their cost. A station without recipes, fuel or level to gain, or a merchant selling nothing, opens on the workshop tab at once. equipement.atelier {cible, joueur} opens the station's or merchant's window on that tab from a script, for that player only (without joueur: the host). atelier_x is no longer used (version 5). Refusals go through equipement.refus with the reason shown. All this changes only the record (usure, usure_max, amelioration, gemmes): the bonuses follow through bonusEffectifs and objet.change, and the save keeps them with the records.
  • The screens (version 8), delivered by the system and filled by its scripts: never draw an equipment window with game.hud_*, and never edit the system; change their look, places and sizes with screens_set (styles, not hand-made values). « Équipement » (modules/equipement/ecrans/fenetre.json): Fenêtre, Titre, the list Emplacements (row Ligne: Image of the item worn, hidden without one; Case, a button with the action retirer), Caractéristiques, the list Valeurs (row Valeur, hidden when empty), Fermer (action fermer, B or Escape). Keys of a row: Équipement/Case#N, N in the order of the slots (Équipement/Case#1: the first, « Tête » in jeu_de_role); dragging an inventory slot onto it wears the item there. The script sets each Case to the width of its row (minus the image), read on screen. fenetre_x left at 0.115: the window stays where its shape Fenêtre is (move it in the screen); another value puts it there, as before. « Atelier de l'équipement » (ecrans/atelier.json): shown inside the host window's tab shape (Fabrication/Onglet ouvert, Boutique/Onglet ouvert) with game.screen_show(name, {inside = key}): Atelier, Rien, the list Objets (row Ligne: Objet, Action 1 to Action 6, buttons with the action service; an item with more services goes on to the next row; up to two services each button keeps its width in the screen, more share the width of two). « Message de l'équipement » (ecrans/message.json): its Message, at the top, red for a refusal, gone after a few seconds. With several players, each player has their own screens.
  • Displayed names of values: the list valeurs (valeur, libelle, translated) names values everywhere (game.stat_name, which also works in a guest's game: the names go with the progression); the Progression names its own.
  • Several players: one copy per player, on the host. A guest's request (to wear, repair, upgrade, set a gem) only cites their own records (game.item_citable), and the upgrade roll is made on the host; what shows is an attached copy that guests receive. What is worn is saved per player and per world.
