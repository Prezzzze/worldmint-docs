Combat simple¶

Le joueur frappe devant lui avec une touche, avec les dégâts, la portée et la recharge réglés sur lui. Il touche ce qui porte Santé. Pour des ennemis qui attaquent, ajoutez « Ennemis ».
Identifiant combat.basique
Version 11
Se pose sur des objets oui
Active aussi Santé

Événements¶

Les descriptions viennent du code et sont en anglais, la langue de travail de l'agent.

Ce qu'il annonce¶

Événement Champs Description
combat.attaque source (id) Someone struck, whether it hit or not: the stamina module counts it.
combat.coup cible (id), source (id), montant (number) An attack hit something. The health module, if present, has been asked to remove the damage.
sante.blesser cible (id), montant (number), source (id?) Ask the health module to remove health from an object.
sante.garde cible (id), part (number), angle (number) An object protects itself, or stops (part 0): hits coming from in front of it, within angle degrees, lose part of their damage (0 to 1) before its armor. A hit without a source cannot be blocked.

Ce qu'il écoute¶

Événement Champs Description
endurance.change valeur (number), max (number), epuise (boolean) The player's stamina changed. epuise is true from zero until they regain a share of it: meanwhile they cannot run, jump or strike.
joueur.occupe occupe (boolean), par (text) The player's hands are taken by something else than fighting (version 23): a building mode where the click places a piece, a tool being aimed. occupe true when it starts, false when it ends; par names who says it ("construction"), so several can overlap. Fighting modules do not strike nor block while any of them keeps the player busy.
action.prise action (text), prise (boolean), par (text), joueur (id?) A system takes a control action for itself, or gives it back (prise false) (version 24): gathering takes attack while the player aims at a resource that needs a tool, so the press chops instead of swinging a weapon. par names who says it ("collecte"). Fighting modules do not strike with that action while any system holds it.

Basic combat (combat.basique)¶

The player strikes the nearest object carrying Health in front of them with a key (attack, the left mouse button by default). Activating it activates the health module.

  • Place it on the player with systems_attach: the player's damage, reach, strike angle, cooldown, knockback and hit animation are their values (systems_set). Without it on the player, the key does nothing. The animation is a clip of the player's model, read with animation_clips (never guess names): the hit lands at moment (% of the clip, 50 by default), and the next strike waits for the end of the gesture, whose length is the clip's divided by vitesse_animation. Without an animation, the hit is instant.
  • What can be hit: objects carrying sante. The damage sent is raw: the target's armor is applied by the health module.
  • It handles the player's hits only. Enemies that move and attack are the module ennemis: add it only if the user asked for enemies that fight.
  • Blocking: set parade on the player (the share of a hit stopped, in %, such as 70 or 100; 0 by default: no blocking). While the action block is held (right mouse button by default, LB on a gamepad), hits from in front within angle_parade lose that share before armor, the player walks at vitesse_parade and cannot strike. animation_parade is a clip of the player's model, replayed while blocking. A blocked hit is announced by sante.pare (cible, source, montant stopped).
  • With the stamina module (endurance), each strike costs stamina, and an exhausted player cannot strike or block.
  • While the game shows the cursor (game.show_cursor(true): an inventory, a menu), or while something keeps the player busy (the event joueur.occupe: construction's building mode, where the click places a piece), the player neither strikes nor blocks. A script of yours that takes the player's hands (aiming a fishing rod) sends joueur.occupe with occupe true, then false, and its own par.
  • While another system takes the attack action for itself (the event action.prise: collecte while the player aims at a resource that needs a tool, so the click chops), the player does not strike with it: one press, one gesture.
  • React to hits: listen to combat.coup (cible, source, montant), for a sound or an effect. Deaths come from the health module: sante.mort.
  • Knockback moves characters only. A dead object stays unless its health values say to remove it.

Several players¶

One copy per player: each player has their own cooldown, block and hits. Players do not hurt each other unless the game setting blesser_joueurs ("Players can hurt each other") is ticked.
