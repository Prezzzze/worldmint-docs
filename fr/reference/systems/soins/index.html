Soins¶

Ce qui porte ce système rend de la vie au joueur : une source qu'on touche, ou une potion qu'on ramasse dans l'inventaire et qu'on boit avec une touche, chacune avec ce qu'elle rend.

Événements¶

Les descriptions viennent du code et sont en anglais, la langue de travail de l'agent.

Ce qu'il annonce¶

Événement Champs Description
soins.soin cible (id), montant (number), objet (text) The healing module gave health back: a potion drunk, a spring touched. objet is the kind of potion or the name of what healed.
sante.soigner cible (id), montant (number) Ask the health module to give health back, up to its maximum.
inventaire.retirer objet (text), quantite (number) Ask the inventory to remove items, such as a payment. Answered by inventaire.change, or inventaire.manque if there are not enough.

Ce qu'il écoute¶

Événement Champs Description
scene.change depuis (text), vers (text) Sent by the engine once the player has arrived in another scene of the game (game.go_to, or a passage). Scripts keep running across scenes: this is when a module looks at the objects of the new scene.
sante.change cible (id), vie (number), max (number) An object's health changed, or is announced once when the game starts.
interaction.utiliser cible (id), joueur (id) The player used the object in reach: a door, a merchant, an item to pick up. Whoever owns that object decides what it does.
inventaire.change objet (text), quantite (number), total (number), chargement (boolean?) The quantity of an item changed. total is the new quantity. chargement is true when a saved game gives the items back (game.load): nothing was picked up.

Healing (soins)¶

Objects carrying this system give health back to the player, each with its own montant (systems_set). Activating it activates the health module. Use it whenever the player can be hurt and fights last: without a way to heal, a long fight is lost in advance.

  • Picked up and drunk later (potions, food): place inventaire on the object too. It is picked up like any item, under its kind (the inventory's nom, or its name without a trailing number; a kind renamed by overriding inventaire.sorte is not seen here, so set nom instead). The action heal (R by default, ↑ on a gamepad) drinks the first kind carried and gives that kind's montant. recharge is the time between two.
  • Used on the spot (a spring, a heart, a campfire): no inventory. It heals when the player walks over it (game setting au_contact, on by default), or when used if it also carries interaction. Then it is removed, or comes back after reapparition seconds: an arena whose healing comes back keeps a long fight possible.
  • With vie_pleine on (the default), nothing is used up while the player's health is full.
  • Each heal is announced by soins.soin (cible, montant, objet): listen to it for a sound or an effect. Health bars follow on their own (sante.change).
  • Name potions and food in the user's language. Do not heal from your own script with sante.soigner when this module fits.

Several players¶

One copy per player: what a player touches or drinks heals that player.
