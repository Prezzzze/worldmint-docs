Décalcomanie¶

Une image projetée sur ce qui se trouve sous l'objet : une trace de pas, un impact, une tache, une flaque, une brûlure, un panneau peint sur un mur. Créer > Décalcomanie pose une boîte invisible qui la porte : l'image couvre sa largeur et sa profondeur, et se plaque sur le sol ou le mur autour de sa base.
Identifiant decalcomanie
Version 1
Se pose sur des objets oui
Géré par le moteur oui : le moteur le fait vivre lui-même, sans script

Decal (decalcomanie)¶

An object carrying this system projects an image on what lies around its base: the ground, a wall, a floor of cubes. A footprint, an impact, a stain, a puddle, a scorch mark, a sign. The system has no script: the renderer projects it. The game report counts decals under decals.

  • Make a decal: create a box (builtin:cube) with visible: false and no collider, then systems_attach this system on it. The image covers the box's width (x) and depth (z), and lies on the surface around the box's base, within half its height above and below (y). Scale the box to size the image: 1 × 0.5 × 1 for a puddle of one metre, 0.3 × 0.3 × 0.5 for a footprint. Turn the box around its height to turn the image.
  • On a wall: tip the box so that its height points out of the wall (rotate 90 degrees around x or z), and place its base on the wall.
  • Per object (systems_set): image (tache, impact, brulure, flaque, pas, trace, or fichier with fichier: an image of the project, from assets_search kind "image"; its transparency is kept), opacite (0 to 1), couleur ("#rrggbb", a tint: dark red for blood, green for poison; white leaves the image as it is).
  • A decal shows as long as its object is in the game: game.destroy removes it. Do not make its box visible: that would show the box.
  • From a script, during the game: game.decal("impact", pos, {size = 0.6, normal = vec(0, 1, 0)}) (a crater where a fireball lands, footprints that follow the player). The game keeps a limited number of them (fewer on a small graphics card): the oldest fades out to make room. See the script reference.
  • It projects on everything inside its box, characters walking through included: keep the box thin over a ground where characters walk.

Several players¶

The engine runs it once for the whole game: decals are seen by the host.
