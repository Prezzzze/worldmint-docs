Les événements des systèmes¶

Les systèmes ne lisent jamais les variables les uns des autres : chacun annonce ce qui s'est passé, et qui veut réagir écoute. Un script peut écouter ces événements (game.on) et en envoyer (game.emit) : voir Les scripts de jeu. Un champ marqué ? peut manquer.

Les descriptions viennent du code et sont en anglais, la langue de travail de l'agent.

sante.blesser¶

Ask the health module to remove health from an object.

  • Champs : cible (id), montant (number), source (id?)
  • Annoncé par : Besoins, Combat simple, Ennemis
  • Écouté par : Santé

sante.soigner¶

Ask the health module to give health back, up to its maximum.

  • Champs : cible (id), montant (number)
  • Annoncé par : Besoins, Soins
  • Écouté par : Santé

sante.change¶

An object's health changed, or is announced once when the game starts.

  • Champs : cible (id), vie (number), max (number)
  • Annoncé par : Santé
  • Écouté par : Barre de vie, Besoins, Ennemis, Soins

sante.garde¶

An object protects itself, or stops (part 0): hits coming from in front of it, within angle degrees, lose part of their damage (0 to 1) before its armor. A hit without a source cannot be blocked.

  • Champs : cible (id), part (number), angle (number)
  • Annoncé par : Combat simple
  • Écouté par : Santé

sante.pare¶

A hit was blocked by a guard: montant is the damage it stopped. For a clang, sparks, a stamina cost.

  • Champs : cible (id), source (id?), montant (number)
  • Annoncé par : Santé
  • Écouté par : Sons

sante.mort¶

An object's health reached zero. What happens next belongs to the game: the health module does nothing else, unless its setting says to destroy the object.

  • Champs : cible (id), source (id?)
  • Annoncé par : Santé
  • Écouté par : Poupée de chiffon, Construction, Déclencheur, Ennemis, Sons

zone.entree¶

Something entered a zone (an object carrying the zone system): a player, a character or a moving object, depending on what the zone detects (joueur: every player). joueur is set when qui is a player. Announced by the engine.

  • Champs : cible (id), qui (id), joueur (id?)
  • Annoncé par : Zone
  • Écouté par : Déclencheur

zone.sortie¶

Something left a zone. Not announced for an object removed from the game while inside.

  • Champs : cible (id), qui (id), joueur (id?)
  • Annoncé par : Zone
  • Écouté par : Déclencheur

hud.clic¶

A button of the HUD was clicked: pressed, then released on it; or chosen with A (Enter) while it has the gamepad or keyboard focus; or the window's back button (back = true), with B (Escape). cible is the button's key (game.hud_button), joueur the player who clicked (a guest clicks on their own computer; the host plays the click). Announced by the engine, before the scripts' on_update of that step.

  • Champs : cible (text), joueur (id?)
  • Annoncé par : —
  • Écouté par : Boutique, Carte, Dialogue, Fabrication, Inventaire, Menu, Journal de quêtes

hud.valider¶

A text field of the HUD was validated with Enter. cible is the field's key (game.hud_input), texte what it holds. Announced by the engine; the field is no longer selected.

  • Champs : cible (text), texte (text), joueur (id?)
  • Annoncé par : —
  • Écouté par : —

video.fin¶

A video ended: watched to the end, skipped by the player (passee true), stopped by a script, or replaced by another full-screen one. video is the handle game.video or game.hud_video returned, fichier its file. Announced by the engine at the step it ends, before the scripts' on_update; the event given to game.video (event) is announced too, with the same fields.

  • Champs : video (number), fichier (text), passee (boolean)
  • Annoncé par : —
  • Écouté par : —

menu.jouer¶

The player left the title screen with Play: the game really starts. For an intro message, a music, a timer that should not run behind the title.

  • Champs : —
  • Annoncé par : Menu
  • Écouté par : —

menu.pause¶

The pause menu opened (ouvert true) or closed (ouvert false). The world is stopped while it is open.

  • Champs : ouvert (boolean)
  • Annoncé par : Menu
  • Écouté par : —

dialogue.ouvrir¶

Ask the dialogue system to start the dialogue of cible, as if the player had used it: from a zone, a trigger, a script.

  • Champs : cible (id)
  • Annoncé par : —
  • Écouté par : Dialogue

dialogue.debut¶

A dialogue window opened: cible is the character who talks.

  • Champs : cible (id)
  • Annoncé par : Dialogue
  • Écouté par : —

dialogue.choix¶

The player chose an answer in a dialogue: choix is its rank among the answers shown (from 1), texte its text, etiquette the label of the line it answered (empty if none). Sent before the answer's own event, if it has one.

  • Champs : cible (id), choix (number), texte (text), etiquette (text)
  • Annoncé par : Dialogue
  • Écouté par : —

dialogue.marque¶

A dialogue set a mark: a word the game remembers (a line shown, an answer chosen). Every dialogue's conditions can use it.

  • Champs : marque (text)
  • Annoncé par : Dialogue
  • Écouté par : Journal de quêtes

dialogue.marquer¶

Ask the dialogue system to set a mark, from a script or another system; with retirer true, to remove it. Dialogue conditions then see it.

  • Champs : marque (text), retirer (boolean?)
  • Annoncé par : Journal de quêtes
  • Écouté par : Dialogue

dialogue.fin¶

A dialogue window closed: after its last line, or a choice leading to fin.

  • Champs : cible (id)
  • Annoncé par : Dialogue
  • Écouté par : —

interaction.proche¶

The object the player can use changed: a new one is in reach, or none (cible missing).

  • Champs : cible (id?)
  • Annoncé par : Interaction
  • Écouté par : —

interaction.utiliser¶

The player used the object in reach: a door, a merchant, an item to pick up. Whoever owns that object decides what it does.

  • Champs : cible (id), joueur (id)
  • Annoncé par : Interaction
  • Écouté par : Besoins, Boutique, Collecte, Déclencheur, Dialogue, Fabrication, Inventaire, Passage, Soins, Sons

inventaire.ajouter¶

Ask the inventory to add items, such as a reward. Answered by inventaire.change, or inventaire.plein. With several players, joueur says whose bag; without it, the player whose action is being handled, or every player.

  • Champs : objet (text), quantite (number), joueur (id?)
  • Annoncé par : Boutique, Collecte, Construction, Fabrication, Journal de quêtes
  • Écouté par : Inventaire

inventaire.en_main¶

What the player holds in hand changed (version 24): the slot chosen in the inventory's bar (keys 1 to 9, wheel), an item used up, a load (chargement true). objet is the kind held, "" for empty hands; avant the one held before; place the bar slot (0: nothing). Gathering checks the tool held; a game script equips a sword with it.

  • Champs : objet (text), avant (text), place (number), chargement (boolean?), joueur (id?)
  • Annoncé par : Inventaire
  • Écouté par : Collecte

inventaire.tenir¶

Ask the inventory to hold something in hand (version 24): a bar slot (place), or a kind of item (objet; one outside the bar takes the chosen slot). objet "" puts the item away: empty hands.

  • Champs : objet (text?), place (number?), joueur (id?)
  • Annoncé par : —
  • Écouté par : Inventaire

action.prise¶

A system takes a control action for itself, or gives it back (prise false) (version 24): gathering takes attack while the player aims at a resource that needs a tool, so the press chops instead of swinging a weapon. par names who says it ("collecte"). Fighting modules do not strike with that action while any system holds it.

  • Champs : action (text), prise (boolean), par (text), joueur (id?)
  • Annoncé par : Collecte
  • Écouté par : Combat simple

inventaire.choisi¶

The player clicked a slot of the inventory window: objet is the kind of item, total how many they carry. What it does belongs to the game: eat, equip, read.

  • Champs : objet (text), total (number)
  • Annoncé par : Inventaire
  • Écouté par : Besoins

inventaire.retirer¶

Ask the inventory to remove items, such as a payment. Answered by inventaire.change, or inventaire.manque if there are not enough.

  • Champs : objet (text), quantite (number)
  • Annoncé par : Besoins, Boutique, Construction, Fabrication, Journal de quêtes, Soins
  • Écouté par : Inventaire

inventaire.change¶

The quantity of an item changed. total is the new quantity. chargement is true when a saved game gives the items back (game.load): nothing was picked up.

  • Champs : objet (text), quantite (number), total (number), chargement (boolean?)
  • Annoncé par : Inventaire
  • Écouté par : Besoins, Boutique, Collecte, Construction, Dialogue, Fabrication, Journal de quêtes, Soins, Sons

inventaire.plein¶

An item could not be added: every slot holds another kind of item.

  • Champs : objet (text)
  • Annoncé par : Inventaire
  • Écouté par : Boutique, Fabrication, Sons

inventaire.manque¶

Items could not be removed: there are not enough.

  • Champs : objet (text), quantite (number)
  • Annoncé par : Inventaire
  • Écouté par : Boutique, Construction, Fabrication

langue.change¶

Sent by the engine when the player changes the game's language (game.set_language, the Menu options). A module redraws what it already shows: its texts go through game.t and game.language.

  • Champs : langue (text)
  • Annoncé par : —
  • Écouté par : Besoins, Construction, Dialogue, Inventaire, Jour et nuit

scene.change¶

Sent by the engine once the player has arrived in another scene of the game (game.go_to, or a passage). Scripts keep running across scenes: this is when a module looks at the objects of the new scene.

  • Champs : depuis (text), vers (text)
  • Annoncé par : —
  • Écouté par : Carte, Ennemis, Jour et nuit, Passage, Santé, Soins, Sons

joueur.arrivee¶

Sent by the engine, in a game with several players (the game setting Multiplayer), on the computer hosting the game, when a guest joins: joueur is their character (a copy of the player's), numero their place (1 to 7, the host being 0), nom the name they chose. game.players() lists them all. Scripts only run on the host.

  • Champs : joueur (id), numero (number), nom (text)
  • Annoncé par : —
  • Écouté par : —

joueur.depart¶

Sent by the engine on the host when a guest leaves the game (they quit, or their connection was lost): their character is removed right after. A module forgets what it kept for them.

  • Champs : joueur (id), numero (number)
  • Annoncé par : —
  • Écouté par : Collecte, Passage

joueur.occupe¶

The player's hands are taken by something else than fighting (version 23): a building mode where the click places a piece, a tool being aimed. occupe true when it starts, false when it ends; par names who says it ("construction"), so several can overlap. Fighting modules do not strike nor block while any of them keeps the player busy.

  • Champs : occupe (boolean), par (text)
  • Annoncé par : Construction
  • Écouté par : Collecte, Combat simple, Inventaire

monde.sommeil¶

Sent by the engine in a large world when objects fall asleep: their 256 m tiles are now beyond the activity radius around the player. objets is the list of their ids (those carrying a system or a tag, characters and moving objects). They still exist, but do not move, walk, animate or detect, and game.with_system, game.with_tag and game.near leave them out unless {asleep = true}. A module sets aside what it was doing with them.

  • Champs : objets (list of ids)
  • Annoncé par : —
  • Écouté par : —

monde.reveil¶

Sent by the engine in a large world when objects wake up, as the world kept them: the player came back near them. objets is the list of their ids. A module takes them back; to catch up the time they slept, compare game.time() with the time it noted.

  • Champs : objets (list of ids)
  • Annoncé par : —
  • Écouté par : —

timeline.debut¶

Sent by the engine when an animation made by hand or a cutscene starts (game.play_timeline, a trigger, or one that plays from the start). sorte: animation or cutscene.

  • Champs : timeline (text), sorte (text)
  • Annoncé par : —
  • Écouté par : —

timeline.fin¶

Sent by the engine when an animation or a cutscene ends: at its end, skipped by the player (passee), or stopped by game.stop_timeline (arretee). An animation that loops never ends on its own. After a cutscene, the player has control again.

  • Champs : timeline (text), sorte (text), passee (boolean), arretee (boolean)
  • Annoncé par : —
  • Écouté par : —

collecte.coup¶

The player hit a resource (a use of something carrying collecte). restants is how many hits are left before it is used up.

  • Champs : cible (id), coups (number), restants (number)
  • Annoncé par : Collecte
  • Écouté par : Sons

collecte.recolte¶

A resource was used up and gave what it holds. The inventory, if present, has been asked to add it.

  • Champs : cible (id), ressource (text), quantite (number), joueur (id?)
  • Annoncé par : Collecte
  • Écouté par : —

collecte.epuise¶

A resource is used up and is about to disappear (destroyed, or hidden until it grows back): it still exists while this is sent.

  • Champs : cible (id)
  • Annoncé par : Collecte
  • Écouté par : Sons

collecte.repousse¶

A used-up resource grew back and can be gathered again.

  • Champs : cible (id)
  • Annoncé par : Collecte
  • Écouté par : —

collecte.outil_manquant¶

The player tried to gather a resource without the tool it needs: not held in hand (the default, with attack), or not in the inventory (gathering set to the interaction).

  • Champs : cible (id), outil (text), joueur (id?)
  • Annoncé par : Collecte
  • Écouté par : —

ennemi.etat¶

An enemy changed state: patrouille (patrols), poursuite (chases the player), attaque (hits in reach), attente (waits its turn near the player, when enough others are already striking), retour (goes back to its spot), mort (dead, does nothing more).

  • Champs : cible (id), etat (text)
  • Annoncé par : Ennemis
  • Écouté par : Sons

combat.attaque¶

Someone struck, whether it hit or not: the stamina module counts it.

  • Champs : source (id)
  • Annoncé par : Combat simple
  • Écouté par : Endurance, Sons

endurance.change¶

The player's stamina changed. epuise is true from zero until they regain a share of it: meanwhile they cannot run, jump or strike.

  • Champs : valeur (number), max (number), epuise (boolean)
  • Annoncé par : Endurance
  • Écouté par : Combat simple, Sons

soins.soin¶

The healing module gave health back: a potion drunk, a spring touched. objet is the kind of potion or the name of what healed.

  • Champs : cible (id), montant (number), objet (text)
  • Annoncé par : Soins
  • Écouté par : Sons

combat.coup¶

An attack hit something. The health module, if present, has been asked to remove the damage.

  • Champs : cible (id), source (id), montant (number)
  • Annoncé par : Combat simple, Ennemis
  • Écouté par : Sons

boutique.ouvrir¶

Ask the shop system to open the shop of cible, as if the player had used it: from a dialogue answer, a trigger, a script.

  • Champs : cible (id)
  • Annoncé par : Dialogue
  • Écouté par : Boutique

boutique.achat¶

The player bought an item in a shop: cible is the merchant, quantite how many they received, prix what they paid, in monnaie (a kind of inventory item). The inventory has already changed.

  • Champs : cible (id), objet (text), quantite (number), prix (number), monnaie (text)
  • Annoncé par : Boutique
  • Écouté par : —

boutique.vente¶

The player sold an item to a merchant (cible): quantite how many they gave, prix what they received, in monnaie. The inventory has already changed.

  • Champs : cible (id), objet (text), quantite (number), prix (number), monnaie (text)
  • Annoncé par : Boutique
  • Écouté par : —

quetes.demarrer¶

Ask the quest log to give a quest to the player, by its title: from a script, a dialogue or a trigger. Nothing happens if it was already given.

  • Champs : quete (text)
  • Annoncé par : —
  • Écouté par : Journal de quêtes

quetes.debut¶

A quest started: quete is its title.

  • Champs : quete (text)
  • Annoncé par : Journal de quêtes
  • Écouté par : Carte

quetes.etape¶

A step of a quest is done: etape is its rank in the quest (from 1), texte what it said. Sent before quetes.fin when it was the last one.

  • Champs : quete (text), etape (number), texte (text)
  • Annoncé par : Journal de quêtes
  • Écouté par : Carte

quetes.fin¶

A quest is finished: all its steps are done. Its reward, if any, has been asked to the inventory.

  • Champs : quete (text)
  • Annoncé par : Journal de quêtes
  • Écouté par : Carte

quetes.echouer¶

Ask the quest log to make a quest in progress fail, by its title: from a script or a trigger. Nothing happens if it is not in progress.

  • Champs : quete (text)
  • Annoncé par : —
  • Écouté par : Journal de quêtes

quetes.abandonner¶

Ask the quest log to abandon a quest in progress, by its title, as the player does from the log. Nothing happens if it is not in progress.

  • Champs : quete (text)
  • Annoncé par : —
  • Écouté par : Journal de quêtes

quetes.echec¶

A quest failed: an event, a mark, its time ran out, or quetes.echouer. It gives no reward; it may be given again if its settings allow it.

  • Champs : quete (text)
  • Annoncé par : Journal de quêtes
  • Écouté par : —

quetes.abandon¶

The player abandoned a quest from the log (or quetes.abandonner asked for it). It gives no reward; it may be given again if its settings allow it.

  • Champs : quete (text)
  • Annoncé par : Journal de quêtes
  • Écouté par : —

carte.ouverte¶

The big map opened (ouvert true) or closed (ouvert false). The world is stopped while it is open, unless its setting says otherwise.

  • Champs : ouvert (boolean)
  • Annoncé par : Carte
  • Écouté par : —

carte.decouvert¶

The player discovered a map marker (cible), one shown only once discovered: they came near enough, or carte.reveler revealed it. It now shows on the map.

  • Champs : cible (id)
  • Annoncé par : Carte
